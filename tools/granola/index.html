Granola

Granola is an artificial intelligence meeting notepad application designed for founders, product managers, designers, consultants, and professionals who participate in frequent collaborative calls. Traditional meeting transcription tools typically rely on automated video bots that join calls as external participants, which can introduce social friction, distract meeting attendees, or violate internal organizational policies. Granola bypasses this limitation by capturing system audio and microphone input directly on the user's local operating system, eliminating the need to deploy meeting bots into calls.

The platform takes a human-in-the-loop approach to meeting documentation. During a call, the user types shorthand notes, key bullet points, or customer reactions in a clean note-taking interface. While device audio capture occurs locally on the user's machine, speech-to-text transcription and generative AI synthesis are processed via secure cloud infrastructure. Following the call, Granola combines the user's typed notes with the cloud transcript, applying AI models to expand shorthand bullets into structured, coherent meeting summaries that preserve the author's authentic voice and intent.

Granola provides native desktop applications for macOS and Windows, as well as mobile companion apps for iOS and Android. It includes conversational chat capabilities and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations with Claude and ChatGPT, enabling users to query recent 30-day personal notes on the Basic plan or full workspace archives on Business tiers. In accordance with platform privacy practices, original audio recordings are automatically deleted after cloud transcription is complete across all subscription tiers.

Best For

  • Founders, product leaders, and consultants who prefer taking active notes without having a visible bot join calls.
  • Sales reps and account managers conducting customer discovery interviews who need structured CRM summaries.
  • Professionals working in privacy-sensitive environments that restrict third-party meeting bot integrations.

Not Ideal For

  • Teams looking for completely unattended meeting recording where no human attendee is present.
  • Users on the Free plan who require in-app access to meeting archives older than 30 days without upgrading.
  • Organizations seeking automated video screen recording alongside audio transcription.

How It Works

A user installs the native Granola desktop application on macOS or Windows and connects their digital calendar. When a meeting begins on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Slack, the user starts recording with a single click. Granola captures local microphone input and internal computer audio directly through device audio loopback without sending an external bot into the call. Captured audio streams are securely transmitted to cloud services for automated speech-to-text processing.

While the conversation takes place, the user types quick bullet points or notes inside the editor. In the cloud, speech-to-text models process the incoming audio stream into a verbatim transcript. Once the call concludes, Granola synthesizes the typed bullets with the transcript, applying custom templates to produce structured summaries, key takeaways, and action items. Once transcription and note generation are complete, the underlying audio recording is permanently deleted from servers.

Users can review and edit generated notes, share formatted summaries to Slack, or query discussions via interactive side panels. Teams can connect Granola to Claude or ChatGPT via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to query recent 30-day personal notes on the free Basic tier, or access full workspace archives and dedicated developer API endpoints on the paid Business tier.

Key Features

Device Audio Capture & Cloud Transcription

  • Direct device microphone and system audio capture on macOS and Windows without external meeting bots.
  • Secure cloud speech-to-text transcription with automatic audio file deletion after processing.
  • Compatible with major conferencing tools including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack.

Human-in-the-Loop Note Synthesis

  • Editor merging user-typed shorthand notes with verbatim cloud transcripts for authentic summaries.
  • Customizable note-taking templates for customer discovery, 1:1 meetings, sprint syncs, and interviews.
  • Conversational AI chat allowing users to query transcripts and extract specific meeting decisions.

MCP Integrations & Workspace Knowledge

  • Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration querying personal notes from the last 30 days on Basic.
  • Business plan expands MCP to full workspace history and unlocks dedicated developer API access.
  • Export and sharing integrations with Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Attio, Affinity, and Zapier.

Practical Workflows

  1. Customer interview synthesis: Capture shorthand notes during discovery calls while Granola records local audio, producing structured problem statements and pain-point summaries.
  2. Team sync action item tracking: Record internal standups and sprint planning calls to automatically extract decisions, task assignments, and follow-up deadlines.
  3. Meeting knowledge querying: Use the interactive AI chat assistant to search across meeting discussions, extract direct quotes, and review agreed specifications.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Captures internal audio directly on the user device without deploying an intrusive bot into video conference calls.
  • Synthesizes user-typed notes with speech transcripts to maintain human perspective and context.
  • Free plan allows unlimited meetings and transcriptions without rigid monthly call duration caps.
  • Supports Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration with ChatGPT and Claude even on the free Basic tier.

Limitations

  • Audio transcription and AI note synthesis require cloud processing and cannot operate in offline mode.
  • Basic plan limits in-app note visibility and MCP queries to a rolling 30-day historical window.
  • Developer API access and advanced CRM integrations (Notion, HubSpot, Attio) require a paid Business plan.
  • Focuses strictly on audio and text notes, without recording visual video feeds or shared screens.

Pricing

Granola offers a freemium pricing structure split into Basic, Business, and Enterprise tiers. The Basic plan is completely free ($0/month) and includes unlimited meetings, device audio capture, cloud transcription, in-meeting AI chat, custom templates, Slack integration, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) access to personal notes from the last 30 days; however, in-app note visibility is constrained to that rolling 30-day window. The Business plan costs $14/user/month and unlocks unlimited meeting history, advanced AI reasoning models, expanded MCP access across personal and workspace notes, full developer API access, and workflow integrations with Notion, HubSpot, Attio, Affinity, and Zapier. The Enterprise plan starts at $35/user/month, adding administrative controls for MCP and APIs, Single Sign-On (SSO), SCIM provisioning, organization-wide auto-deletion schedules, priority support, and team-wide opt-outs for AI model training. Device audio recordings are automatically deleted after cloud transcription is complete across all tiers.

How It Compares

Unlike traditional AI meeting assistants like Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Fathom that send visible bot participants into calls, Granola captures audio locally on the host machine with zero bot presence. Compared to basic transcription utilities, Granola prioritizes human-in-the-loop note taking by combining user-typed bullet points with underlying transcripts to generate summaries that reflect the author's authentic perspective.
