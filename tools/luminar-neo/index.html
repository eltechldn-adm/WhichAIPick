Luminar Neo

Luminar Neo (by Skylum) is a highly advanced, AI-centric photo editing software built sequentially for both macOS and Windows. While traditional photo editors (like Adobe Lightroom) provide users with sliders to manipulate raw light and color data entirely manually, Luminar Neo is fundamentally structured around intelligent, one-click artificial intelligence mechanics designed to automate the most tedious, complex retouching tasks imaginable.

Its primary differentiator is its approach to localized, complex visual problems. Instead of spending 45 minutes painting a complex mask over a sky in Photoshop to replace it, Neo utilizes "Sky AI" to map the horizon, replace the sky automatically, and relight the entire foreground to match the new atmosphere in seconds. It possesses highly specialized AI engines for portrait retouching (smoothing skin, whitening teeth), structure enhancement, and powerline removal.

It is heavily utilized by semi-professional photographers, real estate shooters, portrait hobbyists, and social media managers who demand stunning, highly polished photographic imagery but completely lack the time or desire to learn the grueling nuances of Adobe Photoshop layer masking.

Best For

  • Enthusiast and semi-professional photographers
  • Real estate and landscape photographers
  • Portrait and wedding shooters managing massive volume
  • Users wanting Photoshop-level manipulation without the steep learning curve

How It Works

A real estate photographer uploads a raw photo of a house taken on a gloomy, overcast day. The image is flat and has ugly telephone wires crossing the roof. They open Luminar Neo. With one click, "Erase AI" detects and completely removes the powerlines. They switch to "Sky AI," selecting a dramatic sunset from the library; the AI instantly replaces the grey sky, cleanly masking around the intricate tree branches, and automatically adjusts the lighting on the house to reflect the warm, orange glow of the new sunset. Finally, they apply "Enhance AI," which intelligently bumps the contrast and saturation across the image. A 45-minute Photoshop job is completed in 30 seconds.

Key Features

Specialized AI Engines

  • Sky AI (Replacement and relighting)
  • Portrait AI (Face, Skin, and Body automated masking)
  • Structure/Enhance AI (Intelligent sharpening)
  • Erase/Remove AI (Powerlines and object removal)

Core Editing Workflow

  • Non-destructive RAW file processing workflows
  • Layer-based editing capabilities
  • Plugin integration with Adobe Lightroom & Photoshop
  • Batch processing functionality

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The Sky AI and relighting engine is arguably the most impressive, accurate implementation of sky replacement in the consumer market
  • Functions accurate$2 either as a standalone application or as a seamless plugin running *inside* Lightroom/Photoshop
  • Drastically reduces the time required for tedious masking; the AI accurately detects complex edges like hair or tree branches autonomously
  • The interface is highly modern, visual, and significantly less intimidating than the archaic panels of Photoshop

Cons

  • While highly powerful, heavy utilization of multiple AI layers can cause significant software lag and performance issues on older or less powerful computers
  • The heavy reliance on AI can easily lead to over-processed, "fake-looking" HDR images if the user pushes the sliders too proactively
  • It lacks the deep, granular cataloging and extreme file organization architecture that makes Adobe Lightroom the industry standard for professional asset management
  • The pricing structure has historically been confusing, shifting between one-time licenses and intense pushes for premium subscription "Extensions"

Pricing

Luminar Neo offers a bifurcated pricing approach. Users can purchase a lifetime license for the core software (a one-time fee). However, Skylum currently heavily incentivizes a Pro Subscription SaaS model, which includes the core software plus continuous access to highly advanced, generative AI extensions (like GenErase and GenExpand) and all future updates.

How It Compares

Luminar Neo competes fiercely against Adobe Lightroom, Adobe Photoshop, and DxO PhotoLab. Lightroom is the established king of cataloging and manual color grading. Photoshop is the king of established pixel manipulation. Luminar Neo differentiates itself as the established master of *AI Shortcuts*. You don't use Luminar to organize 50,000 photos; you use it specifically because replacing a sky or smoothing skin across 100 portraits in Lightroom takes hours, and Luminar Neo’s targeted AI executes the exact same task accurate$2 in mere minutes.
