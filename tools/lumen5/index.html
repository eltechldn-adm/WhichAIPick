Lumen5

Lumen5 is an AI-powered video creation platform heavily targeted at marketing departments, internal communications teams, and social media managers. Designed to make video creation as simple as building a slide deck, the platform specializes in repurposing written content—such as blog posts, whitepapers, or listicles—into engaging, text-heavy video formats popular on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.

Rather than providing a complex cinematic editing timeline, Lumen5 uses AI to construct a storyboard. It reads the provided text, summarizes the key points, pairs the concepts with relevant background stock footage, and overlays typography automatically calculated for reading speeds.

It is the tool of choice for enterprise marketing teams who lack video production skills but understand that video drives higher engagement on social channels than static text.

Visit Lumen5

Best For

  • B2B Marketing teams targeting LinkedIn engagement
  • Corporate communication and HR departments
  • Content teams with large archives of text articles
  • Users with zero video editing experience

How It Works

Users input text, upload a document, or paste a blog URL. The Lumen5 algorithm analyzes the text using NLP to identify important sentences, summarizing the content into bite-sized highlights. The software creates a storyboard, selecting background footage from its Getty Images library that matches the keywords of each scene. Users review the storyboard, drag and drop to swap media or adjust text, select a brand color palette, and export the video.

Key Features

AI Automated Generation

  • URL-storyboard conversion
  • AI text summarization
  • Automated stock media pairing
  • Machine learning text-timing calculation

Enterprise Branding

  • Premium Getty Images integration
  • Custom brand kits (fonts, colors, logos)
  • Drag-and-drop scene editor
  • Multi-format resolution export

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • One of the easiest possible learning curves for video creation
  • Brand kit enforcement ensures visually consistent corporate output
  • Summarization AI is highly tuned for social media attention spans
  • Excellent integration with premium stock libraries

Cons

  • Output videos can feel formulaic or "cookie-cutter"
  • Editing timeline lacks granular control for advanced video pacing
  • Not suitable for narrative, talking-head, or cinematic video production
  • AI media pairing can sometimes be frustratingly literal or irrelevant

Pricing

Lumen5 offers a free community tier with strict limitations, watermarks, and low resolution suitable only for testing. Paid plans are tiered for creators and professionals, scaling the resolution, removing watermarks, and unlocking premium stock assets. The Enterprise tier focuses on custom brand setups and team workflows.

How It Compares

Lumen5 competes closely with Pictory and InVideo. Pictory is its most direct rival in the "text-to-video" space, though Lumen5 has traditionally courted a more enterprise, B2B marketing audience with a cleaner, slide-like interface. InVideo and Canva offer more traditional timeline editing capabilities. Lumen5 differentiates by being the most restrictive, yet therefore the fastest and most foolproof, platform for turning corporate text into social video assets.
