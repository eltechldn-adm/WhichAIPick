Diffit

Diffit is an AI-powered instructional differentiation platform built specifically for K-12 educators and instructional coaches. It enables teachers to take any curriculum topic, article, YouTube video, or uploaded document and instantly adapt the content to any specific reading level from 2nd through 12th grade.

In addition to leveled reading passages, Diffit automatically generates complementary learning resources—including vocabulary lists with kid-friendly definitions, text-dependent comprehension questions, open-ended writing prompts, and interactive graphic organizers. All generated materials export directly into Google Classroom, Google Slides, Google Docs, and printable PDF worksheets.

Best For

  • K-12 classroom teachers needing to adapt grade-level texts for mixed-ability classrooms, ELL students, and IEP accommodations
  • Instructional coaches and curriculum specialists designing differentiated resource packs aligned to state reading standards
  • Special education educators requiring simplified vocabulary, scaffolded graphic organizers, and multi-modal reading supports

Not Ideal For

  • Higher education professors and researchers needing graduate-level technical discourse and peer-reviewed citation modeling
  • Corporate workplace training departments looking for corporate compliance LMS course authoring
  • Educators without access to digital classroom tools (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365) seeking solely custom physical textbooks

How It Works

Teachers begin by entering a topic, pasting text, inserting a public URL, or linking a YouTube video, then selecting a target reading level (from Grade 2 to Grade 12+). Diffit's pedagogical language models parse the source material, rewrite the text to match the syntactic complexity and vocabulary appropriate for that grade level, and preserve core curriculum facts.

Alongside the leveled passage, Diffit generates a complete instructional package including summary bullet points, key vocabulary terms with contextual definitions, text-dependent multiple-choice questions, short-answer prompts, and extension activities. Teachers can edit the content on screen, customize questions, and click export to generate interactive Google Slides, Google Docs, Google Forms quizzes, or printable PDF handouts.

Key Features

Instructional Differentiation & Content Generation

  • Reading level adaptation scaling any text, article, or video across 2nd to 12th+ grade Lexile bands
  • Automated extraction and generation of kid-friendly vocabulary lists with contextual definitions
  • Standards-aligned comprehension question generation including multiple-choice and open-ended prompts
  • Multilingual translation allowing classroom texts to be translated into over 40 languages for ELL students

Classroom Export & Pedagogical Templates

  • One-click export to Google Classroom, Google Slides, Google Docs, Google Forms, and Microsoft Word
  • Over 60 ready-to-use pedagogical graphic organizers (Frayer models, Venn diagrams, CER frameworks)
  • Student digital activity view enabling pupils to complete and submit interactive assignments directly
  • Teacher collaboration tools with shared school libraries and district-wide curriculum alignments

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Saves teachers hours of weekly preparation time by automatically creating leveled texts and scaffolds
  • Seamless export into Google Workspace ecosystem enables instant deployment to existing student devices
  • High pedagogical quality with age-appropriate vocabulary definitions and scaffolded comprehension questions
  • Free tier is functional and does not limit access to core grade-level differentiation tools

Limitations

  • Full Google Workspace and Microsoft export capabilities require a paid Teacher or School subscription
  • Free plan limits resource history retention to the most recent 90 days of generated materials
  • Specialized STEM diagram generation and complex mathematical formulas are less comprehensive than text humanities
  • Individual teacher annual subscriptions ($149.99/year) may require out-of-pocket spending if schools do not fund licenses

Pricing

Diffit provides a functional free tier alongside individual teacher and school-wide institutional licensing.

The Basic Plan ($0) is free for teachers and includes core reading generation across any grade level, vocabulary lists, comprehension questions, print-to-PDF sharing, and access to content created within the last 90 days. Diffit for Teachers costs $14.99 per month or $149.99 per year (equivalent to $12.50/month), unlocking unlimited resource history, direct one-click exports to Google Slides, Docs, Forms, Classroom, and Microsoft formats, interactive student digital activities, and advanced pedagogical templates. Diffit for Schools & Districts offers custom annual institutional quotes based on student enrollment, providing school-wide licenses, central administration, LMS integrations, single sign-on (SSO), and dedicated professional development.

How It Compares

Compared to MagicSchool AI, which offers a broad suite of dozens of administrative prompts (lesson plans, IEP generators, email drafters), Diffit is more deeply specialized in reading passage leveling and multi-level classroom resource formatting. Compared to Curipod, which focuses on live interactive polling and gamified slide presentations during class, Diffit focuses on curriculum content adaptation and printable/digital worksheets. Compared to general-purpose chat tools like ChatGPT, Diffit generates ready-to-print pedagogical layouts and graphic organizers formatted directly for classroom use without manual prompt engineering.
