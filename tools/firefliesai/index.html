Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that joins online meetings to record, transcribe, and summarise conversations. It integrates with video conferencing platforms including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, and automatically creates searchable transcripts and AI-generated summaries with action items after meetings.

Fireflies is designed for sales, recruiting, and business teams that have high meeting volumes and need structured records of conversations for follow-up, coaching, and knowledge retention. It also offers CRM integrations to push meeting data directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, and other systems.

It competes directly with Otter.ai and tl;dv in the AI meeting transcription category, with a particular strength in its CRM integration and sales team use cases.

Best For

  • Sales teams documenting customer calls
  • Recruiting teams capturing interview records
  • Operations teams tracking meeting outputs
  • Sales managers reviewing call quality
  • Teams requiring CRM-integrated meeting data

How It Works

Users connect Fireflies to their conferencing tools and calendar. An AI assistant (Fred) automatically joins scheduled meetings and records audio, then generates a transcript and summary after the meeting ends. The transcript is searchable, with speaker labels and timestamps. AI analysis highlights action items, decisions, and questions. Integrated with CRM tools, Fireflies can auto-push meeting notes and follow-up tasks directly into sales or recruitment workflows.

Key Features

Core Capabilities

  • Automatic meeting transcription
  • Speaker identification and labels
  • AI meeting summary and action items
  • Searchable transcript archive
  • Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex integration

Advanced Capabilities

  • CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Conversation intelligence filters
  • Topic tracking across meetings
  • API access for custom integrations
  • Team workspace and analytics

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Strong CRM integration for sales and recruiting workflows
  • Searchable across all past meeting transcripts
  • Automatic meeting join requires minimal setup
  • Topic and keyword tracking enables coaching use cases
  • Integrates with virtually all major conferencing platforms

Cons

  • Automatic joining requires prior notification in some jurisdictions
  • Transcription accuracy affected by audio quality and accents
  • Privacy and consent considerations for recorded meetings
  • Free plan is limited in transcription hours
  • AI summary quality varies with meeting structure

Pricing

Fireflies.ai offers a free plan with limited monthly transcription minutes and basic features. Pro plans increase transcription limits, add full AI analysis, CRM integrations, and conversation intelligence features. Business and Enterprise plans add advanced analytics, higher storage, and team management. Billing is per seat on monthly or annual terms.

How It Compares

Fireflies.ai competes most directly with Otter.ai, tl;dv, and Fathom. Otter focuses more on real-time transcription with broad use cases. tl;dv targets video meeting clips for sharing specific moments. Fathom is free for individuals with strong ease of use. Fireflies differentiates on its CRM integration depth, sales team focus, and ability to search and filter across large meeting archives.
