Cleanup.pictures

Cleanup.pictures is a deeply specialized, single-purpose AI image editing tool designed exclusively for "inpainting"—the process of removing unwanted objects, text, or people from an image while seamlessly reconstructing the background behind them. Powered by advanced machine learning models (initially LaMa - Large Mask Inpainting), it performs this specific task faster and often better than complex legacy desktop software.

Its primary differentiator is its established simplicity. It fundamentally removes the need to understand Photoshop concepts like the "Clone Stamp" or "Healing Brush." Users simply paint over the object they want gone, and the AI algorithm analyzes the surrounding pixels, lighting, and textures to natively generate a photorealistic background replacement in seconds.

It is heavily utilized by real estate agents removing clutter from listing photos, e-commerce managers removing watermarks or logos from product shots, and social media managers rapidly cleaning up vacation or event photography on their phones.

How It Works

A user visits the web app (on desktop or mobile) and uploads a photo. Using their mouse or finger, they adjust a brush size and highlight the unwanted element—for example, a trash can on a sidewalk. The highlighted area is masked in bright green. Upon releasing the brush, the AI processes the image and physically "paints in" the missing sidewalk and wall textures, effectively erasing the trash can from existence accurately.

Key Features

Core Editing

  • AI-powered object removal (Inpainting)
  • Text and watermark erasure
  • Adjustable brush sizing
  • Real-time rendering

Workflow & Export

  • Cross-platform web interface (Mobile & Desktop)
  • High-resolution export (Pro tier)
  • Before/After toggle preview
  • API access for developers

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • highly fast and exceptionally easy to use; requires zero technical design skill
  • The underlying LaMa AI model reconstructs complex backgrounds (like brick walls or repeating patterns) far better than basic clone stamping
  • Works accurately via mobile web browsers without needing to download an app
  • The API allows developers to integrate the removal tool directly into their own SaaS platforms

Limitations

  • Does one thing only; it cannot generate new objects, adjust colors, or crop images
  • The AI can occasionally struggle with highly complex, intersecting geometry (like removing a chain-link fence from in front of a person's face)
  • Free tier heavily restricts the resolution of the exported image (typically capping at 720p)
  • Does not support complex layer management or non-destructive editing workflows

Pricing

Cleanup.pictures offers a robust Free tier that allows unlimited edits but enforces a strict resolution limitation (usually 720p) on the downloaded final image. The Pro tier—available as a monthly/yearly subscription or via a one-time API credit purchase—unlocks unlimited full-resolution downloads and faster processing times.

How It Compares

Cleanup.pictures competes with the "Generative Fill" features in Adobe Photoshop, and mobile apps like Retouch or Canva's capability Eraser. Photoshop is vastly more powerful but requires a heavy desktop installation, high cost, and technical knowledge. Canva is excellent but requires navigating a broader design ecosystem. Cleanup.pictures dominates its specific niche by offering the fastest, lowest-friction, highest-quality dedicated erasure tool available instantly via a simple web URL.
