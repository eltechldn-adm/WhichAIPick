CapCut

CapCut is a free video editing application developed by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok. It is available on mobile and desktop and has grown into one of the most-used video editing tools globally, particularly among social media creators producing short-form content for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

CapCut includes a range of AI-powered features integrated into the editing workflow, including AI background removal, AI-powered video effects, text-to-speech, auto captions with styling, and script-to-video generation. These features lower the barrier to producing polished video content for non-professional editors.

CapCut's strength is its combination of no-cost access, deep TikTok social layer integration, and a comprehensive AI feature set that makes polished mobile and short-form video production accessible to anyone with a phone.

How It Works

Users import video clips, images, and audio into the CapCut timeline editor on mobile or desktop. AI features are accessible through dedicated menus — auto captions generate and sync subtitles from audio, AI background removal isolates subjects, and template-based editing allows users to apply viral editing styles to their footage. Script-to-video takes written content and generates a video with AI-selected visuals, voiceover, and captions. Finished videos can be exported and published directly to social platforms.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Free with a comprehensive feature set
  • Deep TikTok integration and trending template access
  • Strong AI features for a no-cost tool
  • Mobile-first with a capable desktop version
  • Auto captions save significant editing time

Cons

  • ByteDance ownership raises data privacy concerns in some markets
  • Less capable for long-form professional video production
  • Watermark on some export options requires workarounds
  • Advanced features require CapCut Pro
  • Reliance on TikTok ecosystem may not suit all brand contexts

Pricing

CapCut is free for all basic and most AI features. CapCut Pro removes export watermarks, unlocks more effects and templates, and provides access to additional AI tools at a monthly or annual subscription price. The free tier includes a substantial tool set sufficient for social media content production.

How It Compares

CapCut competes with InVideo, Clipchamp, and Adobe Premiere Rush in the consumer video editing space. For social-first short-form content, CapCut's trends and templates library (tied to TikTok) is a unique advantage. Adobe Premiere Rush and Elements are more professional but paid. InVideo focuses more on marketing video templates and AI generation. CapCut differentiates on price (free) and mobile-first social content optimisation.
