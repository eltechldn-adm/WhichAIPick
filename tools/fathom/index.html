Fathom

Fathom is an AI-powered meeting assistant designed to record, transcribe, highlight, and summarize video conference calls on platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. It operates as an unobtrusive bot that joins calls to handle all note-taking responsibilities, allowing participants to focus entirely on the conversation.

Fathom separates itself in a crowded market through its proactive focus on simplicity and its completely free core product for individuals. It emphasizes real-time highlighting: users can click a button during a call to flag a moment, and Fathom automatically extracts that specific clip and transcript section into a neat summary afterward.

It is heavily adopted by sales professionals, customer success managers, and remote teams who need to sync meeting outcomes directly into CRM platforms (like Salesforce or HubSpot) without manual data entry.

Visit Fathom

Best For

  • Sales and customer success teams using CRMs
  • Remote managers conducting frequent 1-on-1s
  • Recruiters conducting multiple daily interviews
  • Individuals seeking a free, high-quality meeting recorder

How It Works

A user installs the Fathom app or browser extension. When a Zoom, Meet, or Teams call begins, the Fathom bot automatically requests to join (providing mandatory recording consent notices to participants). During the call, Fathom transcribes the dialogue in real-time. The user has a side panel with buttons (e.g., "Action Item", "Insight", "Positive"). Clicking a button flags the current conversation. After the call, an AI generates a structured summary, and the flagged highlights are packaged as shareable video clips.

Key Features

Recording & Transcription

  • Bot integration (Zoom, Meet, Teams)
  • Real-time multi-language transcription
  • Live highlighting tags
  • Automatic AI meeting summaries

Sharing & Integration

  • Deep CRM synchronization (HubSpot, Salesforce)
  • Shareable video clips and playlists
  • Slack and Zapier integrations
  • Team-wide searchable meeting repository

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The core recording and transcription product for individuals is 100% free with no limits
  • CRM integrations are seamless and automatic
  • Live highlighting buttons prevent the need to review the entire transcript later
  • Provides localized recording consent warnings to maintain compliance

Cons

  • Requires a visible bot to join the call, which some external clients may find intrusive
  • Audio quality relies heavily on the constraints of the conferencing software connection
  • Advanced team analytics require expensive enterprise plans
  • Not ideal for highly confidential board meetings where local recording (like Krisp) is preferred

Pricing

Fathom offers an immensely popular Free tier for individuals that provides unlimited recording, transcription, and summarization. Paid Team Edition plans provide team-wide searchable repositories, advanced strict CRM syncing, unified billing, and conversational analytics for sales coaching.

How It Compares

Fathom competes directly with Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and native tools like Zoom AI Companion. Fireflies offers slightly more robust developer APIs and custom conversational intelligence tracking for large sales teams. Otter is excellent for general transcription and university lectures. Fathom differentiates by offering the best completely free individual tier on the market, combined with an extremely intuitive live-highlighting UI that appeals accurate$2 to individual sales and success reps.
