Avoma

Avoma is a comprehensive AI meeting assistant and conversation intelligence platform tailored specifically for B2B sales, customer success, and high-velocity meeting environments. While many tools simply record and transcribe audio, Avoma covers the entire lifecycle of a meeting: from collaborative pre-meeting agendas to live bookmarking, robust AI summarization, and deep integration deep into Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems.

Its core differentiator is its focus on structured, actionable outputs. Instead of returning a large-scale wall of transcribed text, Avoma's AI automatically extracts and categorizes information into distinct business buckets—such as "Next Steps," "Pain Points," "Competitor Mentions," and "Budget Discussions."

It is heavily utilized by mid-market Revenue Operations (RevOps) teams to ensure that critical deal intelligence is never lost, reducing the administrative burden of manual CRM data entry for sales representatives by seamlessly syncing AI-generated notes directly to appropriate Salesforce or HubSpot records.

Best For

  • B2B Sales and Account Executive teams
  • Customer Success and Onboarding managers
  • Revenue Operations leaders focusing on CRM hygiene
  • Organizations seeking a unified meeting workflow tool

How It Works

Users connect Avoma to their calendar (Google/Outlook). The Avoma bot automatically joins designated Zoom, Teams, or Meet calls. Before the meeting, users can utilize shared agenda templates. During the meeting, the AI transcribes the conversation with speaker identification. Post-meeting, the AI analyzes the transcript to generate categorized summaries. Representatives review the automated notes, edit them if necessary, and Avoma pushes the final, structured data directly to the corresponding Contact and Opportunity fields in the CRM.

Key Features

Meeting Management

  • Collaborative agenda templates
  • Automatic calendar recording
  • Live bookmarking
  • Multi-participant note editing

AI & Intelligence

  • Categorized AI summaries (Pain points, Next Steps)
  • Competitor and keyword tracking
  • Talk-time analytics by speaker
  • Deep native CRM syncing (Salesforce, HubSpot)

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The categorization of summaries (grouping by pain points/action items) is exceptionally practical for B2B reps
  • Significantly tighter CRM integrations than standalone transcription apps
  • Combining agendas and notes into a single platform handles the entire meeting lifecycle
  • Provides high-end conversation intelligence (similar to Gong) at a more accessible tier

Limitations

  • The visual presence of the recording bot is required, which some clients may question
  • To extract maximum value, teams must adapt to the platform's specific workflow and CRM mapping structures
  • Pricing increases significantly when unlocking the most advanced, pipeline-wide revenue intelligence features
  • Non-sales teams may find the intense CRM focus unnecessary

Pricing

Avoma offers a Free tier for basic transcription and collaborative agendas. Its paid tiers (Starter, Plus, Business) scale up to unlock AI-generated structured summaries, advanced CRM syncing, and broad organizational conversation intelligence capabilities targeting sales leadership.

How It Compares

Avoma essentially bridges the gap between individual note-takers (like Otter or Fathom) and large-scale enterprise revenue platforms (like Gong or Chorus). Otter is great for a university student; Avoma is terrible for a student. Gong analyzes a 1,000-person sales floor; Avoma provides similar strategic coaching intelligence but anchors heavily on giving the *individual rep* the ultimate pre-to-post meeting workflow tool, differentiating itself through its intense CRM hygiene and categorized action-item extraction.
