Describely

Describely is an AI-powered e-commerce content generation platform built specifically to automate the creation of large-scale product catalogs. While general AI writers can write a single product description, Describely is engineered for scale: processing hundreds or thousands of SKUs simultaneously to generate SEO-optimized titles, bullet points, and robust meta descriptions for online retailers.

Its primary differentiator is its deep integration into the e-commerce backend (acting as a Product Information Management (PIM) system overlay) and its bulk-generation capabilities. A retailer can import a raw CSV from a supplier containing only basic specs (e.g., "Shirt, Cotton, Blue, XL"), and Describely will autonomously flesh out unique, brand-aligned sales copy for the entire catalog in minutes.

It is heavily utilized by Shopify store owners, large-scale dropshipping operations, and e-commerce marketing agencies who understand that rich, unique product descriptions are critical for SEO and conversion rates, but find the manual writing process prohibitively slow and expensive.

Best For

  • Shopify store owners and e-commerce managers
  • High-volume dropshippers and retail arbitragers
  • E-commerce marketing agencies managing multiple catalogs
  • Brands migrating massive amounts of legacy SKUs into a new storefront

How It Works

An e-commerce manager connects their Shopify store to Describely. The platform imports all 5,000 active products. The manager selects 50 "Winter Coats" that currently have terrible, factory-default descriptions. They set the AI rules: "Tone: Energetic, Target Keyword: Waterproof Winter Coat, Length: 2 Paragraphs + 5 Bullets." They click "Generate Bulk." The AI processes all 50 SKUs concurrently, referencing their tags and images to write highly unique descriptions for each. The manager reviews the generated text in a spreadsheet-like interface, clicks "Sync," and the live Shopify pages update instantly.

Key Features

Catalog & PIM

  • Bulk generation functionality (hundreds of SKUs at once)
  • Spreadsheet-style catalog management interface
  • Data extraction from product images
  • SEO scoring and keyword injection mechanisms

E-commerce Integrations

  • Native, two-way sync with Shopify
  • Direct integrations with WooCommerce and Wix
  • Amazon listing optimization formatting
  • Bulk CSV import/export

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The bulk generation capability is an established game-changer, turning weeks of manual copywriting into a 10-minute automated task
  • Native two-way CMS syncing (e.g., Shopify) removes the nightmare of copy/pasting text back and forth between platforms
  • The spreadsheet-style interface is deeply familiar and efficient for e-commerce managers used to managing catalogs
  • Forcing SEO keyword inclusion across an entire bulk run guarantees a baseline level of search visibility

Cons

  • It is a hyper-specialized tool; it is useless for writing long-form blog posts, emails, or creative fiction
  • If the initial supplier data or product imagery is completely devoid of details, the AI will naturally struggle to generate accurate, non-generated long-form text
  • Pricing is structured around "Generations" (usage limits), which can become very expensive for enterprise retailers with catalogs pushing 50,000+ SKUs
  • The UI, while functional for data, is utilitarian and lacks the sleek, modern feel of consumer-facing AI apps

Pricing

Describely operates strictly on a B2B SaaS model. Tiers are gated heavily by the number of products/SKUs a user needs to manage and the total volume of AI words generated per month. Advanced integrations (like native Shopify syncing) are typically reserved for the mid-to-high tiers.

How It Compares

Describely competes with tools like Hypotenuse AI and Copysmith. Copysmith is a highly robust enterprise marketing tool that includes e-commerce features. Hypotenuse AI is highly strong at generating individual, highly creative product single pages. Describely differentiates itself by functioning almost like a high-speed Product Information Management (PIM) system attached to an AI. If a user needs to write one amazing product page, Hypotenuse is great; if a user needs to rewrite 10,000 product pages by Friday, Describely is the established choice.
