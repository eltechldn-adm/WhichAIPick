Perplexity Pages

Perplexity Pages is a formalized publishing feature integrated into the Perplexity AI search engine. Perplexity traditionally functions as an interactive, conversational search interface, synthesizing information from the web into cited conversational summaries. Pages extends this capability by allowing users to compile, edit, and publish these AI-generated research outputs into structured, shareable web documents directly on the platform.

The feature operates by prompting the user for a core topic or research question. The Perplexity engine then autonomously queries the web, extracts relevant data, and drafts a comprehensive, formatted article complete with internal citations, section headers, and generated or sourced imagery. Crucially, the user retains editorial control, able to modify the text, adjust the visual presentation, and append new research queries to expand specific sections before publishing the document publicly.

It is utilized by educators compiling resource guides, researchers disseminating synthesized topical overviews, and content marketers generating foundational drafts based on complex industry queries.

Best For

  • Researchers requiring structured summaries of complex, multi-faceted topics
  • Educators drafting reference materials and study guides
  • Knowledge workers compiling and sharing internal industry briefs

How It Works

A user logging into Perplexity selects "Create Page" and enters the topic "The History of Submarine Cable Networks." The engine executes multiple search operations, synthesizes the historical data, and generates a formatted article with distinct sections for origins, modern architecture, and environmental vulnerabilities. The user reviews the output, highlighting a section on fiber-optics to prompt the AI to "expand on bandwidth evolution." The AI retrieves further data and integrates it. The user adjusts the header image and clicks publish, generating a public URL for sharing.

Key Features

Content Generation

  • Topic-to-Article synthesis
  • Section-specific expansion capabilities
  • Automated citation and source inclusion

Publishing & Editorial

  • Rich text editing interface
  • Visual media integration
  • Public domain URL generation and sharing

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Substantially accelerates the transition from reading search results to producing a structured, shareable document
  • The inclusion of visible citations mitigates the risk of referencing undocumented hallucinations commonly found in other generative text
  • Content naturally updates dynamically as users prompt the system to research current developments

Cons

  • The resulting articles often bear the structurally generic, predictable tone characteristic of AI generation without significant human editorial intervention
  • Published pages exist within the Perplexity ecosystem, presenting challenges for SEO strategies reliant on self-hosted domains
  • Users must vigilantly verify the underlying sources, as the AI’s synthesis of complex web data can occasionally flatten necessary nuanced context

Pricing

Perplexity Pages is available to all users. However, access to the more advanced underlying models (such as GPT-4 or Claude 3) for driving the research quality is restricted to the Perplexity Pro subscription tier, which operates on a standard monthly commercial rate.

How It Compares

Perplexity Pages bridges the gap between conversational search (like Google Gemini) and content generation platforms (like Jasper). While standard search engines provide links, and generation platforms write un-cited copy, Pages uniquely focuses on synthesizing cited search intelligence directly into formatted, public-facing knowledge artifacts.
