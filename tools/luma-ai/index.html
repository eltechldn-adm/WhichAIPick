Luma AI

Luma AI is an AI video and 3D content generation platform. It offers Dream Machine, a text-to-video and image-to-video generation model, and also provides tools for capturing 3D scenes from photographs using neural radiance fields (NeRF) technology. Luma was founded in 2021 and focuses on AI-generated visual media for creative and commercial applications.

Dream Machine gained significant attention in 2024 for producing high-quality, physically realistic video from text prompts and reference images. The platform positions itself as a professional-grade creative tool for filmmakers, designers, and visual content producers.

Luma AI targets creative professionals seeking high physical coherence in AI-generated video — a known limitation of other video generation tools — along with product teams needing 3D capture without specialist hardware.

How It Works

For Dream Machine video generation, users enter a text prompt and optionally provide a reference image as the first frame. The model generates a short video clip (typically 5–10 seconds) with motion and physical coherence. Multiple clips can be generated from the same prompt and stitched together. For 3D capture, users upload a series of photographs taken around an object or space, and Luma AI processes them into a navigable 3D model. Both outputs can be downloaded or shared.

Key Features

Dream Machine (Video)

  • Text-to-video generation
  • Image-to-video generation
  • Physically coherent motion
  • Clip extension
  • Multiple output resolution options

3D Capture

  • Photo-based 3D scene capture
  • NeRF-based model generation
  • 3D model download and embed
  • Web-embeddable interactive 3D viewer
  • API for developer integration

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Strong physical coherence in video generation
  • Unique combined offering of video and 3D capture
  • Image-to-video capability for precise starting frame control
  • API access for programmatic video generation
  • Improving quality with each model update

Cons

  • Video clip duration is limited (seconds per generation)
  • Credit costs can increase rapidly at production volumes
  • Output consistency varies with prompt complexity
  • 3D capture quality depends on photograph quality and coverage
  • Less widespread adoption ecosystem than Runway

Pricing

Luma AI offers a free tier with a limited monthly generation credit allocation. Paid plans increase monthly credits and provide access to higher resolution outputs and priority generation. API access is available for developers with usage-based pricing. Enterprise agreements for high-volume commercial use are available.

How It Compares

Luma AI Dream Machine competes most directly with Runway Gen-3, Pika, and Sora (when available). Runway is more established with a broader toolkit for professional video workflows. Pika is simpler and well-suited for casual creators. Luma differentiates on physical realism and its unique combination of video generation with 3D scene capture. Sora (OpenAI) remains the aspirational benchmark but has limited public availability.
