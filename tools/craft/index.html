Craft

Craft (or Craft.do) is a premium, highly visual document creation and knowledge management platform available on Mac, iOS, Windows, and Web. While initially positioned as a competitor to Notion, Craft differentiates itself with a relentless focus on native performance, aesthetic excellence, and the creation of highly polished, shareable documents rather than complex relational databases.

Its AI integration, the "Craft Assistant," acts as an omnipresent editor and ghostwriter within the document. It focuses heavily on structured rewriting, translation, and text generation natively integrated into Craft's unique block-based architecture.

It is predominantly used by creative agencies, product managers, and executives who prioritize presenting beautiful, structured information (like product specs, client proposals, or mood boards) to external stakeholders or internal teams smoothly and instantly.

Best For

  • Creative agencies producing client-facing proposals
  • Executives and product managers creating structured briefs
  • Users who prioritize native desktop/mobile app performance and aesthetics
  • Teams needing a beautiful, fast alternative to Google Docs or Notion

How It Works

A user opens a new document in the highly responsive, native Mac or iOS app. They type out a messy brainstorm for a client proposal. They highlight the text, hit a keyboard shortcut, and prompt the Craft Assistant to "Rewrite this into a professional, persuasive pitch." The AI instantly reformats the text. Because Craft is block-based, the AI respects structural elements like headers, toggles, and bullet points. The user embellishes the document with high-res images and beautiful gradient card styling, then generates a single "Secret Link" to share the accurate$2 formatted web page instantly with the client.

Key Features

AI Assistant

  • In-line text generation and expansion
  • Structured rewriting (Professional, Casual, Concise)
  • Instant multilingual translation
  • Document summarization and synthesis

Document Engine

  • Lightning-fast native applications (Mac/iOS focus)
  • Highly aesthetic, visual block styling and cards
  • Instant, beautiful web-publishing via Secret Link
  • Robust offline mode and collaboration

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The sheer aesthetic quality and speed of the native applications completely outclasses web-heavy electron apps like Notion
  • The shared web links look like custom-built websites, making client proposals look highly professional instantly
  • The AI integration is seamless, respecting the visual block structure rather than just dumping raw text
  • Superb offline capabilities ensure continuity even without internet access (a major flaw in Notion)

Cons

  • Lacks the deep, complex relational databases and formulas that make Notion a powerhouse for operational task tracking
  • AI features are mostly text-based (summarize, rewrite); lacks advanced programmatic automation or complex data sorting AI
  • While it has Windows/Web versions, the platform is undeniably optimized for and heavily favors the Apple ecosystem
  • The extreme focus on visual hierarchy can occasionally feel restrictive compared to a raw, unstructured canvas like Obsidian

Pricing

Craft offers a Free tier suitable for individuals, restricted primarily by the total number of blocks (elements) a user can create. Subscription tiers (Pro, Business, Enterprise) unlock unlimited blocks, team collaboration features, advanced analytics on shared external links, and higher limits for AI Assistant queries.

How It Compares

Craft competes directly with Notion, Obsidian, and Google Docs. Google Docs is the generic standard. Obsidian is the hyper-customizable tool for technical "second brain" thinkers. Notion is the ultimate operational database. Craft differentiates itself by proactively prioritizing *presentation* and *speed*. It is the established choice for users whose primary goal is to draft thoughts quickly, use AI to polish the text, and instantly share the most visually stunning document possible without fighting complex formatting menus.
