Conker

Conker is an AI-powered formative assessment and quiz creation platform designed specifically for educators, instructional designers, and school institutions. Created by Mote Technologies, Conker enables teachers to generate curriculum-aligned quizzes, comprehension tests, and practice worksheets from any topic, reading passage, or source article in a matter of seconds.

Rather than manually authoring dozens of multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions, teachers can customize assessments based on target grade levels, national curriculum standards (such as NGSS, TEKS, and Common Core), and accessibility requirements. Quizzes can be assigned directly to students through shareable web links, exported seamlessly to Google Forms and Canvas LMS, or printed as clean physical worksheets with complete answer keys.

Best For

  • K-12 teachers and university instructors wanting to generate curriculum-aligned check-for-understanding quizzes in seconds
  • Educators differentiating instructional assessments for mixed-ability classrooms and English-language learners (ELL)
  • Schools and school districts heavily integrated with Google Classroom, Google Forms, and Canvas LMS ecosystems

Not Ideal For

  • Corporate HR departments seeking high-stakes employee certification testing with strict proctoring and identity verification
  • Advanced technical coding instructors requiring automated software code execution and unit test sandboxing
  • Casual trivia creators looking for gamified live team buzzer animations in the style of Kahoot

How It Works

Teachers begin by visiting Conker and choosing a prompt method: entering a specific educational topic, pasting a source reading text (up to 5,000 words on Pro), or linking an external web article. Teachers select target parameters including grade level (from elementary through higher education), question count, question types (multiple choice, true/false, multi-select, or fill-in-the-blank), and language.

Conker's AI generates the assessment questions, providing verified answer keys and contextual rationales explaining why specific answers are correct. Teachers can edit questions, shuffle choices, and add custom items. Once finalized, the quiz can be launched in Conker's student-friendly online mode, shared via QR code, exported to Google Forms with one click, or downloaded as a printable PDF worksheet.

Key Features

Assessment Generation & Curriculum Alignment

  • Rapid quiz generation from custom topics, pasted reading texts, and web article URLs
  • Curriculum standard alignment covering NGSS, TEKS, Common Core, and UK national curricula
  • Multiple question formats including Multiple Choice, Multi-Select, True/False, and Fill-in-the-Blank
  • Integrated read-aloud voice accessibility powered by Mote audio technology for emergent and ELL readers

Export, Grading & LMS Integrations

  • One-click direct export to Google Forms, allowing automated student response collection and grading
  • Canvas LMS integration supporting seamless assignment creation and gradebook synchronization
  • Print-ready worksheet exports generating clean student question pages and teacher answer keys
  • Student response analytics providing instant question-level breakdown to identify classroom learning gaps

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Drastically reduces teacher preparation time by generating high-quality comprehension assessments in under a minute
  • Direct integration with Google Forms and Google Classroom fits naturally into existing public school workflows
  • Built-in audio read-aloud support ensures accessibility for early readers, dyslexic students, and English learners
  • Very affordable individual teacher pricing ($5.99/month) with a functional free tier for occasional use

Limitations

  • Free tier restricts teachers to sharing 5 quizzes and collecting a maximum of 50 student responses total
  • Input source text on the free plan is capped at 1,200 words, requiring truncation of longer chapters
  • Does not provide gamified, competitive live classroom animations like Kahoot or Quizizz
  • Canvas LMS integration and centralized administrative roster management require a custom Schools contract

Pricing

Conker offers a functional Free plan for individual teachers alongside affordable Pro subscriptions and custom district licensing.

The Free Plan ($0 per month) allows educators to create and share up to 5 quizzes, collect up to 50 total student responses, create up to 10 questions per quiz, and input up to 1,200 words of source reading material. The Pro Plan is priced at $5.99 per month (or approximately $4.99 per month billed annually at roughly $59.99 per year), providing unlimited quizzes, unlimited student responses, up to 40 questions per quiz, expanded 5,000-word reading input, advanced quiz analytics, and priority support. The Schools Plan provides custom institutional pricing for schools and districts, adding Canvas LMS integration, district administrator dashboards, and custom branding. Conker complies with standard student data privacy regulations (FERPA and COPPA).

How It Compares

Compared to Diffit (/tools/diffit/), which focuses on adapting reading materials into leveled passages, vocabulary lists, and interactive student activities, Conker is focused specifically on assessment generation, quiz question authoring, and direct Google Forms / Canvas export. Compared to general AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Conker formats questions with answer keys, aligns content directly with K-12 learning standards, and provides student-safe testing interfaces without ad tracking.
