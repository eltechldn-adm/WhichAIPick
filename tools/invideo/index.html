InVideo

InVideo is an AI-powered video creation platform that enables users to produce marketing videos, social content, and explainer videos from text scripts or AI-generated content. It provides a large library of customisable templates and an AI workflow that converts a topic or script into a complete video with selected footage, voiceover, captions, and background music.

InVideo is used by marketers, content creators, and businesses that need to produce video content at volume without specialist video production skills or expensive software. Its AI-driven workflow makes it particularly accessible for teams producing frequent social or marketing video output.

InVideo competes primarily with Pictory and Lumen5 in the AI script-to-video category, positioning itself as a more feature-complete platform with broader template and media library options.

Best For

  • Marketing teams producing social and ad video content
  • Content creators repurposing text content into video
  • Small businesses producing product and explainer videos
  • Social media managers maintaining video publishing schedules
  • Agencies producing video for multiple clients at scale

How It Works

Users select a workflow — script to video, article to video, or AI-generated from a topic — and InVideo generates a video draft with suggested footage clips, a voiceover (AI-generated or text-to-speech), captions, and background music. Users can edit individual scenes, swap media, adjust timing, and customise text within InVideo's browser-based editor. Finished videos are rendered and exported or shared directly to social platforms.

Key Features

Core Capabilities

  • Script-to-video generation
  • Template library (6,000+ templates)
  • AI voiceover generation
  • Auto captions
  • Stock media library (iStock and Shutterstock integration)

Advanced Capabilities

  • AI topic-to-video generation
  • Brand kit with custom colours and fonts
  • Screen recording integration
  • Team collaboration features
  • Multi-platform export presets

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Large template library for rapid video production
  • AI topic and script to video reduces production friction significantly
  • Stock media integration avoids separate asset sourcing
  • Multi-platform export presets
  • Accessible for non-video professionals

Cons

  • Output quality depends on template and stock media selection
  • Final videos require editing to avoid generic appearance
  • AI voiceover quality varies by language and style
  • Free plan has watermark on exports
  • Less suitable for narrative or documentary video production

Pricing

InVideo offers a free plan with limited exports and InVideo watermark on output. Paid plans remove the watermark, increase export limits, expand the media library access, and unlock team features. Business plans add team collaboration and higher storage. Pricing is per account on monthly or annual billing.

How It Compares

InVideo competes with Pictory and Lumen5 in the AI script-to-video space. Pictory is better known for video repurposing from long-form content. Lumen5 targets social media video with a simpler interface. InVideo offers a broader feature set including editing, templates, and media library depth. For motion and AI avatar-based video, Synthesia and HeyGen are different category tools targeting different production needs.
