Clipchamp

Clipchamp is a web-based, non-linear video editing platform that was acquired by Microsoft in 2021 and subsequently integrated directly into Windows 11 as the operating system's default video editor. It is designed to make video editing accessible to beginners and casual creators while running entirely in the browser (or as a lightweight desktop app).

The platform heavily emphasizes template-based creation, stock media integration, and automated AI tools over the complex timeline management found in professional software. Key AI features include automatic subtitle generation, text-to-speech voiceovers, and an "Auto Create" feature that automatically trims and sequences uploaded photos and videos according to a chosen style.

Clipchamp targets home users, educators, and small business marketers who need to quickly edit social media clips, tutorial videos, or family montages without the learning curve or hardware requirements of traditional rendering engines.

Visit Clipchamp

Best For

  • Windows 11 users needing built-in video editing
  • Beginner video editors and casual creators
  • Educators creating screen-capture tutorials
  • Small business marketers producing basic social content

How It Works

Users drag and drop media into a multi-track web timeline. From the sidebar menus, they can apply filters, transitions, and text overlays similarly to basic professional tools. Clicking the Auto Create button allows the AI to analyze uploaded media, pick pacing, and cut a video to music automatically. Users can generate voiceovers by typing text and selecting an Azure-powered AI voice, or use the auto-captioning tool to generate subtitles directly from the audio track.

Key Features

Core Editing

  • Web-based multi-track timeline
  • Direct screen and webcam recording
  • Transitions, filters, and effect library
  • Green screen removal

AI & Automation

  • Auto Create smart editing
  • Azure-powered text-to-speech voices
  • Automatic subtitle generation
  • Stock media repository integration

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Extremely easy to learn interface
  • Runs highly well in a web browser without heavy hardware
  • Text-to-speech voice quality is excellent (Microsoft Azure powered)
  • Direct integration with OneDrive and Windows OS

Cons

  • Lacks advanced color grading or complex keyframing for professionals
  • High-quality 4K export and premium stock access require a paid subscription
  • Editing large format, heavy files (like raw 4K) can lag in-browser
  • Heavily reliant on stable internet connection for asset loading

Pricing

Clipchamp offers a free tier that allows for 1080p watermark-free exports using basic tools. The Premium subscription unlocks 4K exports, premium stock audio/video, advanced filters, and full access to the brand kit for commercial usage.

How It Compares

Clipchamp competes with CapCut, Canva's video capability, and legacy tools like iMovie. CapCut is significantly stronger for viral TikTok trends and complex mobile editing. Canva is superior for graphic-heavy motion design. Clipchamp excels as the modern replacement for Windows Movie Maker: a highly accessible, timeline-based editor that works universally on desktop browsers with strong built-in Microsoft AI services.
