Castmagic

Castmagic is an AI-powered content repurposing workspace designed around existing audio and video recordings. It helps podcasters, marketers, educators and media teams transform spoken material into written content, promotional assets and searchable resources.

After a recording is imported, Castmagic can create transcripts, summaries, show notes, newsletters, blog drafts, social posts and suggested highlights. Its reusable prompts and collaborative Spaces help teams create multiple formats from the same source rather than starting each piece of content from scratch.

Castmagic is primarily a post-recording content production platform. Although it includes media clipping tools, it should not be mistaken for a complete professional video-editing workstation.

Best For

  • Podcasters turning episodes into written and social content.
  • Marketing teams repurposing webinars, interviews and long-form video.
  • Content agencies managing several recording libraries and repeatable production workflows.

Not Ideal For

  • Users needing sophisticated multitrack audio or video timeline editing.
  • People who want a permanent free content-creation plan.
  • Buyers expecting transcription-hour allowances to reset every month.

How It Works

Users import an existing recording or connect a supported content source, such as a podcast feed or video platform. Castmagic transcribes the material, identifies speakers and timestamps, and makes the transcript available for searching, editing and reuse.

Users select content templates or provide their own prompts to generate articles, show notes, newsletters or promotional copy. They can refine drafts using Magic Chat, organise projects into Spaces and create selected clips or audiograms.

Generated material still needs editorial checking. Automatic transcription can mishear names, technical terms or unclear speech, and generated summaries may omit important context.

Key Features

Recording transcription and content library

  • Timestamped, speaker-labelled transcripts
  • Support for more than 60 languages
  • Imports from files and supported recording or publishing services

Content generation and repurposing

  • Custom prompts, show notes, newsletters, blog drafts and social posts
  • Clips, carousels and audiograms created from existing recordings

Collaboration and integrations

  • Project Spaces, shared recording libraries and Magic Chat
  • RSS-based workflows, Zapier and API integrations on qualifying plans

Practical Workflows

  1. Podcast publishing: Import a recorded episode, produce a transcript and draft show notes, then prepare a newsletter and selected social posts for editorial review.
  2. Webinar repurposing: Upload a webinar, identify key discussion points and turn them into blog sections, short highlights and a follow-up email.
  3. Client content production: Organise recordings in separate Spaces, apply reusable prompts and review draft outputs before delivering them to different clients.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Turns a single recording into multiple reusable content formats.
  • Supports custom prompts and repeatable publishing workflows.
  • Provides searchable transcripts and shared project organisation.
  • Includes media repurposing capabilities alongside written content generation.

Limitations

  • Generated content needs fact-checking and tone editing.
  • Not a replacement for advanced multitrack editing software.
  • No permanent free subscription tier.
  • Additional transcription capacity and plan-specific integrations may affect costs.

Pricing

Castmagic offers paid Hobby, Starter, Team and larger Business & Scale plans, with a trial option.

Hobby costs $29/month on monthly billing or $239/year paid annually. Starter costs $69/month or $579/year. Team costs $199/month or $1,669/year, with five included seats. Business & Scale pricing starts from advertised higher-volume rates and may require discussing specific requirements with sales.

The most important limitation is that included transcription hours are library totals, not monthly allowances. Hobby includes 30 hours, Starter 100 hours and Team 400 hours. Extra hours can be purchased.

The website displays rounded-down monthly equivalents for annual plans, so buyers should rely on the exact annual amount charged. API availability is listed for Starter and Team in the feature comparison, although buyers should confirm their required access because the vendor's FAQ emphasises API workflows for teams.

How It Compares

Choose Castmagic when the goal is to turn recorded conversations and presentations into multiple publishing assets.

Consider Descript when detailed transcript-based audio and video editing is more important. Consider Riverside when recording quality and remote studio capture are the main priorities. Consider OpusClip when automated short-form video clipping is your central requirement.

Castmagic makes the most sense when your bottleneck is creating and organising useful content after recording.
