Grain

Grain records meetings and creates searchable notes for shared workspaces. Recording seats and free viewer seats have different permissions.

Visit Website Compare Grain

Editorial Overview

Grain is an AI-powered meeting recording and conversation intelligence platform designed to capture and share spoken customer feedback across teams. While enterprise revenue intelligence platforms focus heavily on sales pipeline forecasting, Grain concentrates on meeting recording, transcription, and collaborative clip sharing for product management, user research, and customer success teams. The core workflow connects to scheduled Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls, records the session, and generates a searchable, time-stamped transcript alongside automated meeting notes. Users can highlight specific transcript passages to instantly generate video clips of critical customer feedback, which can then be shared directly into Slack channels, Notion documents, or supported CRM systems. This structured clipping workflow allows product and engineering teams to review short, context-specific excerpts from user interviews and customer discussions without having to watch full-length meeting recordings.

How it works

Users connect Grain to their calendar. The Grain bot autonomously joins scheduled Google Meet or Zoom calls as a participant. It records the audio/video and generates a live transcript. Post-meeting, the AI generates a brief summary (e.g., "Feature Requests," "Action Items"). A User Researcher reviewing the transcript highlights a paragraph where the customer discusses a competitor. Grain instantly creates a 45-second video clip of that exact moment. The researcher clicks "Share," dropping the video link directly into the engineering team's Slack channel, allowing them to watch the customer's reaction instantly without logging into Grain.

Key Features

Recording & AI Transcription

  • Autonomous calendar bot joining (Zoom, Meet, Teams)
  • High-accuracy, multi-language transcription
  • AI-generated meeting summaries and action items
  • Speaker identification and separation

Clipping & Sharing

  • Text-based video clipping (Highlighting)
  • Multi-clip storytelling "Highlight Reels"
  • Native integrations (Slack, Notion, Jira, CRM)
  • Publicly shareable video links without login barriers

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Highlight-to-clip workflow quickly creates concise video excerpts directly from transcript text
  • Enables cross-functional teams to share specific customer conversation clips without watching full meeting recordings
  • Focused, straightforward user interface without the configuration complexity of enterprise revenue platforms
  • Direct integrations with Slack, Notion, and CRMs simplify sharing customer feedback across organizations

Limitations

  • Lacks the ultra-deep, predictive revenue analytics and competitor-mention tracking required by enterprise VP of Sales (unlike Gong)
  • The bot joining the call as a visible participant can occasionally unnerve highly sensitive prospects if not introduced properly
  • While transcripts are excellent, heavily accented or highly technical jargon can still occasionally confuse the AI
  • Heavy reliance on video storage can quickly eat through limits on the free or lower-tier plans

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Teams sharing meeting evidence with non-recording colleagues

Not Ideal For

  • Free users needing uploaded recordings or unrestricted personal-email bot recording
  • Organizations requiring a verified self-hosted deployment option

  • Pricing Model: freemium

  • Free Tier: Free tier available
  • Free Trial: Free trial available

Grain offers a generous Free tier that allows individual users to transcribe a limited number of meetings per month and share video clips. Paid subscription tiers (Starter, Business, Enterprise) remove meeting limits, unlock the ability to download raw video, provide deep CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce), and allow for shared team workspaces.

How It Compares

Grain competes in the conversation intelligence space against Gong, Chorus, and Otter.ai. Gong and Chorus are large-scale enterprise revenue platforms tailored for sales leadership and pipeline forecasting. Otter.ai serves primarily as a general-purpose meeting transcription assistant. Grain differentiates itself by focusing on product, research, and customer support workflows, emphasizing rapid highlight clipping and easy sharing of specific meeting moments across Slack, Notion, and team documentation.
