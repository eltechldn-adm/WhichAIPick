Grain

Grain is a specialized, AI-powered meeting recording and conversation intelligence platform designed proactively around ease-of-use and the democratization of customer insights. While large-scale enterprise tools focus heavily on sales-coaching and revenue operations, Grain is built for User Researchers, Product Managers, and Customer Success teams to easily capture, synthesize, and share "Voice of the Customer" moments.

Its primary differentiator is its focus on the "Highlight Reel" and cross-functional sharing. Grain's AI auto-joins Zoom or Google Meet calls, generates a highly accurate transcript, and automatically summarizes the meeting into brief notes. However, its true power lies in allowing a product manager to highlight a 30-second transcript segment where a customer complains about a bug, instantly converting that highlight into a shareable video clip pasted directly into a Slack or Notion channel.

It is heavily utilized by agile product teams and UX researchers who need to ensure that the actual engineers writing the code hear the raw, unedited frustration (or joy) of the customer, bypassing lengthy corporate briefs in favor of instant video evidence.

Best For

How It Works

Users connect Grain to their calendar. The Grain bot autonomously joins scheduled Google Meet or Zoom calls as a participant. It records the audio/video and generates a live transcript. Post-meeting, the AI generates a brief summary (e.g., "Feature Requests," "Action Items"). A User Researcher reviewing the transcript highlights a paragraph where the customer discusses a competitor. Grain instantly creates a 45-second video clip of that exact moment. The researcher clicks "Share," dropping the video link directly into the engineering team's Slack channel, allowing them to watch the customer's reaction instantly without logging into Grain.

Key Features

Recording & AI Transcription

  • Autonomous calendar bot joining (Zoom, Meet, Teams)
  • High-accuracy, multi-language transcription
  • AI-generated meeting summaries and action items
  • Speaker identification and separation

Clipping & Sharing

  • Text-based video clipping (Highlighting)
  • Multi-clip storytelling "Highlight Reels"
  • Native integrations (Slack, Notion, Jira, CRM)
  • Publicly shareable video links without login barriers

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The ability to create a video clip simply by highlighting text in the transcript is accurately executed and highly fast
  • It vastly improves cross-departmental communication; engineers actually watch 30-second clips, whereas they ignore 10-page research PDFs
  • The UI is intentionally simple and frictionless, completely avoiding the bloat of large-scale sales-intelligence platforms
  • Unlocking the "Voice of the Customer" and making it shareable in Slack builds immense internal empathy for the user

Cons

  • Lacks the ultra-deep, predictive revenue analytics and competitor-mention tracking required by enterprise VP of Sales (unlike Gong)
  • The bot joining the call as a visible participant can occasionally unnerve highly sensitive prospects if not introduced properly
  • While transcripts are excellent, heavily accented or highly technical jargon can still occasionally confuse the AI
  • Heavy reliance on video storage can quickly eat through limits on the free or lower-tier plans

Pricing

Grain offers a generous Free tier that allows individual users to transcribe a limited number of meetings per month and share video clips. Paid subscription tiers (Starter, Business, Enterprise) remove meeting limits, unlock the ability to download raw video, provide deep CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce), and allow for shared team workspaces.

How It Compares

Grain competes in the conversation intelligence market against Gong, Chorus, and Otter.ai. Gong and Chorus are large-scale, expensive enterprise tools designed specifically for Sales VPs to analyze revenue pipelines. Otter.ai is a generalist transcription utility. Grain differentiates itself by aiming squarely at the *Product and Research* teams. Unconcerned with complex sales forecasting, Grain hyper-focuses on the democratization of video: making it as easy to clip and share a specific customer conversation as it is to send a text message, bridging the gap between the customer and the engineering team.
