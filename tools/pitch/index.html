Pitch

Pitch is an highly fast, modern, and highly collaborative presentation software designed to fundamentally decimate and replace legacy slideware like Microsoft PowerPoint and Google Slides. While originally celebrated purely for its breathtaking UI and real-time multiplayer editing, Pitch has now integrated generative AI deeply into its core, allowing teams to generate stunning, design-accurate$2 startup decks and sales pitches in seconds.

Its primary differentiator is its uncompromising dedication to premium aesthetics and team collaboration. It is not designed to create routine, bullet-pointed corporate lectures. Pitch is engineered for high-stakes moments: startup founders raising series-A rounds, sales teams pitching enterprise software, and agencies presenting large-scale creative concepts. The AI does not just drop text onto a white slide; it generates complex layout blocks, automatically aligns imagery, and strictly enforces brand typography to ensure the deck looks like it was touched by a senior graphic designer.

It is universally adored by forward-thinking startups, creative agencies, and product teams who view beautifully crafted presentations as a critical weapon for winning business, and who despise the clunky, archaic formatting nightmares associated with 20-year-old desktop software.

Best For

  • Startup founders raising venture capital
  • Progressive B2B Sales and Marketing teams
  • Creative, PR, and Advertising agencies
  • Companies desperate to abandon the bloat of Microsoft PowerPoint

How It Works

A founder needs a compelling pitch deck by morning. They open Pitch and type an AI prompt: "A 12-slide seed funding deck for a neo-banking startup disrupting the mortgage industry, utilizing a dark, neon-green cyberpunk aesthetic." The AI autonomously generates the entire structural narrative: the Problem Slide, the Solution Slide, the Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM), and the Team Slide. Crucially, it formats these with complex, beautiful visual layouts, bespoke icons, and tailored copy. The founder then invites their co-founder to the deck. They both edit the same slide simultaneously in real-time. Finally, they share a secure web link with a venture capital firm, allowing the founder to track exactly which slide the investor stopped reading.

Key Features

Creation & AI Design

  • Generative text-to-presentation drafting
  • Strict, unbreakable smart-formatting layouts
  • large-scale template library built by professional designers
  • Rich media embedding (Gifs, YouTube, Figma files, Loom)

Collaboration & Publishing

  • Real-time multiplayer editing natively in the browser
  • Workflow statuses (To-Do, In Progress, Done)
  • Granular link-tracking analytics
  • PDF and PowerPoint export capabilities

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The aesthetic quality of Pitch is unparalleled in the industry; it makes standard Google Slides look intensely archaic and cheap by comparison
  • The AI generation excels because it adheres to strict design principles; it refuses to let the user create ugly, misaligned slides
  • Real-time collaboration is accurately executed, bringing the fluidity of Figma or Google Docs directly to presentation design
  • Link tracking is a large-scale advantage for sales reps, providing established clarity on whether a prospect actually reviewed the proposal

Cons

  • Pitch is heavily focused on beautiful structural design; it completely lacks the insanely complex, dense data-visualization and charting capabilities required by Wall Street bankers living in Excel/PowerPoint
  • The established rigidity of the "smart formatting" can slightly frustrate hardcore graphic designers who want total, chaotic freedom to place elements anywhere
  • Offline capabilities exist but are secondary; Pitch is undeniably a cloud-first, browser-centric application requiring an internet connection to truly shine
  • Animations and complex slide transitions are intentionally restrained compared to the cinematic capabilities of Apple Keynote

Pricing

Pitch offers an exceptionally generous Free tier that allows teams to build full decks collaboratively and utilize standard templates (with a small Pitch watermark). Upgrading to the professional SaaS tiers (Pro, Business) removes the watermark, unlocks heavy workspace analytics, introduces large-scale video-upload limits, and provides deep, custom brand access control.

How It Compares

Pitch competes fiercely with Tome, Gamma, and the legacy giants (PowerPoint/Google Slides). Tome is highly narrative, experimental, and focused heavily on a fluid, "mobile-first" scrolling aesthetic. Gamma is the master of extremely rapid AI outlining. PowerPoint remains the king of established desktop corporate charting. Pitch differentiates itself by being the ultimate *Modern Replacement*. It marries the familiar, horizontal slide structure of PowerPoint with the breathtaking modern UI and multiplayer mechanics of Figma, augmented by AI text-generation—making it the established standard weapon for modern venture capital pitches.
