Chorus.ai

Chorus.ai (acquired by ZoomInfo) is a premier Conversation Intelligence (CI) platform built specifically to support high-performance B2B sales teams. It captures and deeply analyzes a company's customer interactions—across phone calls, video meetings, and emails—to provide actionable insights that increase win rates and shorten sales cycles.

Its primary differentiator is its intense integration into the large-scale ZoomInfo B2B data ecosystem. Chorus doesn't just transcribe a call; it uses proprietary AI to analyze the "Voice of the Customer," identifying specific moments when competitors are mentioned, when pricing objections occur, or when key decision-makers engage. It aggregates this data across the entire sales floor, providing leadership with a macroscopic view of what the top-performing representatives are saying differently from the bottom performers.

It is predominantly utilized by Sales Enablement leaders, Revenue Operations (RevOps) teams, and VP-level sales executives managing large, complex, multi-month enterprise deals who need visibility into the "black box" of their reps' daily customer conversations.

Best For

  • Mid-market to Enterprise B2B sales teams
  • Sales Enablement and Coaching professionals
  • Revenue Operations (RevOps) leadership
  • Teams already heavily invested in the ZoomInfo ecosystem

How It Works

Chorus connects invisibly to a company's communication stack (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Outreach, Salesforce). When an Account Executive conducts a sales call, Chorus records and transcribes the conversation with distinct speaker separation. Post-call, the AI automatically tags specific "Moments" on the audio timeline (e.g., "Pricing Mentioned," "Feature Request," "Next Steps"). Sales managers can log into the dashboard to review an entire week of calls, searching specifically for moments where a certain competitor was named, allowing them to coach reps directly via timestamped comments on the recording.

Key Features

Conversation Intelligence

  • Automated recording and transcription
  • AI-driven "Moment" tagging (Competitors, Objection, Pricing)
  • Talk-vs-Listen ratio analytics
  • Customizable coaching playlists

Enterprise Integrations

  • Deep ZoomInfo data enrichment ecosystem
  • Native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Dialer and email integration (Outreach, Salesloft)
  • Deal intelligence and pipeline health scoring

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The integration with ZoomInfo provides unparalleled contextual data about the companies your reps are talking to
  • Dramatically improves the onboarding speed of new sales reps via curated call playlists
  • Allows managers to coach objectively based on reality, rather than relying on a rep's subjective summary of a call
  • Highly accurate speaker separation and competitor-mention tracking

Cons

  • Pricing is strictly enterprise-tier and generally completely inaccessible to small startups or solo founders
  • Requires significant organizational buy-in; reps must consent to recording and embrace the coaching culture
  • The platform is heavy and complex, occasionally requiring a dedicated RevOps administrator to yield maximum value
  • In crowded rooms or poor audio conditions, speaker separation can occasionally blur

Pricing

Chorus.ai employs custom, enterprise-level pricing models. Pricing is typically determined by the number of recorded "seats" (sales representatives) and is often bundled or heavily integrated with larger ZoomInfo enterprise contracts. There is no public self-serve or free tier.

How It Compares

Chorus.ai competes directly in a tight duopoly with Gong. Gong is widely considered the established market leader in the conversation intelligence category, known for its predictive revenue insights. Chorus provides a nearly identical core feature set, but proactively differentiates itself through its acquisition by ZoomInfo. If a company already uses ZoomInfo for B2B contact data and prospecting, Chorus becomes the logical, highly synergistic choice, unifying contact data and conversation intelligence securely under one vendor roof.
