Brisk Teaching

Brisk Teaching is an AI-powered, ubiquitous Chrome extension designed explicitly to alleviate the administrative and operational overwhelming burden placed on K-12 educators. Unlike specialized tutoring apps that target students, Brisk functions entirely to serve the teacher, operating directly within the tools they already use—specifically Google Docs, Google Classroom, and standard web browsers.

Its marquee feature is seamlessly reading the context of educational material across the web to generate customized resources instantly. A teacher can open a complex news article, click the Brisk extension, and instantly rewrite that same article at three different, lower reading levels to accommodate varying student proficiencies within the same classroom.

It is heavily adopted by school districts and individual educators seeking to claw back hours spent on manual grading, rubric creation, and differentiated lesson planning, offering the speed of LLMs within an highly frictionless browser interface.

Best For

  • K-12 classroom teachers and educators
  • Special education professionals managing IEPs
  • School administrators and curriculum designers
  • Any educator heavily utilizing the Google Workspace ecosystem

How It Works

A teacher installs the Chrome extension. If they are viewing an educational YouTube video, they can click the Brisk icon and instruct it to automatically generate a 10-question multiple-choice quiz based on the video's transcript, exporting it directly to a Google Form. Inside a Google Doc containing a student's essay, the teacher clicks Brisk to "Give Feedback." The AI analyzes the text against an uploaded specific rubric and instantly inserts targeted, constructive comments inline, mimicking a human grading pass for the teacher to review and approve.

Key Features

Resource Generation

  • Multi-level reading differentiation
  • Instant Quiz to Google Forms generation
  • Lesson plan and syllabus creation
  • Presentation (Slide) generation from text

Grading & Feedback

  • Inline Google Docs feedback integration
  • Custom rubric application
  • AI plagiarism detection
  • Administrative document drafting (IEPs, emails)

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The Chrome extension integration into Google Docs is accurate and eliminates the friction of copy-pasting to external AI dashboards
  • Differentiating resource reading levels is arguably the most powerful equity tool available to modern teachers
  • Generating quizzes directly into Google Forms saves immense formatting time
  • Maintains a strong focus on data privacy complying with educational standards (FERPA)

Limitations

  • Highly reliant on the Google Workspace ecosystem; less effective for schools mandated to use Microsoft 365
  • AI grading feedback, while helpful, still requires significant human review to ensure empathy and accuracy
  • Some schools proactively block Chrome extensions, complicating widespread IT deployment
  • Not a student-facing tool, lacking the Socratic tutoring capabilities of Khanmigo

Pricing

Brisk Teaching offers a robust Free tier for individual educators providing core features like level adjustment and quiz generation. A Premium educator tier unlocks advanced AI models and unlimited feedback generation. Enterprise/School plans are available for district-wide deployment, offering administrative controls and deeper LMS integrations.

How It Compares

Brisk Teaching competes with tools like MagicSchool AI and Eduaide. MagicSchool is highly robust but operates primarily as a standalone "dashboard" where teachers must log in, generate text, and copy-paste it out. Brisk differentiates itself powerfully through its Chrome extension architecture. By operating directly inside Google Docs and on top of active live web pages, it provides a significantly more integrated, frictionless user experience for teachers who already live inside web browsers.
