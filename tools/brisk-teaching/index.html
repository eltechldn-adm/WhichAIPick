Brisk Teaching

Brisk helps teachers create learning materials and draft feedback within their browser workflow. School and district plans add institution-specific controls and curriculum features.

Visit Website Compare Brisk Teaching

Editorial Overview

Brisk Teaching is an AI-powered Chrome extension designed to reduce the administrative and operational workload placed on K-12 educators. Unlike standalone tutoring applications that target students, Brisk is built for teachers and operates directly within the tools they already use—specifically Google Docs, Google Slides, Google Forms, Google Classroom, and standard web browsers. Its core capability is reading the context of educational material across the web to generate classroom-ready resources. A teacher can open a complex news article, launch the Brisk extension, and rewrite that article at multiple reading levels to support differentiated instruction within the same classroom. It is adopted by school districts and individual educators to streamline routine grading feedback, rubric creation, and differentiated lesson planning, delivering LLM-assisted workflows through a low-friction browser interface.

How it works

A teacher installs the Chrome extension. If they are viewing an educational YouTube video, they can click the Brisk icon and instruct it to automatically generate a 10-question multiple-choice quiz based on the video's transcript, exporting it directly to a Google Form. Inside a Google Doc containing a student's essay, the teacher clicks Brisk to "Give Feedback." The AI analyzes the text against an uploaded rubric and inserts targeted, constructive comments inline, providing an initial grading draft for the teacher to review and approve.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The Chrome extension integration into Google Docs is accurate and eliminates the friction of copy-pasting to external AI dashboards
  • Differentiating resource reading levels is arguably the most powerful equity tool available to modern teachers
  • Generating quizzes directly into Google Forms saves immense formatting time
  • Publishes student data privacy commitments and states that it complies with student privacy laws such as FERPA

Limitations

  • Highly reliant on the Google Workspace ecosystem; less effective for schools mandated to use Microsoft 365
  • AI grading feedback, while helpful, still requires significant human review to ensure empathy and accuracy
  • Some schools proactively block Chrome extensions, complicating widespread IT deployment
  • Not a student-facing tool, lacking the Socratic tutoring capabilities of Khanmigo

How It Compares

Brisk Teaching competes with tools like MagicSchool AI and Eduaide. MagicSchool is highly robust but operates primarily as a standalone "dashboard" where teachers must log in, generate text, and copy-paste it out. Brisk differentiates itself powerfully through its Chrome extension architecture. By operating directly inside Google Docs and on top of active live web pages, it provides a significantly more integrated, frictionless user experience for teachers who already live inside web browsers.
