Glean

Glean is an enterprise artificial intelligence search and workplace knowledge discovery platform designed for mid-market and large enterprise organizations. Modern enterprises frequently suffer from fragmented knowledge spread across disconnected cloud software applications, including document repositories, chat channels, issue trackers, customer relationship managers, and code hosts. Glean indexes company information across these separate repositories into a unified knowledge graph, enabling employees to search and synthesize corporate intelligence from a centralized portal.

The platform distinguishes itself through its permission-aware architecture. Enterprise search engines must respect complex data governance and identity rules to ensure employees never view documents, customer details, or compensation data they are not authorized to see. Glean integrates with corporate identity providers like Okta and Active Directory, continuously synchronizing access control lists (ACLs) so that search results and generative AI answers are designed to reflect the documented permissions of the querying user in connected identity systems.

In addition to enterprise search, Glean provides generative chat assistants and an application studio for building custom internal agents. Employees can query corporate policies, draft technical briefs with source citations, or deploy specialized bots that automate routine workflow inquiries across connected systems. By combining deep connector integration with strict enterprise security compliance, Glean serves as a centralized intelligence layer for modern corporate workforces.

Best For

  • Mid-market and enterprise organizations with hundreds to thousands of employees managing fragmented SaaS repositories.
  • IT and security leaders seeking permission-aware access control synchronization for enterprise AI search.
  • Knowledge management teams seeking to reduce employee time spent searching across disparate corporate apps.

Not Ideal For

  • Individual users, freelancers, or small startups with simple document structures that do not require enterprise indexing.
  • Organizations seeking a self-serve credit-card tool without involving enterprise IT security procurement.
  • Teams looking for public web search or general consumer information retrieval.

How It Works

An enterprise IT team deploys Glean by configuring connections to company cloud applications, including Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Jira, Confluence, Salesforce, and GitHub. Glean crawls and indexes structured and unstructured data, establishing semantic relationships between employees, projects, documents, and communication threads to build an enterprise work graph.

Glean continuously syncs access control lists and user permissions from connected applications and identity providers. When an employee submits a natural language search query or asks a question in Glean Chat, the system applies semantic retrieval algorithms across indexed company content while applying permission filters designed to exclude documents that the user is not authorized to view based on synced access control lists.

Search results and synthesized generative answers are returned with clickable citations linking to the authoritative source documents across connected tools. Administrators can monitor organization search analytics, configure custom company-specific dictionary acronyms, and deploy specialized internal agents via the Glean Apps studio to automate departmental support workflows.

Key Features

Enterprise Search & Work Graph

  • Unified search indexing across Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Jira, Salesforce, and GitHub.
  • Semantic work graph mapping relationships between colleagues, teams, documents, and project contexts.
  • Generative assistant delivering cited natural language answers grounded in verified company documents.

Permissions & Security Governance

  • Continuous synchronization of access control lists (ACLs) designed to enforce enterprise permissions from connected data sources.
  • Enterprise identity provider integration supporting SAML, OIDC, Okta, and Microsoft Entra ID.
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance, encryption in transit and at rest, and zero customer data training policies.

Enterprise Agent Studio & Insights

  • Glean Apps studio enabling teams to build and deploy custom internal AI agents without code.
  • Company acronym directory and people search providing verified organizational context.
  • Administrative analytics dashboards tracking knowledge retrieval efficiency and popular search queries.

Practical Workflows

  1. Unified enterprise document discovery: Search across company Google Drive, Confluence, Slack, and Jira instances simultaneously to locate technical specifications and project decisions.
  2. Permission-aware policy querying: Ask natural language questions about internal HR and IT policies, receiving direct answers with citations to documents matching the user access permissions.
  3. Custom department agent deployment: Build specialized internal agents in the Glean Apps studio to automate onboarding FAQs and route common employee support requests.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Comprehensive enterprise search across more than one hundred popular workplace SaaS tools and data sources.
  • Permission-aware indexing architecture designed to limit search results and AI answers to content the querying user is authorized to access.
  • Provides direct source citations for all synthesized answers, facilitating fact-checking and document verification.
  • Enterprise-grade security controls with SOC 2 compliance, SAML SSO, and zero model training on customer data.

Limitations

  • Operates under an enterprise sales-led model with no self-serve checkout, public pricing card, or free plan.
  • Requires extensive organizational IT setup, connector authorization, and administrative oversight to deploy.
  • Search quality depends heavily on existing company documentation hygiene and active internal repository usage.
  • Inaccessible to individual professionals or early-stage startups due to enterprise contract requirements.

Pricing

Glean operates strictly under a sales-led enterprise commercial model tailored to organizational scale, employee headcount, and connector ecosystem requirements. The company does not publish a self-serve rate card, public tier breakdown, or credit-card checkout on its website. Interested organizations must request a formal consultation and product demonstration with the Glean sales team to receive a custom proposal. Agreements are structured as annual enterprise contracts covering seat licensing, platform maintenance, connector syncing, and enterprise support service levels. There is no permanent free tier, public developer sandbox, or self-serve trial available.

How It Compares

Compared to consumer-oriented AI search tools like Perplexity or open-web knowledge engines, Glean is designed strictly for private internal enterprise intranets. Against native search within individual platforms like Google Workspace or Microsoft Copilot, Glean offers neutral cross-platform indexing that connects heterogeneous tool stacks across Google, Microsoft, Slack, Atlassian, and Salesforce under a unified permission-aware architecture.
