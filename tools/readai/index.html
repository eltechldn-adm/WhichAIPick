Read.ai

Read AI produces meeting transcripts and summaries and makes meeting information searchable. Paid plans increase meeting allowances and add collaboration or playback features.

Visit Website Compare Read.ai

Editorial Overview

Read AI (Read.ai) is a meeting intelligence and transcription platform that integrates with video conferencing services—including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet—to summarize discussions and analyze communication dynamics across distributed teams. In addition to generating automated transcripts, summaries, and action items, Read AI evaluates audio pacing, speaker talk-time distribution, and optional video engagement signals to report on overall meeting effectiveness. Its primary differentiator is combining standard meeting documentation with structured participation metrics and speaker coaching. Post-meeting reports break down talk-time balance, pacing, and topic progression so organizers and participants can review how meeting time was allocated and identify opportunities for clearer communication. It is used by remote and hybrid teams, project managers, and operations leaders who want searchable meeting records alongside consistent analytics on meeting length, participation balance, and follow-up commitments.

How it works

A project manager connects their Google or Microsoft calendar to Read AI and hosts a 45-minute sprint planning meeting on Zoom with ten team members. The Read AI assistant joins the call as a scheduled participant to record and transcribe the session. Shortly after the call concludes, the organizer receives a post-meeting report containing an executive summary, key topics, and assigned action items, alongside participation metrics such as talk-time distribution, speaking pace, and timeline highlights showing when engagement shifted during the presentation.

Key Features

Multimodal AI Analysis

  • Text summarization (Action Items, Key Questions, Highlights)
  • Optional video engagement and sentiment indicators
  • Audio analytics (Speaker talk-time ratio, interruption frequency)
  • Automated highlight clip generation (video reels of important moments)

Ecosystem & Scheduling

  • Passive calendar integration (auto-joins Zoom, Teams, Meet)
  • Centralized organization dashboard (Workspace analytics)
  • Real-time in-meeting metrics (displaying engagement as the meeting happens)
  • CRM Integration (HubSpot, Salesforce)

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Combines automated meeting transcription and action-item extraction with structured participation and talk-time analytics
  • Reduces manual note-taking during calls so participants can focus on the discussion while preserving a searchable record
  • Speaker coaching metrics (pacing, talk-time balance, and filler-word tracking) provide actionable feedback for presenters and meeting hosts
  • Automated video highlight clips allow absent team members to review key segments without watching the full recording

Limitations

  • Automated meeting assistants and visual engagement tracking can raise privacy or comfort concerns for participants if recording policies are not communicated clearly
  • Visual sentiment and engagement indicators are heuristic estimates and may misinterpret camera angles, lighting, or quiet concentration
  • Highly technical architecture discussions or whiteboard-heavy sessions still require manual review to verify specialized terminology in the summary
  • Organizations with strict compliance requirements may need Enterprise administrative guardrails and explicit opt-in policies before enabling workspace-wide recording

Who is it for?

Pricing & Access

  • Pricing Model: freemium
  • Starting Price: $19.75
  • Free Tier: Free tier available
  • Free Trial: Free trial available

Read AI operates on a freemium subscription model. The Free plan includes up to 5 meeting transcripts and reports per month (for meetings up to 1 hour) alongside basic search and summary features. Paid plans begin with the Pro tier at $19.75 per user per month billed monthly ($15 per user per month when billed annually), which unlocks unlimited meeting transcripts and workspace integrations, while the Enterprise tier ($29.75 per user per month billed monthly, or $22.50 per user per month billed annually) adds video playback and highlight reels, custom retention policies, and administrative guardrails.

How It Compares

Read AI competes in the meeting intelligence category alongside Otter.ai, Fathom, Fireflies.ai, and Gong. While Otter.ai focuses primarily on real-time text transcription and Gong specializes in enterprise revenue intelligence and CRM pipeline forecasting, Read AI differentiates itself by pairing cross-platform meeting summaries and search with participation analytics, talk-time balance reporting, and individual speaker coaching across video calls, emails, and team messaging.
