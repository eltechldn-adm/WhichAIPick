Apollo

Apollo (Apollo.io) is an integrated B2B lead generation database and sales engagement platform designed to help sales development representatives (SDRs), account executives, and founders discover verified business prospects and automate outbound communication. The platform pairs a proprietary database of over 275 million business contacts and 73 million company profiles with multichannel sales outreach tools, cold email cadences, and CRM synchronization.

Rather than managing separate subscriptions for B2B data vendors, email sequencers, and phone dialers, Apollo provides an end-to-end sales engine. Users can build targeted buyer lists using precise technographic and firmographic filters, verify business email addresses and mobile phone numbers, enroll prospects into multi-stage email cadences, and track pipeline metrics within a single dashboard.

Best For

  • B2B sales development representatives and account executives prospecting target accounts and managing cold outbound cadences
  • Founders and small sales teams wanting contact intelligence and multi-step email sequencing inside one affordable platform
  • RevOps and growth marketing professionals enriching inbound CRM leads with verified firmographic and technographic data

Not Ideal For

  • B2C consumer outreach or direct-to-consumer marketing, as the database is strictly focused on business professionals
  • Teams requiring dedicated enterprise telecommunications systems with physical hardware PBX and international call routing
  • Organizations unwilling to verify contact bounce rates, as unverified database entries can degrade cold email sender reputation

How It Works

Users begin by using Apollo's search interface or Chrome extension to query its B2B database. Filters allow targeting by industry, location, funding stage, hiring trends, and technologies installed on the target company's website. Prospects matching the ideal customer profile (ICP) are saved to lists, where Apollo verifies contact email deliverability and mobile phone numbers using plan credits.

Once prospect lists are established, users enroll contacts into automated Sequences. These cadences schedule multi-day outreach flows consisting of AI-personalized email drafts, automated follow-ups, manual phone call reminders through Apollo's built-in dialer, and LinkedIn connection prompts. All prospect interactions, email opens, link clicks, and booked meetings sync bidirectionally with integrated CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive.

Key Features

B2B Database & Contact Intelligence

  • Searchable database covering 275M+ verified business professionals and 73M+ companies worldwide
  • Granular filtering across 65+ attributes including job title, company revenue, funding status, and installed technologies
  • Chrome extension enabling contact lookup and enrichment directly on LinkedIn, company websites, and Gmail
  • Automated data health enrichment keeping existing CRM contact and account records accurate over time

Multichannel Outreach & Sales Engagement

  • Automated email sequences with customizable scheduling, A/B testing, and AI-assisted personalized message drafting
  • Built-in cloud phone dialer supporting international calling, local presence, and automated call recording
  • Buyer intent data and website visitor tracking identifying in-market accounts researching relevant categories
  • Bidirectional real-time CRM synchronization with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Combines high-volume B2B contact data and sales outreach sequencing into a unified, cost-effective platform
  • Generous free plan allows early-stage founders to test data quality and run basic prospecting without upfront commitments
  • Browser extension provides seamless contact capture directly from LinkedIn profiles and corporate websites
  • Comprehensive filtering capabilities include niche technographics and company growth metrics

Limitations

  • Contact data accuracy varies across international regions and smaller private companies, requiring periodic email hygiene checks
  • Mobile phone number credits and high-tier intent data consume credit allocations rapidly on standard plans
  • Advanced features like custom intent signals, comprehensive reporting, and dialer minutes require higher-tier subscriptions
  • Platform interface can feel overwhelming due to the sheer density of navigation menus and configuration settings

Pricing

Apollo uses a per-seat monthly or annual subscription model supplemented by credit allotments for contact email verification and direct mobile phone numbers.

The Free Plan ($0 per month) includes 900 email credits per year (granted in monthly batches), basic search filters, and limited email sequencing. Paid plans include: Basic at $59 per seat per month (or $49 per seat per month billed annually at $588 per year), providing 30,000 email credits per year, unlimited email sequencing, and standard CRM integrations; Professional at $99 per seat per month (or $79 per seat per month billed annually at $948 per year), which adds 48,000 email credits per year, a built-in dialer, call recording, and A/B testing; and Organization at $149 per seat per month (or $119 per seat per month billed annually at $1,428 per year, with a 3-seat minimum), including 72,000 email credits per year, advanced intent data, customizable permissions, and SSO. A 14-day free trial is available for paid tiers.

How It Compares

Compared to legacy enterprise databases like ZoomInfo, Apollo provides a significantly more accessible self-serve pricing model with built-in email sequencing, making it ideal for startups and mid-market teams. Compared to dedicated email sequencers like Zapier AI (/tools/zapier-ai/) or standalone outreach tools, Apollo eliminates the need to import external CSV lead lists by providing native contact records directly inside the sequencing interface.
