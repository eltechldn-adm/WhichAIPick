Durable

Durable is an AI website builder and comprehensive "business in a box" platform designed specifically for solo entrepreneurs and service-based small businesses (like landscapers, consultants, or independent contractors). Its primary claim to fame is the ability to generate a fully functioning, professional website with images, copy, and a contact form in under 30 seconds.

Unlike complex visual builders that require design skills, Durable restricts users to highly curated, block-based designs. It prioritizes extreme speed to market over granular pixel-pushing. Furthermore, it integrates a lightweight CRM, invoicing tools, and an AI assistant into the dashboard, attempting to replace the need for multiple software subscriptions for a new business.

It targets established beginners who find platforms like WordPress or even Squarespace too complicated or time-consuming, focusing solely on getting a business online and capturing leads instantly.

Best For

  • Solo entrepreneurs launching their first business
  • Local service providers (trades, consultants, cleaners)
  • Non-technical users intimidated by traditional website builders
  • Users needing an all-in-one suite (Site, CRM, Invoicing)

How It Works

A user visits the Durable homepage, enters their business type (e.g., "Roofing"), and their location. Within seconds, the AI generates a multi-section website featuring localized SEO copy, relevant stock imagery, services lists, testionials, and a functional lead capture form. The user enters a lightweight editor to regenerate specific sections (by clicking "regenerate"), edit text, or swap colors based on simple palettes. Once published, any leads captured by the site are funneled into the built-in CRM dashboard.

Key Features

Website Generation

  • Under 30-second AI site generation
  • Location-based SEO copy
  • Automated stock media integration
  • Regenerate-by-section editing

Business Operations

  • Integrated CRM for lead capture
  • Invoicing and payment tools
  • AI marketing copy generation
  • Custom domain management

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Arguably the fastest way to get a functional, good-looking business site online
  • The all-in-one approach (CRM + Invoicing) saves a solo founder hundreds of dollars in disparate software
  • Extremely low learning curve; impossible to "break" the design
  • Copy generation is well-tuned for localized service businesses

Limitations

  • Highly restrictive design capabilities; you cannot drag-and-drop elements freely
  • Not suitable for complex e-commerce, large SaaS sites, or custom web apps
  • Vendor lock-in: sites cannot be exported to other hosts
  • SEO capabilities are basic, lacking the depth needed for highly competitive national campaigns

Pricing

Durable operates on a straightforward SaaS subscription model. Plans (Starter, Business) are billed monthly or annually and include website hosting, custom domain connection, the AI assistant, and the CRM/invoicing tools. It positions itself as a single flat fee replacing multiple subscriptions.

How It Compares

Durable competes with Squarespace, Wix, and 10Web. 10Web builds scalable WordPress sites that require some tech literacy. Squarespace and Wix offer brilliant templates but require hours of manual drag-and-drop design and copywriting to launch. Durable differentiates itself by trading granular design control for established maximum speed to launch, and by bundling operational software (CRM, invoicing) specifically tailored for solo service businesses into a single dashboard.
