Lindy

Lindy is an AI assistant platform designed to handle practical work across connected business applications. Rather than simply answering questions, it can help users organise information, prepare drafts, manage recurring tasks and coordinate work between services.

Its capabilities include inbox support, meeting assistance, scheduled routines, reusable skills, workspace memory and connected workflows. Lindy can be used as a personal productivity assistant or as a shared assistant within a team.

The platform is particularly relevant when tasks require context from several applications. However, its credit usage and per-active-user billing make it important to plan team access and automation frequency before deployment.

Best For

  • Professionals managing a high volume of recurring administrative tasks.
  • Operations teams coordinating information across multiple SaaS applications.
  • Organisations seeking assistants for meeting follow-ups, inbox triage and team workflows.

Not Ideal For

  • Users who require a permanent free subscription after the introductory trial.
  • Teams that cannot monitor credit usage or control the number of paid seats.
  • Organisations unwilling to grant and govern access to connected business services.

How It Works

Users connect supported applications and describe a task or workflow. Lindy can use available information to prepare an answer, draft content, organise records or initiate an automation using its available tools.

Teams can create reusable Skills and Routines, use shared or personal memory, and interact with Lindy through supported environments such as Slack, email and meeting workflows. Higher subscription tiers support more advanced capabilities such as computer use.

Lindy provides approval controls for actions that affect external systems. Users should still review connected permissions and test workflows carefully before granting an assistant access to business information or communications.

Key Features

AI assistants and reusable work

  • Skills, scheduled Routines and workspace Memory
  • Natural-language task instructions

Communications and collaboration

  • Inbox triage, meeting assistance and Slack interaction
  • Scheduling and connected application workflows

Advanced automation and controls

  • Computer Use on eligible plans and integrations
  • Shared credit management and human approval mechanisms

Practical Workflows

  1. Meeting follow-up: Prepare meeting notes, summarise agreed actions and draft follow-up messages for approval.
  2. Inbox triage: Identify messages requiring attention, organise them by priority and prepare suggested replies without sending them automatically.
  3. Team operations: Monitor an approved workflow, collect information from connected tools and create a structured update for the responsible team.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Combines conversational assistance with practical workflow automation.
  • Works with multiple supported business applications.
  • Provides reusable Skills, Routines and shared context.
  • Includes credit controls and approval mechanisms for external actions.

Limitations

  • Charges depend on active team seats and consumed credits.
  • Monthly subscription credits do not carry over indefinitely.
  • Advanced computer-use functionality requires an eligible higher plan.
  • Connecting business tools introduces permission-management and data-governance responsibilities.

Pricing

Lindy offers a 7-day free trial with $50 in introductory credits, without requiring a credit card. It does not provide an ongoing unrestricted free subscription.

Lindy is currently presenting different Team entry offers. Its official pricing page has shown $29.99 per active user per month with 3,000 monthly credits, and $49.99 per active user per month with 5,000 monthly credits. Which offer is available can depend on the pricing version presented to the visitor. Higher published selections include $99.99 per active user per month for 15,000 credits and $199.99 for 35,000 credits. These charges apply per active user rather than to an entire workspace at a flat price. Check the offer shown during checkout before subscribing.

Credits from active seats are pooled within the workspace. Monthly subscription credits do not roll over, while separately purchased top-up credits remain available until used. Top-ups are advertised at $10 per 1,000 credits.

A key billing consideration is that colleagues who actively use Lindy through shared Slack interactions can become billable seats after their introductory trial. Administrators should manage memberships and credit limits carefully.

Lindy states that credit-consuming work pauses when the available pool is depleted, helping avoid uncontrolled usage charges. Phone-related features may have additional credit and number costs.

How It Compares

Choose Lindy when the goal is to use an AI assistant across email, meetings, Slack and connected business applications.

Consider Zapier or Make when predictable trigger-and-action workflows are more important than conversational assistance. Consider Relay.app or Gumloop when you prefer a structured automation builder for specific processes.

Lindy makes the most sense where the value of contextual assistance and saved administrative work justifies the ongoing seat and credit costs.
