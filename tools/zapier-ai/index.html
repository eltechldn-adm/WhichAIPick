Zapier AI

Zapier connects applications using automated workflows. Copilot assists workflow creation; separate Agents and Chatbots products have their own entitlements.

Visit Website Compare Zapier AI

Editorial Overview

Zapier AI integrates artificial intelligence capabilities across Zapier's workflow automation platform. By combining large language model capabilities with an ecosystem of over 6,000 supported applications, Zapier AI allows users to build, format, and execute cross-software workflows using natural-language instructions. Key AI features include the AI Copilot workflow builder, AI Actions, and integrated Formatter steps. The AI Copilot enables users to describe an automation goal in plain language, generating a recommended multi-step Zap with pre-selected applications and field mappings. AI Actions allow external language models, including custom GPTs, to trigger actions in connected workplace software. In addition, built-in AI steps allow users to summarize text, extract structured data, or format unstructured strings within active workflows. Zapier AI is used by operations managers, marketing teams, and businesses to connect disparate SaaS tools without custom code. Successful execution depends on connected application accounts, available API triggers and actions, and proper workflow testing.

How it works

A user enters a natural-language description into the Zapier AI builder (e.g., 'When a new lead arrives in Facebook Ads, use AI to summarize their company notes, create a row in Google Sheets, and notify the sales channel in Slack'). The AI Copilot analyzes the prompt and scaffolds a draft Zap with the relevant triggers and actions. The user authenticates the connected accounts, verifies data field mappings, and tests the step outputs. Once configured, the workflow executes automatically whenever the trigger event occurs.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Connects to an extensive ecosystem of over 6,000 applications, enabling broad cross-platform automation
  • AI Copilot assists in scaffolding multi-step workflows from natural-language descriptions, reducing initial setup time
  • AI Actions enable external language models and custom GPTs to trigger workplace actions securely
  • Built-in AI formatting and data-transformation steps streamline processing unformatted text between incompatible services

Limitations

  • Task-based pricing can scale rapidly for high-frequency automations with multiple workflow steps
  • Natural-language prompts generate draft workflows that still require manual verification of field mappings and authentication credentials
  • Third-party API changes or expired authentication tokens can interrupt automated workflows and require maintenance
  • Enterprise deployment requires careful governance and permission management across connected workplace tools

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Teams automating data transfers and notifications across multiple supported business applications
  • Operations professionals seeking natural-language workflow drafting without custom code
  • Organizations connecting external AI assistants to workplace SaaS tools via AI Actions

Not Ideal For

  • Users on free plans requiring complex multi-step automations or premium app integrations
  • Organizations requiring a verified self-hosted, on-premise automation engine
  • High-throughput enterprise data pipelines where per-task billing is cost-prohibitive

Pricing & Access

  • Pricing Model: freemium
  • Starting Price: $29.99
  • Free Tier: Free tier available
  • Free Trial: Free trial available

Zapier operates a consumption-based freemium model centered on monthly task volumes and automation complexity. A free tier provides foundational single-step automations with limited monthly tasks. Paid plans begin with the Professional tier at $29.99 per month (or $19.99 per month billed annually) for 750 tasks, unlocking multi-step workflows, AI logic tools, and premium app integrations. A 14-day free trial is available for evaluating paid features.

How It Compares

Zapier AI competes with automation platforms including Make and enterprise integration suites like Tray.io and Workato. While Make offers a visual canvas with advanced data mapping at lower task costs, Zapier maintains a significantly larger integration catalog and accessible natural-language workflow generation. Compared to enterprise iPaaS tools that require specialized integration engineering, Zapier AI enables non-technical teams to deploy and manage automated workflows rapidly.
