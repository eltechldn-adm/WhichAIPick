Looka

Looka (formerly Logojoy) is an AI-powered automated branding and graphic design platform designed to serve the fundamental needs of early-stage entrepreneurs, solo-preneurs, and small business operators. Rather than requiring users to learn complex vector editing software (like Adobe Illustrator) or hire expensive creative agencies, Looka uses machine learning to instantly generate comprehensive brand identities.

Its primary differentiator is its holistic "Brand Kit" output. It does not just output a flat PNG of a logo. Looka attempts to replace an entire entry-level design agency. After generating a logo, it autonomously uses the brand's new color palette, typography combination, and icon to instantly generate over 300 branded assets—including business cards, social media banners, email signatures, and invoices—creating instant, cohesive brand alignment for a new company in minutes.

It is heavily utilized by non-technical founders, local service businesses (e.g., plumbers, cafes, real estate agents), and side-hustlers who need to make their brand-new, zero-budget project look highly professional, legitimate, and established on day one.

How It Works

A user launching a new landscaping business types their company name ("GreenRoots") into Looka. They select their industry and click 5 logos they aesthetically like from a grid to train the AI. They select a few colors and symbols (e.g., "tree," "leaf"). The AI instantly generates dozens of custom logo variations pairing elegant typography with vector icons. The user selects their favorite and enters the editor to slightly tweak the font size. Upon purchasing, they unlock the "Brand Kit." Looka instantly populates hundreds of templates: their new logo and green color palette are automatically applied to printable business card templates, a Facebook cover photo, and a branded letterhead, accurate$2 formatted and ready to download.

Key Features

Logo Generation Engine

  • AI-driven significant logo ideation
  • Customizable vector editor (layout, font, spacing tweaking)
  • High-resolution export (PNG, SVG, EPS, PDF)
  • Black & White and transparent variations

Brand Kit Automations

  • Automated business card and stationary design
  • Social media asset generation (Profile/Cover photos)
  • Branded presentation and invoice templates
  • Detailed Brand Guidelines document synthesis

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • The "Brand Kit" is a large-scale value multiplier; generating the logo is easy, but having the platform instantly apply that logo accurate$2 to a business card and Twitter header saves incredible amounts of time
  • Downloading raw SVG/EPS vector files ensures the logo can be given to professional printers or web developers without pixelation
  • It is exceptionally user-friendly for established beginners who have never opened Photoshop
  • Provides a level of visual cohesion (matching fonts/colors everywhere) that amateur founders rarely achieve on their own

Cons

  • The logos rely heavily on a large-scale underlying database of standard fonts and stock vector icons; it cannot create a completely hand-drawn, highly unique, bespoke artistic logo (like a human illustrator could)
  • Highly experienced designers will spot an AI-generated Looka logo immediately due to structural layout similarities
  • The editor, while easy to use, lacks the established pixel-accurate$2 manipulation capabilities of true vector software like Illustrator
  • There is no free download option; while generating designs is free, obtaining the actual files requires purchase

Pricing

Looka allows users to generate and edit logos infinitely for free. Pricing triggers upon download. They offer a one-time purchase option (Basic or Premium Logo Package) for downloading just the logo files. However, they proactively push the Brand Kit Subscription, an annual SaaS fee that provides the logo files plus continuous, unlimited access to the hundreds of automatically generated marketing and social media templates.

How It Compares

Looka competes against Tailor Brands, Canva, and professional marketplaces like Fiverr. Canva is incredible for ongoing graphic design but lacks a dedicated, intelligent brand-generation workflow. Fiverr provides cheap human designers, but quality is a large-scale gamble, and revisions take days. Tailor Brands is highly similar but pushes heavily into LLC formation. Looka differentiates itself by heavily emphasizing the aesthetic quality and breadth of its *Brand Kit*. It is the best choice for a founder who wants to instantly look like a million-dollar company on day one, acquiring all the assets they need without gambling on a cheap freelancer.
