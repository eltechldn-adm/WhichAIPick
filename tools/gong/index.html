Gong

Gong is an enterprise Revenue Intelligence platform that utilizes artificial intelligence to analyze every customer-facing interaction across a company—including video calls, phone calls, and emails. Rather than relying on sales representatives to manually enter notes into a CRM, Gong automatically captures the reality of what is happening in the sales pipeline.

By analyzing the transcripts and metadata of thousands of sales calls, Gong's AI identifies patterns that lead to closed deals. It tracks competitor mentions, objection handling techniques, and even the ratio of how much the representative spoke versus the customer. It provides actionable coaching insights to sales managers.

It acts as the single source of truth for enterprise revenue teams (Sales, Customer Success, and Marketing), ensuring leadership has complete visibility into market sentiment and representative performance without subjective filtering.

Best For

  • Enterprise B2B sales organizations
  • Revenue Operations (RevOps) leaders
  • Sales enablement and coaching teams
  • Product marketing managers researching customer voice

How It Works

Gong connects to a company's email servers, telephony systems (like RingCentral), and conferencing software (Zoom, Teams). It records and transcribes all external interactions automatically. The AI analyzes the transcripts, tagging key moments (like pricing discussions or competitor mentions). Within the dashboard, managers review "Deal Boards" that show the health of a pipeline based on actual communication velocity. Representatives use the platform to search past calls or review AI-generated coaching scorecards.

Key Features

Call Analytics

  • Automatic recording and deep transcription
  • Talk-to-listen ratio tracking
  • Competitor and feature mention tracking
  • Automated CRM data entry

Revenue Intelligence

  • AI-driven deal forecasting
  • Sales coaching scorecards
  • Email sentiment analysis
  • Market intelligence dashboards

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Eliminates the "blind spot" between what reps say happened and what actually happened
  • Drastically reduces manual CRM data entry for sales reps
  • Invaluable for onboarding new hires quickly with real examples
  • Forecasting becomes predictive based on actual deal velocity rather than optimism

Cons

  • Enterprise pricing can be prohibitive for small businesses or solo founders
  • Requires significant organizational change management to overcome the "Big Brother" feeling among reps
  • Integration setup across enterprise telephony and email servers is a complex IT project
  • Only valuable if the team actively uses the coaching and analytics features

Pricing

Gong does not publish generic public pricing. It operates strictly on custom enterprise contracts billed annually, factoring in the number of recorded users, the suite of features required, and the complexity of the enterprise systems being integrated.

How It Compares

Gong's primary competitor is Chorus.ai (acquired by ZoomInfo). Both offer stellar conversation intelligence. Gong generally retains mindshare as the foundational pioneer of the category, often boasting slightly more robust predictive forecasting and a broader third-party integration ecosystem. Smaller standalone tools like Fathom or Otter.ai offer recording and transcription, but they completely lack the enterprise, pipeline-wide revenue forecasting and advanced coaching analytics that justify Gong's premium position.
