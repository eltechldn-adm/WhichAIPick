Novelcrafter

Novelcrafter is a comprehensive story planning, worldbuilding, and drafting platform designed specifically for fiction writers, novelists, and narrative designers. Unlike generic word processors or general-purpose AI writing tools, Novelcrafter integrates an extensive story bible—called the Codex—that dynamically tracks characters, locations, factions, and lore across complex multi-volume series.

The platform employs an open Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture, connecting authors directly to commercial and open-weight AI models (via OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, or local runtimes like Ollama). By injecting relevant story lore into AI prompts based on scene context, Novelcrafter assists authors in outlining, drafting from scene beats, and revising prose without vendor lock-in or inflated token markups.

Best For

  • Fiction writers and indie novelists creating structured multi-chapter books and complex fantasy or sci-fi series
  • Authors wanting deep story bible lore integration that automatically informs AI scene drafting and revisions
  • Writers who prefer a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model to pay exact at-cost API prices for their choice of LLM

Not Ideal For

  • Writers expecting AI text generation on the entry $4/mo Scribe plan, which provides organization tools only
  • Authors seeking an all-in-one monthly plan that bundles unlimited AI generation without managing API keys
  • Non-fiction writers or business copywriters who do not require character codexes or narrative beat sheets

How It Works

Novelcrafter organizes manuscripts through a hierarchical structure comprising the Planner, the Codex, and the Write interface. Writers establish world details in the Codex, creating entries for characters, settings, magic systems, and objects with customizable tags and relationship associations. In the Planner, writers break down their novel into acts, chapters, and scenes with structured narrative beats.

During drafting in the Write view, Novelcrafter monitors scene text and automatically identifies referenced codex entities. When the author triggers AI generation or uses the Workshop chat, the platform compiles a prompt containing the scene beat outline, character context, stylistic guidelines, and recent prose, sending it to the author's configured AI provider via API. Authors maintain complete narrative oversight, editing generated sentences directly, tracking revisions, and exporting finished manuscripts into clean DOCX or Markdown files.

Key Features

Worldbuilding & Story Architecture

  • The Codex: Comprehensive story bible tracking characters, locations, items, lore, and relationships
  • Automatic entity tagging and dynamic lore injection that supplies relevant story context to AI prompts
  • Visual planning board with outline views, act breakdowns, chapter goals, and scene beat sheets
  • Global series management enabling shared lore and codex entries across multi-book universes

AI Drafting & Model Flexibility

  • Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) integration supporting OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, and local Ollama
  • Prose generation from scene beats with granular control over style, point-of-view, and creative freedom
  • Workshop Chat: Interactive in-app brainstorming partner referencing manuscript lore for plot discussions
  • Full export capabilities supporting standard industry formats including Microsoft Word (DOCX) and Markdown

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Deep story bible integration ensures AI-generated prose remains strictly consistent with established novel lore
  • BYOK architecture eliminates token markups and gives authors complete freedom to choose any AI model
  • Granular beat-driven drafting workflow maintains author control over narrative pacing and plot development
  • Comprehensive manuscript organization tools function effectively with or without AI assistance

Limitations

  • The entry-level Scribe plan ($4/month) includes no AI features or BYOK integrations whatsoever
  • Requires setting up and funding external API accounts (e.g., OpenRouter or Anthropic) for AI generation
  • Steep learning curve compared to standard word processors due to extensive codex tagging and beat structures
  • Interactive Workshop Chat and team collaboration features require higher-tier Artisan and Specialist plans

Pricing

Novelcrafter offers a 21-day unrestricted free trial with full access to all features (no credit card required). Following the trial, subscription plans are offered with annual discounts (equivalent to 2 months free):

Scribe is $4/month ($40/year) and provides unlimited books, series tracking, the Codex story bible, and manuscript exports, but does not include AI features. Hobbyist is $8/month ($80/year) and adds BYOK AI integration, scene beat drafting, character extraction, and scene summarization. Artisan is $14/month ($140/year) and adds the interactive Workshop Chat assistant and advanced review features. Specialist is $20/month ($200/year) and adds team management and collaborative writing. Note that AI token usage is billed separately by your chosen provider (e.g., OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or free local models via Ollama).

How It Compares

Compared to Sudowrite, Novelcrafter uses a transparent Bring-Your-Own-Key model where writers pay raw API prices rather than expensive credit bundles, and Novelcrafter offers a superior structured Codex story bible, though Sudowrite has a simpler learning curve with pre-packaged generation buttons. Compared to Scrivener, Novelcrafter provides modern web-based access and native AI integration while preserving similar deep binder organization, whereas Scrivener is a traditional offline software application with no built-in AI. Compared to Plottr, Novelcrafter covers the entire writing lifecycle from outlining to drafting and revision, while Plottr focuses primarily on visual outlining and plotting timelines.
