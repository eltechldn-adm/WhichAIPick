Frase

Frase (Frase.io) is an AI-powered SEO and content optimization platform designed specifically for content marketers and SEO agencies. Unlike general AI writers that simply generate text from a prompt, Frase is engineered to reverse-engineer Google search results, ensuring the content written is mathematically optimized to rank for specific keywords.

It acts as a bridge between SEO research and content creation. It analyzes the top 20 Google results for any given search query to extract the exact headings, keyword frequencies, and topics covered by competitors. It then provides a real-time scoring editor that grades a writer's draft against those metrics.

With the integration of generative AI, Frase allows users to not only outline and optimize articles but to generate the actual paragraphs directly within the optimization editor, creating a highly streamlined SEO content factory workflow.

Best For

  • SEO agencies scaling content production
  • In-house content marketing teams
  • Niche website builders and affiliate marketers
  • Freelance writers needing to prove SEO optimization to clients

How It Works

A user enters a target keyword (e.g., "Best CRM for real estate"). Frase scrapes the top Google results and generates a Research panel detailing the average word count, headings used, and overlapping topics. The user utilizes AI to auto-generate a comprehensive article outline based on competitor headings. They then write (or use the AI writer to generate) the content in the central editor. A dynamic score from 0-100 updates in real-time as the text incorporates semantic keywords suggested by the platform.

Content Creation

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The real-time SEO scoring is highly accurate and gamifies the writing process
  • Automated brief generation saves hours of manual SERP research
  • Integrates AI generation directly into the optimization workflow seamlessly
  • Excellent for identifying semantic LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords

Limitations

  • The AI writer, while good for SEO, can sometimes feel generic without human editing
  • Pricing can be complex, often requiring a base plan plus an "AI Add-on" for unlimited generation
  • Interface can feel overwhelming for non-SEO professionals
  • Does not provide off-page SEO metrics (like backlink data) found in Ahrefs

Pricing

Frase offers tiered subscription plans based on the number of articles optimized per month. An additional monthly "Pro Add-On" is generally required to unlock unlimited AI writing generation and premium SEO data volume, making it an ongoing investment for professional teams.

How It Compares

Frase competes heavily with Surfer SEO, MarketMuse, and general AI writers like Jasper. Jasper is a better pure creative writer but lacks native deep SERP extraction. MarketMuse is aimed at large-scale enterprise content strategy. Surfer SEO is Frase's most direct rival; Surfer is often considered to have slightly deeper keyword correlation data, but Frase generally offers a smoother, more integrated AI writing and outlining workflow natively within its editor.
