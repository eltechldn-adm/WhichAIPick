Pencil

Pencil (which merged with The Brandtech Group) is a specialized generative AI platform dedicated entirely to generating high-performance video and display advertisements for e-commerce brands and agencies. It attacks the core problem of modern digital advertising on platforms like Meta and TikTok: campaigns require constant "Creative Testing" to find a winning ad, and human design teams cannot physically produce enough video variations fast enough to feed the algorithm.

Its primary differentiator is its focus on *Performance Video Generation*. A brand connects their Shopify store or uploads their raw assets (logos, static product photos, existing routine video clips). Pencil's AI analyzes these raw materials and instantly synthesizes dozens of 15-second, high-energy video ads. It autonomously adds driving music, applies engaging typography, implements rapid transitions, and writes the marketing copy. Crucially, it uses historical ad data to determine *how* the video should be edited to maximize conversions.

It is heavily utilized by Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) e-commerce brands, performance marketing agencies, and social media buyers who need to continuously test 50 new video creatives every week to scale their Facebook and TikTok ad accounts profitably.

How It Works

A skincare brand wants to launch a TikTok campaign but only has static photos of their serum and a plain 30-second video of someone applying it. They upload these assets into Pencil. They select a goal: "Drive Purchases." Pencil's AI chops the 30-second video into 3-second attention-grabbing clips. It animates the static photos. It generates 10 different opening hooks (e.g., "Struggling with dry skin?" vs "My new favorite serum"). It layers trending music and text overlays. Within minutes, the AI generates 15 entirely distinct, ready-to-publish video ads. The performance marketer exports them directly to the Meta Ad Manager to test which video generates the cheapest clicks.

Key Features

Generative Video Assembly

  • Automated video editing (clipping, pacing, transitions)
  • Static-image animation (Ken Burns effects, 3D motion)
  • AI Copywriting for on-screen text overlays and hooks
  • Integrated licensed music library

E-commerce Analytics

  • Predictive ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) scoring
  • Direct integrations with Shopify, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads
  • Computer vision tagging of creative elements
  • Performance data feedback loops

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • It democratizes high-velocity video production; an e-commerce brand can test 50 video ads a week without needing to hire a full-time video editor or motion graphics team
  • The "Static to Video" feature is a large-scale revenue driver for brands that only have traditional, routine product photography but need to advertise on TikTok or Reels
  • The predictive AI scoring prevents marketers from throwing money blindly at terrible ad concepts, utilizing mathematical probabilities instead of human guessing
  • Direct integration with ad platforms allows for a seamless, frictionless workflow from generation directly to publishing

Cons

  • Because it is programmatically assembling assets, the generated videos can occasionally feel formulaic, overly "template-driven," or lack the profound nuance of a human-directed commercial
  • The AI is dependent on the quality of the raw assets provided; if you upload terrible, blurry, low-resolution phone photos, it will generate terrible, blurry video ads
  • Pricing scales with the volume of assets and data connections, making it a serious ongoing SaaS commitment rather than a cheap one-off editing tool
  • While it handles the generation, the marketer still must possess a deep understanding of Media Buying strategy on Meta/TikTok to actually utilize the assets effectively

Pricing

Pencil utilizes a tiered subscription model structured for SMBs up through large-scale Enterprise agencies. Lower tiers offer a capped number of video exports and basic generative capabilities. Premium and Enterprise tiers unlock unlimited generation, advanced predictive analytics, custom brand governance, and dedicated account management, making it highly scalable for large-scale media buyers.

How It Compares

Pencil competes in the video generation and ad optimization space against tools like Omneky, VidMob, and basic editors like CapCut. CapCut requires intense human labor. VidMob is a large-scale, highly expensive enterprise analytics leader. Omneky and Pencil are highly similar, but Pencil differentiates itself by being historically hyper-focused on *Video Assembly from Raw Assets* for the SMB and mid-market e-commerce space. It excels at taking a pile of routine Shopify photos and churning out 20 punchy, high-energy TikTok ads in 10 minutes, making it indispensable for digital hustlers and DTC brands.
