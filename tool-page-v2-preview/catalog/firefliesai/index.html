Editorial Overview

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that joins online meetings to record, transcribe, and summarise conversations. It integrates with video conferencing platforms including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, and automatically creates searchable transcripts and AI-generated summaries with action items after meetings. Fireflies is designed for sales, recruiting, and business teams that have high meeting volumes and need structured records of conversations for follow-up, coaching, and knowledge retention. It also offers CRM integrations to push meeting data directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, and other systems. It competes directly with Otter.ai and tl;dv in the AI meeting transcription category, with a particular strength in its CRM integration and sales team use cases.

How it works

Users connect Fireflies to their conferencing tools and calendar. An AI assistant (Fred) automatically joins scheduled meetings and records audio, then generates a transcript and summary after the meeting ends. The transcript is searchable, with speaker labels and timestamps. AI analysis highlights action items, decisions, and questions. Integrated with CRM tools, Fireflies can auto-push meeting notes and follow-up tasks directly into sales or recruitment workflows.
