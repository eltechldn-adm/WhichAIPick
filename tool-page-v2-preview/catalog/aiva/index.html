Editorial Overview

AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) is a generative AI music composition platform designed to assist musicians, content creators, and game developers in scoring original soundtracks. Unlike consumer tools that generate 30-second novelty clips from text prompts, AIVA operates as a deep compositional assistant, generating full, structured MIDI files and audio tracks based on chosen musical styles, emotions, and specific harmonic parameters. AIVA was trained on a large-scale database of classical music (including Bach, Beethoven, and Mozart) alongside modern cinematic scoring structures, giving it a profound understanding of music theory. This makes it particularly adept at generating sweeping orchestral scores, dramatic cinematic trailers, and structured electronic music. It is widely used by independent game studios who need hours of background music but lack budget for a composer, and by professional producers who use AIVA's generated MIDI data as an initial "seed" to spark inspiration before manually orchestrating the track in their own Digital Audio Workstation (DAW).

How it works

Users select a "Generation Profile" ranging from Cinematic Action to Lo-Fi Chill. They can specify the key signature, time signature, and desired instrumentation. AIVA generates a multi-track composition in seconds. Crucially, the web interface includes a built-in piano roll editor. Users can manually click and drag individual generated notes, change the tempo, or delete entire instrument stems. Once satisfied, the track can be exported as an MP3/WAV, or exported as a MIDI file to be loaded into professional software like Logic Pro or Ableton.
