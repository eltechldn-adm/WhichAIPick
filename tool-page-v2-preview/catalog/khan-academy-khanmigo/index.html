Editorial Overview

Khanmigo is an AI-powered tutor and teaching assistant developed by the non-profit educational organization Khan Academy, built in direct partnership with OpenAI utilizing GPT-4. Rather than functioning as a standard chatbot that simply provides the answers to questions, Khanmigo is strictly engineered using pedagogical guidelines to guide students through the learning process using the Socratic method. It acts as a personalized, 1-on-1 virtual tutor. If a student is stuck on a math problem, Khanmigo will rarely give the direct answer; instead, it will ask probing questions, identify the specific step where the student made an error, and encourage them to solve it themselves. Furthermore, it provides a comprehensive suite of tools for teachers, utilizing AI to draft lesson plans, create rubrics, and track the progress of students utilizing the platform, bridging the gap between student personalized learning and administrative relief for educators.

How it works

Khanmigo exists as a chat interface integrated directly alongside Khan Academy's videos and exercises. A student watching a biology video can pause and type, "I don't understand mitochondria." Khanmigo will reply conversationally, explaining the concept and asking a follow-up question to ensure comprehension. For teachers, a separate dashboard provides tools to input a topic and grade level, instructing the AI to instantly generate a 45-minute structured lesson plan adhering to educational standards.

Who is it for?

Best For

Not Ideal For

  • Pricing structure makes it an add-on rather than part of Khan Academy's free core
  • Text-heavy chat interface might struggle to engage very young or visually-oriented learners
  • Hallucinations in complex higher-level math are rare but still a possibility
  • Cannot replace the empathetic human connection of a real teacher
