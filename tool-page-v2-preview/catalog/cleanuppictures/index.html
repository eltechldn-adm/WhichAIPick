Cleanup.pictures

Cleanup.pictures removes selected objects and defects from images using inpainting. Its browser editor and separately metered Clipdrop API have different limits.

Editorial Overview

Cleanup.pictures is a deeply specialized, single-purpose AI image editing tool designed exclusively for "inpainting"—the process of removing unwanted objects, text, or people from an image while seamlessly reconstructing the background behind them. Powered by advanced machine learning models (initially LaMa - Large Mask Inpainting), it performs this specific task faster and often better than complex legacy desktop software. Its primary differentiator is its established simplicity. It fundamentally removes the need to understand Photoshop concepts like the "Clone Stamp" or "Healing Brush." Users simply paint over the object they want gone, and the AI algorithm analyzes the surrounding pixels, lighting, and textures to natively generate a photorealistic background replacement in seconds. It is heavily utilized by real estate agents removing clutter from listing photos, e-commerce managers removing watermarks or logos from product shots, and social media managers rapidly cleaning up vacation or event photography on their phones.

How it works

A user visits the web app (on desktop or mobile) and uploads a photo. Using their mouse or finger, they adjust a brush size and highlight the unwanted element—for example, a trash can on a sidewalk. The highlighted area is masked in bright green. Upon releasing the brush, the AI processes the image and physically "paints in" the missing sidewalk and wall textures, effectively erasing the trash can from existence accurately.
