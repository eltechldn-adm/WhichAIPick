Editorial Overview

Lately (Lately.ai) is a highly sophisticated, deeply analytical AI content generation and social media management platform built primarily for B2B enterprise marketing teams. While basic schedulers (like Buffer) simply publish whatever text you type, Lately is engineered to ingest a company's large-scale, long-form core assets (hours of webinars, large-scale whitepapers, podcasts) and autonomously slice them into hundreds of accurately optimized, short-form social media posts. Its primary differentiator is its "Neuro-linguistic AI" and custom brand voice modeling. It doesn't just rely on generic LLMs to write posts. Lately explicitly connects to your company’s historical social media analytics. It studies exactly which specific words, sentence structures, and media types generated the highest engagement for *your specific brand* over the past two years. When it splices a webinar, it rewrites the accompanying text based strictly on that mathematical analysis, generating a highly potent custom voice model that is proven to drive clicks. It is heavily utilized by global B2B marketing teams, large-scale corporate broadcasting departments, and enterprise thought-leaders who produce highly dense, long-form content but desperately struggle to continuously feed the relentless daily beast of LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook algorithms.

How it works

A B2B SaaS company runs a highly successful, 45-minute video interview with an industry leader. Currently, that video sits dead on YouTube. The marketing manager uploads the raw video file into Lately. The AI engine transcribes the video, identifies the 30 most profound quotes and statistics, and automatically cuts the video into 30 distinct, 1-minute clips. Crucially, the AI then writes the social media copy for each clip. Because Lately has studied the brand's past LinkedIn metrics, it knows that starting posts with a question and using the word "Strategy" yields 40% higher engagement. It applies these rules to all 30 posts. The manager reviews the generated dashboard, clicks "Approve All," and Lately autonomously schedules them to trickle out across LinkedIn over the next three months.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Enterprise B2B Marketing Teams
  • Corporate Social Media Managers handling massive volume
  • Companies producing heavy Long-Form content (Podcasts, Webinars)
  • Thought-leaders and executives managing vast personal brands

Not Ideal For

  • It is exclusively built and priced for the enterprise; it is highly expensive and completely inaccessible to a solo founder or small local business
  • The reliance on historical data means if your past social strategy was terrible and generated zero engagement, the AI has no baseline of "good" data to learn from
  • Onboarding is highly complex; training the custom AI model and setting up the large-scale integration pipelines requires a dedicated implementation period
  • The UI is extremely dense and analytical, prioritizing large-scale data tables over visual simplicity
