Harvey AI

Harvey AI is a Business tool used for legal work automation, contract analysis, and legal research. It is intended for Am Law 100 global law firms, Massive in-house corporate legal departments (Fortune 500), and Partners managing highly complex M&A due diligence.

Editorial Overview

Harvey AI is a highly secretive, elite artificial intelligence platform built exclusively for large-scale, global law firms (Am Law 100) and premier corporate legal departments. Unlike consumer-facing AI, Harvey is powered by customized versions of OpenAI’s GPT models that have been heavily trained on immense datasets of case law, large-scale corporate contracts, and regulatory statutes, backed by significant investment from the OpenAI Startup Fund. Its primary differentiator is its extreme focus on data security, generation reduction, and bespoke enterprise implementation. Harvey is not a web app a solo lawyer signs up for with a credit card; it is a custom-deployed organizational brain. It acts as an infinitely scalable associate attorney, capable of scanning thousands of pages in a multi-billion dollar M&A (Mergers and Acquisitions) data room, drafting initial complex litigation strategies, or analyzing cross-border regulatory compliance in minutes. It is utilized almost exclusively by the world’s largest, most prestigious legal institutions (such as Allen & Overy or Macfarlanes) to drastically reduce unbillable associate hours, granting them a large-scale competitive advantage in executing highly complex, volume-heavy corporate law.

How it works

Because Harvey is a bespoke enterprise tool, the interface varies but functions similarly to an highly secure, law-specific chat interface. A senior partner at a global firm might query Harvey during an M&A transaction: "Review these 400 supplier contracts and highlight every instance where the termination clause triggers upon a change of control, and summarize the financial liabilities." The AI securely ingests the confidential documents into an isolated, protected instance, utilizes its specialized legal training to analyze the dense legalese, and outputs a highly structured, accurate memo directly citing the specific clauses in the uploaded contracts.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Am Law 100 global law firms
  • Massive in-house corporate legal departments (Fortune 500)
  • Partners managing highly complex M&A due diligence
  • Legal institutions requiring absolute data security and zero-trust cloud infrastructure

Not Ideal For

  • It is entirely inaccessible to the general public, solo practitioners, or even mid-sized law firms; it is strictly invite/enterprise-only
  • Because of its secrecy, there is no public trial or transparent pricing, making it a "black box" until you engage their enterprise sales team
  • Requires a large-scale paradigm shift in how law firms bill clients, as AI eliminates the "billable hours" traditionally generated by junior baseline document review
  • Despite legal tuning, senior partners must still rigorously review the AI's output due to the catastrophic consequences of legal hallucinations
