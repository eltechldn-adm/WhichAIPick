Editorial Overview

Fathom is an AI-powered meeting assistant designed to record, transcribe, highlight, and summarize video conference calls on platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. It operates as an unobtrusive bot that joins calls to handle all note-taking responsibilities, allowing participants to focus entirely on the conversation. Fathom separates itself in a crowded market through its proactive focus on simplicity and its completely free core product for individuals. It emphasizes real-time highlighting: users can click a button during a call to flag a moment, and Fathom automatically extracts that specific clip and transcript section into a neat summary afterward. It is heavily adopted by sales professionals, customer success managers, and remote teams who need to sync meeting outcomes directly into CRM platforms (like Salesforce or HubSpot) without manual data entry.

How it works

A user installs the Fathom app or browser extension. When a Zoom, Meet, or Teams call begins, the Fathom bot automatically requests to join (providing mandatory recording consent notices to participants). During the call, Fathom transcribes the dialogue in real-time. The user has a side panel with buttons (e.g., "Action Item", "Insight", "Positive"). Clicking a button flags the current conversation. After the call, an AI generates a structured summary, and the flagged highlights are packaged as shareable video clips.
