SlideSpeak

SlideSpeak generates presentations from supplied content and provides document chat and slide editing. A developer API supports programmatic presentation creation.

Editorial Overview

SlideSpeak (SlideSpeak.co) is a highly specialized, intensely practical AI utility engineered explicitly to solve a deeply unglamorous corporate problem: parsing, summarizing, and presenting large-scale, impenetrable PowerPoint (PPTX) and PDF presentations. While tools like Tome focus on *building* presentations from scratch, SlideSpeak focuses on *interrogating* them. It allows users to upload large-scale, 150-slide corporate pitch decks or dense academic PDF lectures and instantly transforms the static file into an interactive, conversational chatbot wrapper. Its primary differentiator is its "Conversational Document Intelligence." In the modern corporate world, executives are frequently emailed large-scale 80-slide update decks on Friday afternoon. Manually clicking through 80 slides of dense charts to find one specific budget metric is agonizing. SlideSpeak completely halts this workflow. The executive uploads the large-scale PowerPoint. The AI physically reads every slide, extracts the text and context, and provides a chat interface. The executive simply types: "What was the Q3 Revenue projected on slide 42?" and the AI instantly answers the query, providing a direct citation to the exact slide where the data lives, acting as an instant executive assistant. It is heavily utilized by C-Suite Executives attempting to rapidly parse large-scale consulting reports (McKinsey, Deloitte decks), university students uploading 50-slide professor lecture PDFs to study for exams, and B2B sales teams who need to instantly locate a highly specific technical specification hidden deep within a vendor's large-scale product deck during a live client call.

How it works

A B2B software buyer receives a complex 90-slide PowerPoint deck from a vendor detailing a major enterprise software implementation. The buyer does not have 3 hours to read the entire file. They upload the PPTX to SlideSpeak. Crucially, the AI instantly provides a 1-paragraph summary of the entire 90-slide document. The buyer then utilizes the AI Chatbot interface. They ask: "What are the exact data privacy security protocols mentioned in this deck?" SlideSpeak scans its internal ingestion of the file and replies: "The deck states on Slide 64 that AES-256 encryption is standard, and SOC-2 compliance is detailed on Slide 67." The buyer instantly clicks the citation, verifying the data on Slide 67, extracting exactly the information they needed from the large-scale file in 30 seconds.
