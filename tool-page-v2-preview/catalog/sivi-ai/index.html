Editorial Overview

Sivi AI is a highly specialized, programmatic graphic design platform explicitly built to instantly convert raw text into complete, multi-layered visual compositions. While standard generative AI tools (like Midjourney) output a single, flat, uneditable pixel image, Sivi is fundamentally a layout engine. It takes a block of text (like a headline and a promotional offer) and autonomously generates highly structured, editable banners, social media posts, and display ads using intelligent typography pairing, color psychology, and geometric layout algorithms. Its primary differentiator is its "Text-to-Design" architectural capability. A designer does not have to open a Canva template and manually drag text boxes around to fit a new paragraph. The user inputs their brand guidelines (fonts, hex colors) and a paragraph of text. Sivi mathematically calculates the visual weight of the text, selects relevant background imagery or vectors, and instantly renders dozens of completely different structural UI layouts. Crucially, every single element in the generated design remains editable, allowing the user to tweak the padding, swap the image, or change the headline without eliminateing the entire layout. It is heavily utilized by high-volume digital marketing agencies churning out hundreds of display ads per week, e-commerce managers needing rapid promotional banners for a weekend sale without a dedicated graphic designer, and content creators attempting to automate the tedious process of converting a written quote into an Instagram graphic.

How it works

A marketing manager needs to create a set of Facebook Ads for a "Summer 50% Off Shoe Sale." They open Sivi AI. They input the brand's exact color palette (Orange and Navy) and font family (Inter). They type the text: "large-scale Summer Running Shoe Sale! 50% Off Selected Styles. Shop Now." The AI reads the intent. Within seconds, Sivi outputs 20 different layout variations. One is a minimalist layout with large-scale typography. Another features an AI-selected stock photo of a runner integrated behind a geometric text box. The manager selects a layout and clicks a button to instantly resize it from a square (Instagram) into a horizontal rectangle (Facebook Banner). The AI intelligently repositions the text and image to accurately fit the new aspect ratio autonomously.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • High-volume Digital Marketing Agencies running vast display campaigns
  • E-commerce Store Owners needing rapid promotional assets
  • Content Creators automating graphic generation
  • Teams lacking a dedicated, full-time graphic designer

Not Ideal For

  • Because it fundamentally relies on assembling geometric layouts and typography, it struggles to generate notable surreal, complex, highly artistic masterpieces (like a large-scale cinematic fantasy painting), restricting it strictly to corporate/marketing graphics
  • The UI can feel highly structural and programmatic, occasionally lacking the deeply fluid, artistic freedom provided by the large-scale blank canvas of a tool like Adobe Illustrator
  • If the input text is highly dense or chaotic, the algorithmic layout engine will occasionally output hilariously cramped, visually claustrophobic designs as it struggles desperately to fit 500 words onto a small square
  • It operates in a complex competitive space where large-scale leader like Canva are proactively deploying nearly identical "capability Design" features natively inside their established ecosystems
