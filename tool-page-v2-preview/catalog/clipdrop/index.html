Editorial Overview

Clipdrop is an ecosystem of AI-powered image editing and generation tools initially developed by Init ML and later acquired (and subsequently spun out) in the generative AI space (currently backed by Jasper). It utilizes state-of-the-art models, including Stable Diffusion architectures, to provide professional-grade image manipulation directly in the browser. Unlike pure prompt-based generators, Clipdrop focuses on utility workflows for graphic designers, photographers, and e-commerce professionals. Its toolkit tackles specific, historically labor-intensive Photoshop tasks—such as removing objects, swapping backgrounds, uncropping (outpainting) images, and relighting 2D photos. It acts as a lightweight, accessible AI alternative to heavy desktop editing software, providing near-instant results for image correction and augmentation tasks without requiring complex masking or layering skills.

How it works

Users access the various tools through a unified web interface or mobile app. To remove an object, a user scrubs a brush over an item, and the AI utilizes inpainting to erase it and reconstruct the background. For relighting, the AI constructs a normal map of the 2D image, allowing users to drag virtual light sources across the screen to change shadows and highlights dynamically. Background replacement isolates the subject and composites it seamlessly into generated environments.
