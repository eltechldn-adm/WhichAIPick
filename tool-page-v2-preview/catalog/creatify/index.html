Creatify

Creatify produces image and video advertising creatives, including avatar presenters. Paid plans expand production and campaign tools, and an API supports automated generation workflows.

Editorial Overview

Creatify.ai is a highly specialized generative AI platform built explicitly for digital marketers, e-commerce brands, and dropshippers to generate short-form video advertisements. While traditional AI video generators focus on cinematic scenes or corporate talking heads, Creatify focuses on generating high-converting, performance-marketing creatives optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Ads. Its primary differentiator is its "URL-to-Video" capability and its integrated digital avatars. A marketer can paste the link to a Shopify product page, and the AI will autonomously extract the images, write an proactive marketing script, generate a synthetic AI voiceover (or avatar) to speak the script, and edit the final video complete with captions and upbeat background music in 5 minutes. It drastically reduces the cost of performance marketing, allowing media buyers to rapidly A/B test dozens of different video ad hooks and styles without hiring actors, renting studios, or paying freelance video editors.

How it works

A dropshipper looking to test a new gadget pastes the Amazon or Shopify product URL into Creatify. The AI extracts the product details, images, and pricing. The AI then writes 5 different short marketing scripts (e.g., one focusing on pain points, one focusing on features). The user selects a script. They then pick an AI Avatar (a digital human presenter) and an AI voice (e.g., "Enthusiastic Female"). The platform renders the video, showcasing the avatar speaking the script overlaid on the product images, complete with TikTok-style animated captions and trending background audio. The user downloads the MP4 and uploads it directly to the Meta Ad Manager.
