Lumen5

Lumen5 helps turn articles into video storyboards, matching scenes with media and adding voiceover. Its editor runs in a desktop browser; the free Community plan includes Lumen5 branding.

Visit Website Compare Lumen5

Editorial Overview

Lumen5 is an AI-powered video creation platform heavily targeted at marketing departments, internal communications teams, and social media managers. Designed to make video creation as simple as building a slide deck, the platform specializes in repurposing written content—such as blog posts, whitepapers, or listicles—into engaging, text-heavy video formats popular on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram. Rather than providing a complex cinematic editing timeline, Lumen5 uses AI to construct a storyboard. It reads the provided text, summarizes the key points, pairs the concepts with relevant background stock footage, and overlays typography automatically calculated for reading speeds. It is the tool of choice for enterprise marketing teams who lack video production skills but understand that video drives higher engagement on social channels than static text.

How it works

Users input text, upload a document, or paste a blog URL. The Lumen5 algorithm analyzes the text using NLP to identify important sentences, summarizing the content into bite-sized highlights. The software creates a storyboard, selecting background footage from its Getty Images library that matches the keywords of each scene. Users review the storyboard, drag and drop to swap media or adjust text, select a brand color palette, and export the video.
