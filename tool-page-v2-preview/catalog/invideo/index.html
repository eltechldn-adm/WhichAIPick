Editorial Overview

InVideo is an AI-powered video creation platform that enables users to produce marketing videos, social content, and explainer videos from text scripts or AI-generated content. It provides a large library of customisable templates and an AI workflow that converts a topic or script into a complete video with selected footage, voiceover, captions, and background music. InVideo is used by marketers, content creators, and businesses that need to produce video content at volume without specialist video production skills or expensive software. Its AI-driven workflow makes it particularly accessible for teams producing frequent social or marketing video output. InVideo competes primarily with Pictory and Lumen5 in the AI script-to-video category, positioning itself as a more feature-complete platform with broader template and media library options.

How it works

Users select a workflow — script to video, article to video, or AI-generated from a topic — and InVideo generates a video draft with suggested footage clips, a voiceover (AI-generated or text-to-speech), captions, and background music. Users can edit individual scenes, swap media, adjust timing, and customise text within InVideo's browser-based editor. Finished videos are rendered and exported or shared directly to social platforms.
