Editorial Overview

HeyGen is an AI video generation platform that produces talking avatar videos from text scripts, similar in category to Synthesia. HeyGen gained significant market attention in 2023 for its video translation feature, which can dub existing video content — including the speaker's voice and lip movements — into other languages using AI. It is used by content creators, marketing teams, e-learning producers, and sales professionals who need to create professional video without camera equipment or studio production. HeyGen offers a library of AI avatars, voice cloning, and customisable video templates. HeyGen has positioned itself as a more innovation-forward alternative in the AI avatar video space, with frequent new feature releases and a growing user base across both consumer and business markets.

How it works

Users create videos by selecting an avatar, entering a script, choosing a language and voice, and submitting for rendering. The platform animates the avatar to deliver the script with matching lip sync. For video translation, users upload an existing video and select a target language — HeyGen generates a dubbed version with translated audio and re-animated lip movements. Video output can be downloaded or shared via link.
