Editorial Overview

Shortly AI is an highly unique, highly specialized AI writing application engineered entirely around the philosophy of frictionless, unguided creative flow. Before the era of highly structured, rigidly templated "marketing dashboards" (like Jasper or Copy.ai), Shortly AI provided an intensely minimalist, brutally clean, distraction-free environment. It was designed primarily for fiction authors, long-form essayists, and creative writers who did not want to fill out a complex "Facebook Ad" form, but simply wanted an AI co-writer to autonomously continue their exact train of thought indefinitely. Its primary differentiator is its core mechanical interaction: "The Dance." Shortly AI features almost zero buttons, menus, or complex settings. The user opens what looks accurately identical to a blank piece of paper. The writer begins typing a story or an essay organically. When the writer hits terrible writer's block, they simply use the legendary keyboard shortcut (`Cmd + Enter`), invoking the "Write for Me" command. The AI reads the previous text, accurately adopts the user's unique, specific tone, and seamlessly synthesizes the next 3 paragraphs, allowing the human and the machine to collaboratively weave large-scale long-form texts without ever breaking the "flow state." It is heavily utilized by self-published fiction authors generating large-scale 80,000-word novels at complex speeds, dedicated content writers executing highly nuanced, deeply personal narrative essays, and creatives who suffer from profound ADHD and require established, complex visual simplicity to maintain focus.

How it works

A self-published sci-fi author is 20,000 words into a novel. They are writing a highly emotional, complex scene where the protagonist discovers the true nature of the galaxy. They type: "She stared out the viewport, the nebula reflecting in her terrified eyes. She finally realized what the signal meant. It wasn't a warning. It was..." The author suddenly hits complete writer's block. They have no idea what it is. Without clicking any buttons, they press `Cmd + Enter`. The Shortly AI engine instantly reads the emotional context and generates: "...It was a countdown. A large-scale, echoing heartbeat broadcast across three star systems, ticking toward established zero." The author smiles, edits the word "heartbeat" to "frequency," and seamlessly continues typing, having bypassed the creative block in 3 seconds without leaving the document.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Fiction Authors and Self-Published Novelists
  • Creative Essayists and Long-form Content Writers
  • Users with extreme ADHD requiring distraction-free environments
  • Writers who possess a profound hatred for complex, highly corporate "marketing" software templates

Not Ideal For

  • Shortly AI was famously acquired by Jasper AI; consequently, active development on the standalone Historically platform has effectively ceased, leaving it in a state of highly functional but static abandonment
  • Because it lacks templates, highly structured corporate tasks (like writing an accurately formatted Amazon Product Description or a fast Facebook Ad) require significantly more effort and prompting expertise than utilizing a tool built specifically for it
  • The LLM models, while incredible at creative flow, are generally not directly connected to the live internet search APIs, meaning it is prone to large-scale historical generation if asked to write an essay accurately citing 2023 statistics
  • Users terrified of the "Blank Page" will find no comfort here; the tool requires the user to proactively start typing and set the structural tone, rather than guiding them through an automated wizard
