Editorial Overview

Grain is a specialized, AI-powered meeting recording and conversation intelligence platform designed proactively around ease-of-use and the democratization of customer insights. While large-scale enterprise tools focus heavily on sales-coaching and revenue operations, Grain is built for User Researchers, Product Managers, and Customer Success teams to easily capture, synthesize, and share "Voice of the Customer" moments. Its primary differentiator is its focus on the "Highlight Reel" and cross-functional sharing. Grain's AI auto-joins Zoom or Google Meet calls, generates a highly accurate transcript, and automatically summarizes the meeting into brief notes. However, its true power lies in allowing a product manager to highlight a 30-second transcript segment where a customer complains about a bug, instantly converting that highlight into a shareable video clip pasted directly into a Slack or Notion channel. It is heavily utilized by agile product teams and UX researchers who need to ensure that the actual engineers writing the code hear the raw, unedited frustration (or joy) of the customer, bypassing lengthy corporate briefs in favor of instant video evidence.

How it works

Users connect Grain to their calendar. The Grain bot autonomously joins scheduled Google Meet or Zoom calls as a participant. It records the audio/video and generates a live transcript. Post-meeting, the AI generates a brief summary (e.g., "Feature Requests," "Action Items"). A User Researcher reviewing the transcript highlights a paragraph where the customer discusses a competitor. Grain instantly creates a 45-second video clip of that exact moment. The researcher clicks "Share," dropping the video link directly into the engineering team's Slack channel, allowing them to watch the customer's reaction instantly without logging into Grain.
