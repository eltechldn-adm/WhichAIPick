Editorial Overview

Avoma is a comprehensive AI meeting assistant and conversation intelligence platform tailored specifically for B2B sales, customer success, and high-velocity meeting environments. While many tools simply record and transcribe audio, Avoma covers the entire lifecycle of a meeting: from collaborative pre-meeting agendas to live bookmarking, robust AI summarization, and deep integration deep into Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems. Its core differentiator is its focus on structured, actionable outputs. Instead of returning a large-scale wall of transcribed text, Avoma's AI automatically extracts and categorizes information into distinct business buckets—such as "Next Steps," "Pain Points," "Competitor Mentions," and "Budget Discussions." It is heavily utilized by mid-market Revenue Operations (RevOps) teams to ensure that critical deal intelligence is never lost, reducing the administrative burden of manual CRM data entry for sales representatives by seamlessly syncing AI-generated notes directly to appropriate Salesforce or HubSpot records.

How it works

Users connect Avoma to their calendar (Google/Outlook). The Avoma bot automatically joins designated Zoom, Teams, or Meet calls. Before the meeting, users can utilize shared agenda templates. During the meeting, the AI transcribes the conversation with speaker identification. Post-meeting, the AI analyzes the transcript to generate categorized summaries. Representatives review the automated notes, edit them if necessary, and Avoma pushes the final, structured data directly to the corresponding Contact and Opportunity fields in the CRM.
