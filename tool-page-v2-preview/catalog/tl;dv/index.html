Editorial Overview

tl;dv (Too Long; Didn't View) is a highly proactive, fiercely modern AI meeting intelligence and recording platform specifically engineered to fundamentally eliminate the agonizing corporate necessity of the "mandatory 1-hour alignment meeting." While standard software (like a basic Zoom Cloud Recording) outputs a large-scale, useless 2-gigabyte MP4 file that nobody will ever watch, tl;dv actively attends the meeting as a robotic participant, transcribes the entire conversation in 30+ languages, and utilizes heavy Generative AI to instantly synthesize chaotic arguments into highly specific, clickable timestamped highlight reels. Its primary differentiator is its "Democratization of Meeting Asynchrony" and surgical "Highlight Extraction." During a chaotic 45-minute product design call between a PM and an Engineer, a user can simply type `#urgent` in the native tl;dv chat. After the meeting, the AI doesn't just summarize the call; it physically cuts the video into short, specific cinematic clips based on those tags. Furthermore, a large-scale team can leverage the "Global AI Search." A CEO who missed a week of meetings doesn't watch the recordings; they physically search the company's database for the word "Pricing." The AI instantly returns a 2-minute super-cut of exactly every time the word "Pricing" was spoken across 14 different meetings that week, stitched together sequentially. It is heavily utilized by large-scale, highly distributed global remote teams (where synchronous time-zone alignment is impossible), UX Researchers conducting high-volume qualitative user interviews, ambitious startup founders recording rapid investor calls, and cross-functional agile teams seeking to proactively eliminate unnecessary meeting attendance without losing operational context.

How it works

A Product Manager (PM) is conducting a 60-minute User Research interview over Google Meet. They invite the tl;dv bot to the call. The interview is messy and rambling. At minute 42, the user excitedly mentions a critical feature idea. The PM presses a simple keyboard shortcut to "Highlight." As soon as the call ends, the PM does not re-watch the video. They open the tl;dv dashboard. The AI has already generated a 4-bullet point executive summary. The PM clicks the generated "Feature Idea" highlight clip. It is an accurately cut, 30-second video snippet of the user explaining the idea with accurately AI-generated subtitles. The PM clicks a button to seamlessly drop that specific 30-second video directly into a Jira ticket and tags the Lead Engineer. The Engineer instantly understands the user's emotion and context without watching the other 59 minutes of the call.
