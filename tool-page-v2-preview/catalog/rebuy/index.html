Rebuy

Rebuy is a Marketing tool used for ecommerce personalization, smart upsells, and post purchase offers. It is intended for High-volume Shopify and Shopify Plus E-commerce Brands, DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) companies selling consumable or complementary products (Beauty, Supplements, Pet Food), and E-commerce Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Managers.

Editorial Overview

Rebuy (Rebuy Engine) is an highly powerful, AI-driven personalization and merchandising engine built explicitly for the Shopify e-commerce ecosystem. Its entire singular function is to proactively increase a store's AOV (Average Order Value) and LTV (Customer Lifetime Value) by mathematically calculating exactly which products a customer is most likely to buy, and presenting those specific products to them seamlessly across every touchpoint of their shopping journey. Its primary differentiator is its deep, native focus on the "Shopify Post-Click" experience. While an ad agency focuses on getting a user to click a Facebook ad and land on the site, Rebuy takes over the exact second they arrive. It uses historical purchasing data to generate Amazon-style "Frequently Bought Together" widgets on product pages, highly intelligent "Upsell" popups the second an item is added to the cart, and large-scale "Post-Purchase" offers on the checkout confirmation page when the customer's credit card is already "hot." It is heavily utilized by large-scale DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) Shopify Plus brands—coffee roasters, cosmetic companies, and supplement brands—who operate on razor-thin advertising margins and absolutely mathematically require every single customer to add an extra $15 item to their cart before checking out to remain profitable.

How it works

A customer is shopping on a popular Shopify cosmetics store. They add a $30 "Hydrating Face Cleanser" to their cart. Instantly, an elegant, slide-out "Smart Cart" appears (powered by Rebuy). The cart uses AI to analyze the specific cleanser they selected alongside millions of data points from previous shoppers. The AI determines that people who buy that cleanser frequently also buy a specific "Vitamin C Serum." Inside the cart, a dynamic widget offers the Serum with a single "Add for $20" button. The customer clicks it. After paying $50, they land on the order confirmation page. Rebuy strikes again: "Exclusive Offer: Add a travel-sized moisturizer to this exact order for just $10 right now!" The customer clicks yes, instantly modifying the live Shopify order and pushing the total to $60.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • High-volume Shopify and Shopify Plus E-commerce Brands
  • DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) companies selling consumable or complementary products (Beauty, Supplements, Pet Food)
  • E-commerce Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Managers
  • Brands looking to maximize Average Order Value (AOV) to offset rising ad costs

Not Ideal For

  • It is exclusively built for the Shopify ecosystem; if a brand operates on Magento, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce, the platform is functionally useless
  • Pricing can become complex expensive for large-scale brands, as the tiers are specifically tied to total order volume or the exact amount of revenue the tool generates (taking a cut of success)
  • If not configured carefully, bombarding a customer with 4 different proactive pop-up upsells before they can buy a single t-shirt creates a highly stressful, scammy user experience that damages brand trust
  • Setting up the highly specific, custom merchandising rules for a catalog of 10,000 SKUs requires dedicated effort and strategy; it is not purely "set it and forget it"
