Editorial Overview

Craft (or Craft.do) is a premium, highly visual document creation and knowledge management platform available on Mac, iOS, Windows, and Web. While initially positioned as a competitor to Notion, Craft differentiates itself with a relentless focus on native performance, aesthetic excellence, and the creation of highly polished, shareable documents rather than complex relational databases. Its AI integration, the "Craft Assistant," acts as an omnipresent editor and ghostwriter within the document. It focuses heavily on structured rewriting, translation, and text generation natively integrated into Craft's unique block-based architecture. It is predominantly used by creative agencies, product managers, and executives who prioritize presenting beautiful, structured information (like product specs, client proposals, or mood boards) to external stakeholders or internal teams smoothly and instantly.

How it works

A user opens a new document in the highly responsive, native Mac or iOS app. They type out a messy brainstorm for a client proposal. They highlight the text, hit a keyboard shortcut, and prompt the Craft Assistant to "Rewrite this into a professional, persuasive pitch." The AI instantly reformats the text. Because Craft is block-based, the AI respects structural elements like headers, toggles, and bullet points. The user embellishes the document with high-res images and beautiful gradient card styling, then generates a single "Secret Link" to share the accurately formatted web page instantly with the client.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Creative agencies producing client-facing proposals
  • Executives and product managers creating structured briefs
  • Users who prioritize native desktop/mobile app performance and aesthetics
  • Teams needing a beautiful, fast alternative to Google Docs or Notion

Not Ideal For

  • Lacks the deep, complex relational databases and formulas that make Notion a powerhouse for operational task tracking
  • AI features are mostly text-based (summarize, rewrite); lacks advanced programmatic automation or complex data sorting AI
  • While it has Windows/Web versions, the platform is undeniably optimized for and heavily favors the Apple ecosystem
  • The extreme focus on visual hierarchy can occasionally feel restrictive compared to a raw, unstructured canvas like Obsidian
