Nosto

Nosto is a Marketing tool used for ecommerce personalization, product recommendations, and behavioral popups. It is intended for Mid-Market and Enterprise E-commerce Retailers, Digital Merchandising and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Managers, and Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Fashion, Beauty, and Lifestyle brands.

Editorial Overview

Nosto is a comprehensive Commerce Experience Platform (CXP) explicitly designed to bring deep, AI-driven personalization to enterprise online retail. Operating primarily in the e-commerce sector, it analyzes vast amounts of behavioral data—clicks, purchases, search queries, and browsing history—to deliver highly tailored online shopping experiences to individual users in real-time, aiming to increase conversion rates and average order values. Unlike simple "recommended products" plugins, Nosto controls the entire storefront experience. If a user frequently browses premium running shoes, Nosto doesn't just suggest running shoes; it can dynamically alter the homepage banner to feature athletic wear, re-order category pages so running gear appears first, and adjust pricing or pop-up discounts based on the user's highly specific purchasing probability. It is deeply integrated into large-scale e-commerce ecosystems (like Shopify Plus, Magento, and Salesforce Commerce Cloud), providing retail marketers with the immense algorithmic power required to treat every single visitor to their digital storefront as if they were a known VIP customer walking into a physical luxury boutique.

How it works

When a shopper visits a large fashion retailer's website, Nosto immediately begins tracking their behavior. The shopper clicks on two different blue floral summer dresses but does not purchase. They leave and return the next day. Nosto's AI instantly recognizes the returning user. It dynamically changes the homepage hero banner to feature a summer dress collection. It injects a "Picked for You" product carousel featuring other blue dresses. When the user navigates to the broader "Dresses" category, Nosto autonomously sorts the hundreds of dresses to ensure blue, floral, mid-priced options are at the very top of the grid, large-scale increasing the likelihood the shopper will find and buy an item.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Mid-Market and Enterprise E-commerce Retailers
  • Digital Merchandising and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Managers
  • Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Fashion, Beauty, and Lifestyle brands
  • Retail marketers running comprehensive omnichannel campaigns

Not Ideal For

  • It is a heavy, complex, enterprise-grade platform; the learning curve for utilizing the full suite of merchandising tools can be steep for small teams
  • Requires a significant volume of site traffic and sales data to become highly effective; the AI cannot personalize an experience if the site has very few visitors
  • proactive or poorly configured personalization (e.g., chasing a user relentlessly with the exact same pair of shoes across every page) can feel intrusive or annoying to shoppers
  • The pricing structure is a significant investment, entirely ruling it out for small, independent Shopify stores just starting out
