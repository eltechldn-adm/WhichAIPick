Editorial Overview

Intercom Fin is an AI customer support agent developed by Intercom, designed to automatically resolve customer support queries without human involvement. It is built on large language models and trained to understand a company's support documentation to provide accurate, contextual responses to customer questions. Fin operates within Intercom's existing customer messaging platform, meaning it can be deployed by teams already using Intercom without adopting a separate tool. It handles incoming conversations autonomously and escalates to human agents when queries fall outside its ability to resolve confidently. Intercom Fin targets businesses running customer support operations at scale, where a reduction in repetitive query volume for human agents has direct cost and efficiency implications.

How it works

Fin is connected to a company's help centre articles and support documentation. When a customer sends a support message, Fin attempts to resolve it by referencing the trained content base and providing a direct answer. If it cannot confidently resolve the query, it routes the conversation to a human agent. Usage and resolution rate data is tracked in Intercom's reporting dashboard. Fin can be configured with custom personas, escalation rules, and coverage windows.

Who is it for?

  • Requires existing Intercom subscription
  • Fin usage is priced per resolution, adding cost at scale
  • Cannot handle complex or emotional support situations well
