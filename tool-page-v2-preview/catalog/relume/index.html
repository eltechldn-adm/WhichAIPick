Relume

Relume is a Design tool used for generating sitemaps, building wireframes, and web design library. It is intended for Freelance Web Designers utilizing Webflow or Figma, High-volume web design agencies seeking rapid scalability, and UI/UX Designers seeking to skip repetitive wireframing.

Editorial Overview

Relume (Relume Library / Relume AI) is a large-scale innovative, hyper-efficient site architecture and design platform built explicitly for the modern web design industry (specifically leveraging the Webflow and Figma ecosystems). It fundamentally eliminate the traditional, grueling "wireframing" process. Rather than starting a large-scale website build by drawing endless grey boxes in Figma or writing custom HTML/CSS grids, Relume provides the world's largest library of pre-built, pixel-accurately, highly responsive UI components (navbars, hero sections, pricing grids) and utilizes AI to assemble them instantaneously. Its primary differentiator is its "Sitemap-to-Wireframe" AI pipeline. A freelance web designer working with an arbitrary client (e.g., a "boutique dental clinic") does not spend a week struggling with structure. They simply type the client's industry into Relume. The AI engine instantly generates a comprehensive structural sitemap (Home, About Us, Services, Contact). The designer clicks "Generate," and the AI autonomously selects the appropriate structural components from the large-scale library, populates them with highly relevant marketing copy, and outputs a complete, responsive, multi-page wireframe. Crucially, with a single click, this entire structure can be pasted accurately into Figma or Webflow, complete with professional CSS class naming conventions (Client-First). It is heavily utilized by high-end Webflow agencies, freelance web designers, and marketing teams attempting to large-scale scale their production velocity, allowing them to skip the complex "blank canvas phase" and move directly into advanced typography, color branding, and client delivery in a fraction of traditional timelines.

How it works

A freelance Webflow developer lands a $5,000 contract to build a website for a local B2B software startup. Traditional timeline: 1 week wireframing, 1 week design, 1 week development. With Relume, the developer opens the AI site builder. They prompt: "A 5-page website for a B2B SaaS company specializing in AI inventory management." The AI suggests a highly logical sitemap. The developer approves it. The AI instantly generates a wireframe: it pulls a "Hero Section with Video" component for the homepage, an "Alternating Feature Grid" for the product page, and a "Tiered Pricing Block." The developer reviews the AI-generated copywriting ("Say goodbye to stockouts"). They click "Export to Webflow." The entire site—fully responsive, utilizing accurately Flexbox layouts and standard CSS classes—instantly populates in their Webflow canvas. The developer simply swaps the grey backgrounds for brand colors, updates the fonts, and the large-scale structural core of the 3-week project is finished in 4 hours.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Freelance Web Designers utilizing Webflow or Figma
  • High-volume web design agencies seeking rapid scalability
  • UI/UX Designers seeking to skip repetitive wireframing
  • Marketing teams executing massive landing-page operations

Not Ideal For

  • Because thousands of agencies are utilizing the exact same component library, developers who fail to proactively apply custom styling, unique photography, and bespoke interactions will inevitably produce sites that look structurally identical to their competitors
  • It fundamentally operates within the Webflow and Figma ecosystems; if an agency relies heavily on Shopify liquid coding, WordPress Elementor, or custom React, the platform’s primary value proposition is completely negated
  • The AI copywriter generates highly competent structural text, but it completely lacks the deep, nuanced, highly strategic psychological genius required for genuine, elite high-converting sales copy
  • The focus is entirely on structure and layout; it does not generate notable complex 3D animations, custom JavaScript interactions, or hyper-specific complex database integrations
