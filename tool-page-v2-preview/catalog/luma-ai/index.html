Editorial Overview

Luma AI is an AI video and 3D content generation platform. It offers Dream Machine, a text-to-video and image-to-video generation model, and also provides tools for capturing 3D scenes from photographs using neural radiance fields (NeRF) technology. Luma was founded in 2021 and focuses on AI-generated visual media for creative and commercial applications. Dream Machine gained significant attention in 2024 for producing high-quality, physically realistic video from text prompts and reference images. The platform positions itself as a professional-grade creative tool for filmmakers, designers, and visual content producers. Luma AI targets creative professionals seeking high physical coherence in AI-generated video — a known limitation of other video generation tools — along with product teams needing 3D capture without specialist hardware.

How it works

For Dream Machine video generation, users enter a text prompt and optionally provide a reference image as the first frame. The model generates a short video clip (typically 5–10 seconds) with motion and physical coherence. Multiple clips can be generated from the same prompt and stitched together. For 3D capture, users upload a series of photographs taken around an object or space, and Luma AI processes them into a navigable 3D model. Both outputs can be downloaded or shared.
