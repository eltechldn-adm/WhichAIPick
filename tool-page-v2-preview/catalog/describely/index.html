Editorial Overview

Describely is an AI-powered e-commerce content generation platform built specifically to automate the creation of large-scale product catalogs. While general AI writers can write a single product description, Describely is engineered for scale: processing hundreds or thousands of SKUs simultaneously to generate SEO-optimized titles, bullet points, and robust meta descriptions for online retailers. Its primary differentiator is its deep integration into the e-commerce backend (acting as a Product Information Management (PIM) system overlay) and its bulk-generation capabilities. A retailer can import a raw CSV from a supplier containing only basic specs (e.g., "Shirt, Cotton, Blue, XL"), and Describely will autonomously flesh out unique, brand-aligned sales copy for the entire catalog in minutes. It is heavily utilized by Shopify store owners, large-scale dropshipping operations, and e-commerce marketing agencies who understand that rich, unique product descriptions are critical for SEO and conversion rates, but find the manual writing process prohibitively slow and expensive.

How it works

An e-commerce manager connects their Shopify store to Describely. The platform imports all 5,000 active products. The manager selects 50 "Winter Coats" that currently have terrible, factory-default descriptions. They set the AI rules: "Tone: Energetic, Target Keyword: Waterproof Winter Coat, Length: 2 Paragraphs + 5 Bullets." They click "Generate Bulk." The AI processes all 50 SKUs concurrently, referencing their tags and images to write highly unique descriptions for each. The manager reviews the generated text in a spreadsheet-like interface, clicks "Sync," and the live Shopify pages update instantly.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Shopify store owners and e-commerce managers
  • High-volume dropshippers and retail arbitragers
  • E-commerce marketing agencies managing multiple catalogs
  • Brands migrating massive amounts of legacy SKUs into a new storefront

Not Ideal For

  • It is a hyper-specialized tool; it is useless for writing long-form blog posts, emails, or creative fiction
  • If the initial supplier data or product imagery is completely devoid of details, the AI will naturally struggle to generate accurate, non-generated long-form text
  • Pricing is structured around "Generations" (usage limits), which can become very expensive for enterprise retailers with catalogs pushing 50,000+ SKUs
  • The UI, while functional for data, is utilitarian and lacks the sleek, modern feel of consumer-facing AI apps
