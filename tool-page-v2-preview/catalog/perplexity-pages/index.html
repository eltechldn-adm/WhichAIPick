Perplexity Pages

Perplexity Pages is a Research tool used for creating visual articles, sharing research findings, and generating reports. Editorial Overview

Perplexity Pages is a formalized publishing feature integrated into the Perplexity AI search engine. Perplexity traditionally functions as an interactive, conversational search interface, synthesizing information from the web into cited conversational summaries. Pages extends this capability by allowing users to compile, edit, and publish these AI-generated research outputs into structured, shareable web documents directly on the platform. The feature operates by prompting the user for a core topic or research question. The Perplexity engine then autonomously queries the web, extracts relevant data, and drafts a comprehensive, formatted article complete with internal citations, section headers, and generated or sourced imagery. Crucially, the user retains editorial control, able to modify the text, adjust the visual presentation, and append new research queries to expand specific sections before publishing the document publicly. It is utilized by educators compiling resource guides, researchers disseminating synthesized topical overviews, and content marketers generating foundational drafts based on complex industry queries.

How it works

A user logging into Perplexity selects "Create Page" and enters the topic "The History of Submarine Cable Networks." The engine executes multiple search operations, synthesizes the historical data, and generates a formatted article with distinct sections for origins, modern architecture, and environmental vulnerabilities. The user reviews the output, highlighting a section on fiber-optics to prompt the AI to "expand on bandwidth evolution." The AI retrieves further data and integrates it. The user adjusts the header image and clicks publish, generating a public URL for sharing.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Researchers requiring structured summaries of complex, multi-faceted topics
  • Educators drafting reference materials and study guides
  • Knowledge workers compiling and sharing internal industry briefs

Not Ideal For

  • The resulting articles often bear the structurally generic, predictable tone characteristic of AI generation without significant human editorial intervention
  • Published pages exist within the Perplexity ecosystem, presenting challenges for SEO strategies reliant on self-hosted domains
  • Users must vigilantly verify the underlying sources, as the AI’s synthesis of complex web data can occasionally flatten necessary nuanced context
