Framer

Framer is a Design tool used for designing websites, publishing to web, and interactive prototyping. It is intended for Marketing teams building landing pages without engineering, Designers producing portfolio and promotional sites, and Startups shipping marketing pages rapidly.

Editorial Overview

Framer is a visual website builder that combines no-code design flexibility with a high-quality publishing engine optimised for performance. It allows designers and marketers to create websites without writing code, while also supporting custom React component integration for developers who want to extend the platform. Framer AI is the AI-enhanced layer that allows users to generate website sections, landing pages, and copy from text prompts directly within the Framer editor. The AI generates styled, responsive content that integrates immediately with the existing design canvas. Framer targets design-led teams, landing page builders, and marketing departments that want significant visual control without a full engineering team. It competes primarily with Webflow and Squarespace in the no-code website builder market.

How it works

Users design pages in Framer's visual canvas using a component and frame-based system. Framer AI allows users to type a prompt describing a section (e.g., "pricing table with three columns") and the AI inserts a styled, responsive section into the design. Copy can also be generated from prompts. The designer can edit all generated content using the standard visual editor. Sites are published directly from Framer with automatic performance optimisation and CDN hosting.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Marketing teams building landing pages without engineering
  • Designers producing portfolio and promotional sites
  • Startups shipping marketing pages rapidly
  • Agencies building client websites visually
  • Design-led teams wanting visual control over web output
  • Independent web designers and freelancers
  • Startups and founders needing rapid, high-quality landing pages
  • Marketing teams pivoting away from heavy WordPress development
  • Users transitioning from Figma looking for instant publishing

Not Ideal For

  • Can be more complex than simple website builders for non-designers
  • Team plans required for multi-seat collaboration
  • CMS is less mature than dedicated CMS platforms
  • AI output requires design refinement to match brand precisely
  • Exporting code for self-hosting is limited
  • The AI-generated structural layouts can occasionally be somewhat generic or repetitive if you rely solely on the first draft without manual editing
  • While easier than Webflow, true mastery of Framer's layout engine (Stack/Grid) is still required to significantly modify the AI's output without breaking mobile responsiveness
  • Lacks the large-scale, complex backend database and e-commerce capabilities of Shopify or heavy enterprise CMS architectures
  • AI generated images are often generic stock; replacing them with genuine brand assets is mandatory for a professional look
