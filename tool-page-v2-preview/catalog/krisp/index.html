Editorial Overview

Krisp is an AI-powered voice productivity application that runs securely in the background of a computer, acting as a virtual filter between a user's microphone/speakers and their communication apps. Its primary and most famous function is bi-directional noise cancellation: using deep learning models to instantly remove background noise, room echo, and other voices from both ends of a call. As AI capabilities evolved, Krisp expanded beyond noise cancellation into meeting transcription and intelligence. It now offers device-level, completely private transcription and meeting summarization that works across every conferencing app (Zoom, Teams, Meet) without requiring a innovative "bot" to join the call. It is an essential infrastructure tool for remote workers, call center agents, and digital nomads who need to maintain professional audio quality in volatile acoustic environments, while also maintaining records of their conversations.

How it works

Krisp installs as a virtual microphone and speaker on macOS or Windows. Users select "Krisp" as their audio device in Zoom, Teams, or Slack. The AI model runs locally on the device's processor, identifying human voice frequencies and stripping out dogs barking, sirens, or keyboard typing in real-time before the audio hits the call. Simultaneously, it processes the audio stream to provide a live transcript, generating a summarized meeting note locally immediately after the call ends.
