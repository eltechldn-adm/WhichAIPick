Editorial Overview

Fliki is an AI-powered text-to-video and text-to-speech platform designed to convert written content rapidly into publish-ready video formats. It is proactively optimized for content creators, marketers, and bloggers who want to transform articles, scripts, or simple ideas into engaging social media videos without learning complex editing timelines. Unlike traditional video editors, Fliki's interface looks and functions much like a text document or email client. Users edit the video by editing the text; each paragraph or sentence corresponds to a scene, and the AI automatically assigns matching stock video, applies a text-to-speech voiceover, and aligns the captions accurately. The platform heavily focuses on high-quality neural voices and large-scale stock media library integrations, establishing itself as an automated bridge between the written word and the modern social video ecosystem.

How it works

Users start by pasting an article URL, a text script, or a blog post into Fliki. The software breaks the text into scenes. Fliki instantly generates an AI voiceover reading the text. Based on keywords in the text, it auto-selects background b-roll footage from its stock libraries. The user can then click on any scene to swap the background media, change the voice, or tweak the text. The timeline is purely text-driven; changing a word changes the voiceover and caption simultaneously.
