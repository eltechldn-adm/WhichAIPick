Editorial Overview

Salesforce Einstein is the AI capability layer embedded across the Salesforce platform, including Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, and Commerce Cloud. It delivers AI-powered features including predictive lead scoring, automated case classification, smart email personalisation, recommendation engines, and generative AI through Einstein GPT. Einstein is not a standalone product — it is a platform-level AI capability that surfaces intelligence and automation within the tools Salesforce customers already use. This embedded model means its value is proportional to an organisation's depth of Salesforce adoption and data quality within the platform. Enterprise organisations with significant Salesforce investment use Einstein to drive sales productivity, service efficiency, and marketing relevance without adopting separate AI point solutions for each use case.

How it works

Einstein features appear contextually within Salesforce interfaces. In Sales Cloud, predictive lead scores are displayed on lead records based on CRM engagement and demographic data. In Service Cloud, case classification routes tickets based on AI-detected intent. Einstein GPT surfaces generative features such as email draft suggestions in Outlook integrations, service reply recommendations in cases, and code generation in Apex. Administrators configure Einstein feature activation and training preferences in the Salesforce setup menu.
