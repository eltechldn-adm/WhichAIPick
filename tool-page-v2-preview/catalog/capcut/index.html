Editorial Overview

CapCut is a free video editing application developed by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok. It is available on mobile and desktop and has grown into one of the most-used video editing tools globally, particularly among social media creators producing short-form content for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. CapCut includes a range of AI-powered features integrated into the editing workflow, including AI background removal, AI-powered video effects, text-to-speech, auto captions with styling, and script-to-video generation. These features lower the barrier to producing polished video content for non-professional editors. CapCut's strength is its combination of no-cost access, deep TikTok social layer integration, and a comprehensive AI feature set that makes polished mobile and short-form video production accessible to anyone with a phone.

How it works

Users import video clips, images, and audio into the CapCut timeline editor on mobile or desktop. AI features are accessible through dedicated menus — auto captions generate and sync subtitles from audio, AI background removal isolates subjects, and template-based editing allows users to apply viral editing styles to their footage. Script-to-video takes written content and generates a video with AI-selected visuals, voiceover, and captions. Finished videos can be exported and published directly to social platforms.
