Attention Insight

Attention Insight estimates visual attention on designs using predictive models. It produces heatmaps and design reports to help compare layouts before testing them with real users.

Editorial Overview

Attention Insight is an AI-powered predictive eye-tracking and design analytics platform. Utilizing machine learning models trained on vast databases of actual human eye-tracking studies, it predicts exactly where a user's attention will focus when they view a website, mobile app, or digital advertisement—without requiring the time or budget to conduct real human focus group testing. Instead of waiting for post-launch analytics (like Hotjar heatmaps) to see what users click, designers use Attention Insight during the prototyping phase to mathematically validate visual hierarchy. It highlights areas of high visibility and calculates "Clarity Scores" to ensure crucial elements (like a "Buy Now" button or a core headline) are actually capturing the user's gaze. It is predominantly used by UX/UI designers, conversion rate optimization (CRO) specialists, and marketing agencies to scientifically justify design decisions to stakeholders prior to development and launch.

How it works

A UX designer uploads a static mockup (e.g., a JPEG of a landing page) or provides a live URL, or uses the Figma/Adobe XD plugin directly in their design software. Within seconds, the AI generates a predictive heatmap overlay (red indicating high visual attention, blue indicating ignored areas). The designer can draw custom "Areas of Interest" (AOIs) around specific elements—like a CTA button—and the platform calculates the exact percentage of attention that specific button receives relative to the rest of the page.
