Gong

Gong is a Business tool used for revenue intelligence, analyzing sales calls, and deal visibility. It is intended for Enterprise B2B sales organizations, Revenue Operations (RevOps) leaders, and Sales enablement and coaching teams.

Visit Website Compare Gong

Editorial Overview

Gong is an enterprise Revenue Intelligence platform that utilizes artificial intelligence to analyze every customer-facing interaction across a company—including video calls, phone calls, and emails. Rather than relying on sales representatives to manually enter notes into a CRM, Gong automatically captures the reality of what is happening in the sales pipeline. By analyzing the transcripts and metadata of thousands of sales calls, Gong's AI identifies patterns that lead to closed deals. It tracks competitor mentions, objection handling techniques, and even the ratio of how much the representative spoke versus the customer. It provides actionable coaching insights to sales managers. It acts as the single source of truth for enterprise revenue teams (Sales, Customer Success, and Marketing), ensuring leadership has complete visibility into market sentiment and representative performance without subjective filtering.

How it works

Gong connects to a company's email servers, telephony systems (like RingCentral), and conferencing software (Zoom, Teams). It records and transcribes all external interactions automatically. The AI analyzes the transcripts, tagging key moments (like pricing discussions or competitor mentions). Within the dashboard, managers review "Deal Boards" that show the health of a pipeline based on actual communication velocity. Representatives use the platform to search past calls or review AI-generated coaching scorecards.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Enterprise B2B sales organizations
  • Revenue Operations (RevOps) leaders
  • Sales enablement and coaching teams
  • Product marketing managers researching customer voice

Not Ideal For

  • Enterprise pricing can be prohibitive for small businesses or solo founders
  • Requires significant organizational change management to overcome the "Big Brother" feeling among reps
  • Integration setup across enterprise telephony and email servers is a complex IT project
  • Only valuable if the team actively uses the coaching and analytics features
