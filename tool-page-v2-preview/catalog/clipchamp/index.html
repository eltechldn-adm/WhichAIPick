Editorial Overview

Clipchamp is a web-based, non-linear video editing platform that was acquired by Microsoft in 2021 and subsequently integrated directly into Windows 11 as the operating system's default video editor. It is designed to make video editing accessible to beginners and casual creators while running entirely in the browser (or as a lightweight desktop app). The platform heavily emphasizes template-based creation, stock media integration, and automated AI tools over the complex timeline management found in professional software. Key AI features include automatic subtitle generation, text-to-speech voiceovers, and an "Auto Create" feature that automatically trims and sequences uploaded photos and videos according to a chosen style. Clipchamp targets home users, educators, and small business marketers who need to quickly edit social media clips, tutorial videos, or family montages without the learning curve or hardware requirements of traditional rendering engines.

How it works

Users drag and drop media into a multi-track web timeline. From the sidebar menus, they can apply filters, transitions, and text overlays similarly to basic professional tools. Clicking the Auto Create button allows the AI to analyze uploaded media, pick pacing, and cut a video to music automatically. Users can generate voiceovers by typing text and selecting an Azure-powered AI voice, or use the auto-captioning tool to generate subtitles directly from the audio track.
