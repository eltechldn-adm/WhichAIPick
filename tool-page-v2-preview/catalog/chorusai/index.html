Editorial Overview

Chorus.ai (acquired by ZoomInfo) is a premier Conversation Intelligence (CI) platform built specifically to support high-performance B2B sales teams. It captures and deeply analyzes a company's customer interactions—across phone calls, video meetings, and emails—to provide actionable insights that increase win rates and shorten sales cycles. Its primary differentiator is its intense integration into the large-scale ZoomInfo B2B data ecosystem. Chorus doesn't just transcribe a call; it uses proprietary AI to analyze the "Voice of the Customer," identifying specific moments when competitors are mentioned, when pricing objections occur, or when key decision-makers engage. It aggregates this data across the entire sales floor, providing leadership with a macroscopic view of what the top-performing representatives are saying differently from the bottom performers. It is predominantly utilized by Sales Enablement leaders, Revenue Operations (RevOps) teams, and VP-level sales executives managing large, complex, multi-month enterprise deals who need visibility into the "black box" of their reps' daily customer conversations.

How it works

Chorus connects invisibly to a company's communication stack (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Outreach, Salesforce). When an Account Executive conducts a sales call, Chorus records and transcribes the conversation with distinct speaker separation. Post-call, the AI automatically tags specific "Moments" on the audio timeline (e.g., "Pricing Mentioned," "Feature Request," "Next Steps"). Sales managers can log into the dashboard to review an entire week of calls, searching specifically for moments where a certain competitor was named, allowing them to coach reps directly via timestamped comments on the recording.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Mid-market to Enterprise B2B sales teams
  • Sales Enablement and Coaching professionals
  • Revenue Operations (RevOps) leadership
  • Teams already heavily invested in the ZoomInfo ecosystem

Not Ideal For

  • Pricing is strictly enterprise-tier and generally completely inaccessible to small startups or solo founders
  • Requires significant organizational buy-in; reps must consent to recording and embrace the coaching culture
  • The platform is heavy and complex, occasionally requiring a dedicated RevOps administrator to yield maximum value
  • In crowded rooms or poor audio conditions, speaker separation can occasionally blur
