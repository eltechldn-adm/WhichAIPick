Editorial Overview

Pencil (which merged with The Brandtech Group) is a specialized generative AI platform dedicated entirely to generating high-performance video and display advertisements for e-commerce brands and agencies. It attacks the core problem of modern digital advertising on platforms like Meta and TikTok: campaigns require constant "Creative Testing" to find a winning ad, and human design teams cannot physically produce enough video variations fast enough to feed the algorithm. Its primary differentiator is its focus on *Performance Video Generation*. A brand connects their Shopify store or uploads their raw assets (logos, static product photos, existing routine video clips). Pencil's AI analyzes these raw materials and instantly synthesizes dozens of 15-second, high-energy video ads. It autonomously adds driving music, applies engaging typography, implements rapid transitions, and writes the marketing copy. Crucially, it uses historical ad data to determine *how* the video should be edited to maximize conversions. It is heavily utilized by Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) e-commerce brands, performance marketing agencies, and social media buyers who need to continuously test 50 new video creatives every week to scale their Facebook and TikTok ad accounts profitably.

How it works

A skincare brand wants to launch a TikTok campaign but only has static photos of their serum and a plain 30-second video of someone applying it. They upload these assets into Pencil. They select a goal: "Drive Purchases." Pencil's AI chops the 30-second video into 3-second attention-grabbing clips. It animates the static photos. It generates 10 different opening hooks (e.g., "Struggling with dry skin?" vs "My new favorite serum"). It layers trending music and text overlays. Within minutes, the AI generates 15 entirely distinct, ready-to-publish video ads. The performance marketer exports them directly to the Meta Ad Manager to test which video generates the cheapest clicks.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) E-commerce Brands
  • Performance Marketing and Social Media Ad Agencies
  • Shopify store owners needing to scale ad campaigns
  • Media buyers who require massive volumes of creative assets

Not Ideal For

  • Because it is programmatically assembling assets, the generated videos can occasionally feel formulaic, overly "template-driven," or lack the profound nuance of a human-directed commercial
  • The AI is dependent on the quality of the raw assets provided; if you upload terrible, blurry, low-resolution phone photos, it will generate terrible, blurry video ads
  • Pricing scales with the volume of assets and data connections, making it a serious ongoing SaaS commitment rather than a cheap one-off editing tool
  • While it handles the generation, the marketer still must possess a deep understanding of Media Buying strategy on Meta/TikTok to actually utilize the assets effectively
