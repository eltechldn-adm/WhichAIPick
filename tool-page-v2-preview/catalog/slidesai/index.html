SlidesAI

SlidesAI turns prompts and documents into slide presentations through its web app and integrations with Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Visit Website Compare SlidesAI

Editorial Overview

SlidesAI (SlidesAI.io) is a hyper-focused, highly efficient generative AI extension built specifically to live natively inside Google Workspace (Google Slides). It is explicitly engineered to eliminate the grueling, soul-crushing manual labor of formatting presentation decks. Rather than forcing a user to bounce between a large-scale generative AI platform (like Tome or Gamma) and then export the result back into Google Drive, SlidesAI operates directly inside the existing corporate environment, instantly converting large-scale blocks of raw text into heavily structured, professional presentation slides. Its primary differentiator is its profound "Frictionless Native Integration." A user does not log into a separate website. They open a blank presentation in Google Slides, click the "Extensions" tab, and launch SlidesAI. They paste a multi-page PDF, a 3,000-word article, or a chaotic Wikipedia page into the text box. The AI instantly reads the document, extracts the core arguments, determines the optimal number of slides, writes the bullet points, and applies a unified visual theme across the deck in less than 60 seconds. It is heavily utilized by exhausted university students rapidly converting large-scale academic essays into mandatory 10-minute class presentations, B2B sales professionals turning a dense technical whitepaper into an executive pitch deck 5 minutes before a Zoom meeting, and corporate educators generating large-scale training modules from internal company policy documents.

How it works

A B2B marketing manager has a 5,000-word "State of the Industry" report (a large-scale block of text) and needs to present the core findings to the executive team in 20 minutes. Creating a slide deck manually would take 4 hours. The manager opens a new Google Slide and activates the SlidesAI extension. They copy-paste the entire 5,000-word report into the input box. They select "Presentation Type: Educational" and choose the "Corporate Blue" theme. They click "Create Slides." The AI reads the large-scale report, synthesizes the established most important data points, and autonomously builds a 12-slide presentation natively in Google Workspace. It creates a Title slide, an Agenda, 8 data-driven bullet-point slides summarizing the trends, and a Conclusion, allowing the manager to walk into the boardroom fully prepared.
