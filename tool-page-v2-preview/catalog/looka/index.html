Editorial Overview

Looka (formerly Logojoy) is an AI-powered automated branding and graphic design platform designed to serve the fundamental needs of early-stage entrepreneurs, solo-preneurs, and small business operators. Rather than requiring users to learn complex vector editing software (like Adobe Illustrator) or hire expensive creative agencies, Looka uses machine learning to instantly generate comprehensive brand identities. Its primary differentiator is its holistic "Brand Kit" output. It does not just output a flat PNG of a logo. Looka attempts to replace an entire entry-level design agency. After generating a logo, it autonomously uses the brand's new color palette, typography combination, and icon to instantly generate over 300 branded assets—including business cards, social media banners, email signatures, and invoices—creating instant, cohesive brand alignment for a new company in minutes. It is heavily utilized by non-technical founders, local service businesses (e.g., plumbers, cafes, real estate agents), and side-hustlers who need to make their brand-new, zero-budget project look highly professional, legitimate, and established on day one.

How it works

A user launching a new landscaping business types their company name ("GreenRoots") into Looka. They select their industry and click 5 logos they aesthetically like from a grid to train the AI. They select a few colors and symbols (e.g., "tree," "leaf"). The AI instantly generates dozens of custom logo variations pairing elegant typography with vector icons. The user selects their favorite and enters the editor to slightly tweak the font size. Upon purchasing, they unlock the "Brand Kit." Looka instantly populates hundreds of templates: their new logo and green color palette are automatically applied to printable business card templates, a Facebook cover photo, and a branded letterhead, accurately formatted and ready to download.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Early-stage startups and solo entrepreneurs
  • Local small businesses and tradespeople
  • Side-hustlers needing immediate professional legitimacy
  • Users with zero budget for professional graphic design agencies

Not Ideal For

  • The logos rely heavily on a large-scale underlying database of standard fonts and stock vector icons; it cannot create a completely hand-drawn, highly unique, bespoke artistic logo (like a human illustrator could)
  • Highly experienced designers will spot an AI-generated Looka logo immediately due to structural layout similarities
  • The editor, while easy to use, lacks the established pixel-accurately manipulation capabilities of true vector software like Illustrator
  • There is no free download option; while generating designs is free, obtaining the actual files requires purchase
