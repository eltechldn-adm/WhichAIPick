Editorial Overview

Hootsuite Insights (powered by Brandwatch) is a specialized, enterprise-grade social listening and brand monitoring intelligence platform. It exists as a premium expansion to the core Hootsuite social media scheduling dashboard. While basic schedulers tell you how your own posts performed, Insights utilizes AI to monitor the entire public internet—analyzing millions of global conversations across Twitter, Reddit, news blogs, and forums in real-time. Its primary differentiator is its deep Natural Language Processing (NLP) sentiment analysis and crisis detection. It doesn't just count how many times a brand is mentioned; it understands the context, automatically categorizing mentions as "Positive," "Angry," or "Sarcastic." It allows PR teams to track emerging trends, monitor competitors, and detect a developing public relations crisis before it breaks into mainstream news. It is heavily utilized by Fortune 500 Corporate Communications teams, global PR agencies, and enterprise marketing divisions who need to defend large-scale brand equity, requiring a macroscopic view of global human sentiment regarding their products, competitors, or overarching industry.

How it works

An enterprise PR director configures a complex boolean search query: tracking all mentions of "Brand X" AND "Customer Service," while excluding mentions of "Brand X Jobs." The AI crawls the global internet 24/7. When a video of a terrible customer service interaction goes viral on Reddit, the system detects a large-scale 400% spike in negative sentiment related to the tracked keywords. "Iris" (the AI analyst) instantly triggers an SMS and email alert to the PR team. The team logs into the dashboard, views a real-time word-cloud of the specific complaints, and issues a targeted public statement via the core Hootsuite publishing tool to mitigate the crisis.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • Fortune 500 Corporate Communications and PR teams
  • Global marketing and advertising agencies
  • Enterprise market research and strategy divisions
  • Teams managing brands highly susceptible to public relations crises (Airlines, Banks, Tech)

Not Ideal For

  • Pricing is astronomical; it is an enterprise product requiring large-scale annual contracts completely out of reach for small businesses or solo creators
  • Building effective Boolean logic queries requires training and skill; a bad query will pull in large-scale amounts of irrelevant "noise" data
  • Because it monitors the public internet, it cannot track sentiment inside enormous closed platforms (like private Facebook Groups or Discord servers)
  • The dashboard is intensely data-heavy and complex, requiring a dedicated analyst to truly interpret the macroscopic trends effectively
