Brisk Teaching

Brisk helps teachers create learning materials and draft feedback within their browser workflow. School and district plans add institution-specific controls and curriculum features.

Visit Website Compare Brisk Teaching

Editorial Overview

Brisk Teaching is an AI-powered, ubiquitous Chrome extension designed explicitly to alleviate the administrative and operational overwhelming burden placed on K-12 educators. Unlike specialized tutoring apps that target students, Brisk functions entirely to serve the teacher, operating directly within the tools they already use—specifically Google Docs, Google Classroom, and standard web browsers. Its marquee feature is seamlessly reading the context of educational material across the web to generate customized resources instantly. A teacher can open a complex news article, click the Brisk extension, and instantly rewrite that same article at three different, lower reading levels to accommodate varying student proficiencies within the same classroom. It is heavily adopted by school districts and individual educators seeking to claw back hours spent on manual grading, rubric creation, and differentiated lesson planning, offering the speed of LLMs within an highly frictionless browser interface.

How it works

A teacher installs the Chrome extension. If they are viewing an educational YouTube video, they can click the Brisk icon and instruct it to automatically generate a 10-question multiple-choice quiz based on the video's transcript, exporting it directly to a Google Form. Inside a Google Doc containing a student's essay, the teacher clicks Brisk to "Give Feedback." The AI analyzes the text against an uploaded specific rubric and instantly inserts targeted, constructive comments inline, mimicking a human grading pass for the teacher to review and approve.
