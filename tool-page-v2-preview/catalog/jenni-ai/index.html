Jenni AI

Jenni assists academic writing with sentence continuation, source libraries, citations and document chat. It helps develop and revise a draft while keeping references in the writing workflow.

Editorial Overview

Jenni AI is a highly specialized generative writing assistant built expressly for the academic and research community. While generalist AI tools (like ChatGPT) are notorious for hallucinating fake academic citations or hallucinating data, Jenni AI is engineered to function as a collaborative co-author for university students, PhD candidates, and professional researchers drafting severe, peer-reviewed literature. Its primary differentiator is its "In-Text Citation Engine" and PDF grounding. Rather than relying on the AI’s abstract memory of the internet, users upload their specific research PDFs directly into Jenni. As the user writes their essay, they can prompt the AI to expand a paragraph. The AI will generate the text and—crucially—automatically insert proper APA, MLA, or Harvard citations referencing only exactly the PDFs the user provided, ensuring established factual adherence and preventing academic fraud. It is heavily utilized by graduate students writing complex literature reviews, researchers struggling with the blank-page syndrome of drafting large-scale grants, and academics who need a tool to accelerate the physical act of writing while retaining established control over the factual narrative.

How it works

A PhD student begins a literature review on marine biology. They upload 10 peer-reviewed journal PDFs into their Jenni library. They type an opening sentence: "Ocean acidification has severely impacted coral bleaching." They trigger the AI "Autocomplete." Jenni suggests the next two sentences, pulling data specifically from one of the uploaded PDFs, and automatically adds an in-text citation (Smith et al., 2022). The student reviews the suggestion, hits "Accept," and continues writing. If they need to paraphrase a dense paragraph from the PDF, they highlight it, and Jenni rewrites it into their academic tone, instantly mapping the correct citation style.
