Editorial Overview

Lavender is a highly specialized, AI-powered email intelligence platform built exclusively as a coaching tool for B2B Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) and Account Executives. Operating primarily as a chrome extension overlaid directly on Gmail or Outlook, it serves as a strict, real-time editor that forces salespeople to write cold outreach emails that actually generate positive replies. Its primary differentiator is its ruthless, data-backed optimization engine. While general AI tools (like Jasper) will happily write a bloated, 5-paragraph sales pitch filled with marketing jargon, Lavender actively stops the rep from sending it. It scores the email from 0-100 in real-time, analyzing millions of successful global sales emails to enforce strict rules: the email must be readable by a 5th grader, under 50 words, mobile-optimized, and free of "salesy" buzzwords. Its generative AI then actively rewrites the bad draft to meet these exact scientific parameters. It is heavily utilized by hyper-growth SaaS sales teams, revenue leaders, and ambitious SDRs who realize that standard, bloated outbound email templates are ignored by executives, and that intense, psychological brevity is the only way to book meetings.

How it works

An SDR opens a new email in Gmail to pitch a VP of Marketing. They type a standard, terrible sales draft: "Hi, I wanted to reach out and see if you have 15 minutes to synergize regarding our market-leading platform..." The Lavender sidebar updates in real-time, giving the email a score of 42/100. It highlights "synergize" as a buzzword, flags the email as too long for a mobile screen, and notes the reading level is too complex. The SDR clicks "Personalize." Lavender scans the VP's recent LinkedIn posts and Twitter activity, suggesting a highly relevant opening hook. The SDR clicks "Rewrite," and the AI instantly condenses the 3-paragraph pitch into a punchy, 4-sentence email scoring 95/100. The SDR hits send.

Who is it for?

Best For

  • B2B Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
  • Account Executives (AEs) running outbound campaigns
  • VP of Sales and Revenue Operations Managers
  • Founders executing enterprise-level Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

Not Ideal For

  • It is exclusively built for B2B cold sales; it is completely useless for writing internal memos, marketing newsletters, or customer support emails
  • Adherence to the 90+ score can sometimes make emails feel slightly robotic or overly terse if the rep relies entirely on the AI without injecting their own personality
  • Requires a fundamental mindset shift; sales reps must be willing to abandon their beloved, bloated corporate templates to succeed with it
  • The strict rules (5th-grade reading level) might feel counterintuitive when selling highly complex, deeply technical engineering software (though data proves it still works)
