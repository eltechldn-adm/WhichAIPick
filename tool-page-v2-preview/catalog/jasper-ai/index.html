Editorial Overview

Jasper is an AI writing platform tailored for marketing and content teams. It provides tools for generating blog posts, ad copy, social media content, product descriptions, and other marketing materials. Originally launched as Jarvis in 2021, it rebranded to Jasper and expanded its feature set substantially as the AI writing market matured. Jasper is designed for marketing professionals and agencies who need to produce large volumes of content at speed. It offers templated workflows for common content formats, tone controls, and brand voice configuration, making it easier for teams to maintain consistency across output. In a market increasingly filled with AI writing tools, Jasper differentiates on its marketing-specific features, team collaboration functionality, and integrations with tools like Surfer SEO and Google Ads.

How it works

Users start from a template or blank document within the Jasper editor. They provide context such as brand name, product detail, target audience, and tone, then prompt the AI to generate content. The editor supports iterative generation — users can regenerate sections, adjust tone, or instruct Jasper to expand or shorten content. Jasper Brand Voice allows users to upload existing content so the model can match their established writing style. Team collaboration features allow multiple editors to work within shared projects.
