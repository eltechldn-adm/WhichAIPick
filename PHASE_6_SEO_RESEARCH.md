# Phase 6: SEO Opportunity & Intent Research

This document outlines the current search landscape, competitor strategies, and the recommended semantic architecture for WhichAIPick.

## 1. Competitor Overview & Strategic Positioning
**Current Landscape (2026):**
*   **Toolify / There's An AI For That:** Highly automated, massive directories. They rank well for "AI for [X]" but often suffer from thin content, generic listicles, and poor qualitative guidance.
*   **G2 / Capterra:** Traditional B2B software directories. Extremely strong domain authority, but their AI categorizations can be rigid, and their user reviews are often general software reviews rather than AI-specific workflow reviews.
*   **Futurepedia:** More curated than Toolify but still leans towards broad categorization rather than deep workflow integration.

**The WhichAIPick Advantage:**
"AI Software Decision Intelligence." We don't just list tools; we provide structured context. If a user searches for "best ai tools for network engineers", they don't want a random list of LLMs. They want tools segmented by *troubleshooting*, *scripting*, and *documentation*. Our structured dataset (with verified `primaryUseCases`, `pricing`, `hasFreeTier`) gives us a massive programmatic advantage to generate high-quality, decision-focused pages.

## 2. Intent Family Analysis

### Family 1: "Best AI Tools for X"
**Search Intent:** Discovery & Problem Solving. Users know their profession or workflow but don't know what AI can do for them.
**Examples Researched:**
*   *Best AI Tools for Network Engineers:* High commercial intent, low/medium competition. Content gap: Most articles just list ChatGPT and move on. Real network engineers need AIOps (Juniper Mist), Lab builders (NetPilot), and specific documentation bots (kapa.ai).
*   *Best AI Tools for Recruiters:* High commercial intent, high competition. Users need categorization: Sourcing (hireEZ) vs. Screening (HireVue).

### Family 2: "X Alternatives"
**Search Intent:** Evaluation & Switch-Cost Analysis. Users are dissatisfied with their current tool (usually due to price, platform lock-in, or performance) and want a direct replacement.
**Examples Researched:**
*   *Cursor Alternatives:* Extremely high volume, medium competition. Searchers are looking for specific differentiators: "Free/BYOK" (Cline), "Speed" (Zed), "Enterprise" (GitHub Copilot).

### Family 3: "X vs Y"
**Search Intent:** Final Decision & Validation. Bottom-of-funnel users choosing between two finalists.
**Examples Researched:**
*   *ChatGPT vs Claude:* Massive volume, massive competition. Hard to rank for the generic term, but highly valuable for users already on the site making a choice.

## 3. Recommended Priority Targets (Launch Set)

Based on a balance of commercial intent, structured data availability, and current SERP gaps, the following launch set is recommended:

**Best AI Tools Pages (Wave 1):**
1.  **Network Engineers** (High gap, strong technical audience)
2.  **Recruiters** (High commercial value, clear workflow segmentation)
3.  **Teachers UK** (Specific localized intent, lower competition than generic "teachers")
4.  **Research** (Academic/corporate, high need for citations/accuracy)
5.  **Customer Support** (High B2B commercial intent)

**Alternatives Pages (Wave 1):**
1.  **Cursor** (Trending rapidly due to AI coding shift)
2.  **Perplexity** (Users seeking open-source or free alternatives)
3.  **Claude**
4.  **ChatGPT**
5.  **Midjourney** (Users seeking easier UX or free tiers)

**SEO Comparison Pages (Wave 1):**
1.  **ChatGPT vs Claude** (Flagship comparison)
2.  **Cursor vs GitHub Copilot** (High developer intent)
3.  **Midjourney vs Leonardo** (High creative intent)

**Guides (Wave 1):**
1.  *How to choose an AI coding assistant* (Supports Cursor alternatives/comparisons)
2.  *Free vs paid AI tools: what actually changes?* (Supports budget filtering)

## 4. Pages Not Worth Pursuing (Currently)
*   **"Best AI Tools" (Root):** Too generic, impossible to rank against massive publications (Forbes, NYT).
*   **"ChatGPT Alternatives" (without context):** Over-saturated. Better to target "ChatGPT alternatives for coding" or similar sub-intents later if possible.
*   **"AI News":** Not our strategic position. Leave this to media outlets.
