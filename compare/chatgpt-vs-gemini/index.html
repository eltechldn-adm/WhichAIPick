ChatGPT vs Google Gemini: Free vs Paid, Workflows and Pricing

Compare ChatGPT and Google Gemini across free and paid plans ($20/mo), Google Workspace integration, coding, research, voice, APIs, and daily workflows.

Research checked: 2026-11-08

Quick Answer

ChatGPT and Google Gemini both offer free tiers, low-cost entry plans, ~$20/month flagship personal plans ($20 USD / £20 GBP for ChatGPT Plus; $19.99 USD / £18.99 GBP for Google AI Pro), business workspaces, and separately billed developer APIs. Neither assistant is a universal winner: ChatGPT is built as a standalone workspace for coding, Python data analysis, Canvas editing, and Projects, whereas Google Gemini connects directly into Gmail, Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, and Meet and bundles Google One storage on consumer plans.

  • → Choose ChatGPT if you want a standalone workspace for writing and coding in Canvas, sandboxed Python spreadsheet analysis, persistent Projects, and native ChatGPT Images generation. (Editorial recommendation · Sourced)
  • → Choose Google Gemini if your daily work lives in Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, and Meet and you want native side-panel assistance across your Google account. (Editorial recommendation · Sourced)
  • → Personal plans span Free ($0 / £0), lightweight entry tiers where available (ChatGPT Go at $8 USD/mo; Google AI Plus at $4.99 USD/mo), and flagship plans at $20 USD/mo (£20 GBP/mo in UK) for ChatGPT Plus versus $19.99 USD/mo (£18.99 GBP/mo in UK) for Google AI Pro with Google One cloud storage. (Official source fact)
  • → Apps, business workspaces, and APIs are billed separately: ChatGPT Business (formerly Team, $20 USD/user/mo annual or $25 monthly) and Google Workspace ($7-$22+ USD / £5.90-£18+ GBP per user/mo annual ex. VAT) cover team governance, while OpenAI Platform API and Google AI Studio / Vertex AI charge per token. (Official source fact)

Key Differences

Ecosystem & Workspace Integration

Google Gemini connects natively to Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, and Meet via Workspace extensions and side panels. ChatGPT operates as a standalone workspace with Projects, Custom GPTs, Canvas, and cloud file connectors.

Free, Entry-Level & Flagship Plans (USD & GBP)

Both offer a free tier and lightweight entry plans in eligible regions (ChatGPT Go at $8 USD/mo; Google AI Plus at $4.99 USD/mo with 400 GB storage). Standard personal plans cost $20 USD/mo (£20 GBP/mo incl. VAT in UK) for ChatGPT Plus and $19.99 USD/mo (£18.99 GBP/mo in UK) for Google AI Pro (Gemini Advanced), which bundles 2 TB-5 TB of Google One storage.

Product Scope: Apps, Models, Business Plans & APIs

Both providers separate consumer apps (chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com), underlying model families, business plans (ChatGPT Business, formerly Team, vs Google Workspace with Gemini), and token-billed developer APIs (OpenAI Platform API vs Google AI Studio and Vertex AI).

Web Research & Document Synthesis

Both support live web search and multi-step Deep Research reports. Gemini cross-references web findings directly against files in your Google Drive, whereas ChatGPT pairs web search with uploaded files and Python data execution inside Projects.

Writing, Coding & Data Analysis

ChatGPT includes an interactive Canvas editor for side-by-side writing and code revisions plus sandboxed Python execution (Advanced Data Analysis) for spreadsheets and charts. Gemini offers Gemini Canvas, one-click export to Google Docs, Sheets, and Google Colab, and large-context document review.

Voice & Multimodal Capabilities

ChatGPT features Advanced Voice Mode for spoken dialogue and native image generation and editing via ChatGPT Images. Gemini offers Gemini Live for conversational voice alongside Imagen-powered image generation and multimodal file inputs.

Strengths & Limitations

ChatGPT

Key Strengths

  • Standalone workspace with Projects, Custom GPTs, and an interactive Canvas editor for writing and code (Official source fact)
  • Sandboxed Python execution (Advanced Data Analysis) for cleaning CSV/Excel files and plotting charts (Official source fact)
  • Multimodal suite combining Advanced Voice Mode and native ChatGPT Images generation (Official source fact)
  • Tiered personal and team plans from Free and Go ($8/mo) up to Plus ($20/mo), Pro ($200/mo), and Business ($20-$25/user/mo) (Official source fact)

Notable Limitations

  • Does not run natively inside Gmail, Google Docs, or Google Sheets side panels (Editorial synthesis)
  • Free tier applies stricter rate limits on flagship models, image generation, and file uploads during peak hours (Official source fact)
  • Consumer plans do not bundle cloud storage or OpenAI Platform API token credits (Official source fact)

Google Gemini

Key Strengths

  • Native integration across Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, and Meet on eligible plans (Official source fact)
  • Google AI Pro ($19.99 USD / £18.99 GBP per month) bundles 2 TB-5 TB of Google One cloud storage (Official source fact)
  • Large context window on paid tiers for analysing lengthy PDFs, transcripts, and multi-file uploads (Official source fact)
  • One-click export of generated tables to Google Sheets, drafts to Google Docs, and Python code to Google Colab (Official source fact)

Notable Limitations

  • Ecosystem advantages are much smaller if your workflow relies on Microsoft 365, Notion, or Slack instead of Google Workspace (Editorial synthesis)
  • Google Workspace Business Starter ($7 USD / £5.90 GBP user/mo) includes basic Gemini access, but full cross-app side panels require Business Standard ($14 USD / £11.80 GBP) or higher (Official source fact)
  • Consumer Google One AI plans do not include Vertex AI or paid Google AI Studio API token credits (Official source fact)

Decision Support

Choose ChatGPT if...

  • You want an interactive Canvas workspace and sandboxed Python execution for coding and spreadsheet analysis.
  • You rely on natural spoken dialogue via Advanced Voice Mode.
  • You want native conversational image creation and editing via ChatGPT Images.
  • You organise recurring client or internal work using persistent Projects and Custom GPTs.
  • You want standalone personal or ChatGPT Business ($20-$25/user/mo) plans outside Google Workspace.

Choose Google Gemini if...

  • You want to draft, summarise, and search directly inside Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, and Meet.
  • You want a ~$20/month (£18.99/mo UK) AI plan that also bundles Google One cloud storage.
  • You regularly analyse long PDFs, transcripts, or multi-document folders stored in Google Drive.
  • You want one-click export of generated tables to Google Sheets and Python code to Google Colab.
  • Your organisation already manages accounts and permissions through Google Workspace.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChatGPT or Google Gemini better on the free plan?

Both free plans ($0 / £0) cover everyday writing, web search, voice chat, and image prompts. Choose Gemini Free if you use Gmail, Docs, and Drive daily and want fast Google Search grounding; choose ChatGPT Free if you prefer a standalone workspace with Custom GPTs, Canvas editing, and ChatGPT Search.

How do ChatGPT and Google Gemini paid plans compare in USD and GBP?

Both offer lightweight entry plans in eligible regions (ChatGPT Go at $8 USD/mo; Google AI Plus at $4.99 USD/mo with 400 GB storage). Their flagship individual plans cost $20 USD/month (£20 GBP/month incl. VAT on UK web checkout) for ChatGPT Plus and $19.99 USD/month (£18.99 GBP/month in the UK) for Google AI Pro, which also bundles 2 TB-5 TB of Google One storage. High-capacity plans scale to $200 USD/mo (£200 GBP/mo) on ChatGPT Pro and $99.99-$249.99 USD/mo on Google AI Ultra.

Which assistant integrates natively with Google Workspace (Docs, Gmail, Drive, Sheets)?

Google Gemini integrates natively across Google Workspace. On Google AI Pro and Google Workspace Business Standard ($14 USD / £11.80 GBP per user/month annual ex. VAT) or higher, Gemini appears directly inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, and Meet side panels (whereas Business Starter at $7 USD / £5.90 GBP includes the Gemini app and Gmail writing help). ChatGPT can read Drive files via connectors, but does not run inside Google Workspace editors.

Why does the catalogue table list API available as No for ChatGPT and Yes for Google Gemini?

WhichAIPick catalogues the consumer ChatGPT app (/tools/chatgpt/) separately from OpenAI's developer platform (/tools/openai-api/), whereas the Google Gemini entry (/tools/google-gemini/) records both the assistant and Google's developer API availability. Both companies provide full developer APIs (OpenAI Platform API and Google AI Studio / Vertex AI), and neither includes API token credits inside a consumer subscription.

Which is better for coding and spreadsheet data analysis: ChatGPT or Gemini?

ChatGPT suits interactive coding and spreadsheet analysis because Canvas supports side-by-side code revisions and Advanced Data Analysis runs Python in a sandbox to clean CSV/Excel files and generate charts. Google Gemini suits large-context multi-file code review, one-click Python export to Google Colab, and direct table export into Google Sheets.

Can I use ChatGPT or Google Gemini for HR, operations, and team workflows?

Yes. Organisations on Google Workspace Business Standard ($14 USD / £11.80 GBP user/mo annual) or Plus ($22 USD user/mo) often use Gemini to summarise Google Meet interviews, draft Gmail replies, and search HR policies in Drive. Standalone teams often use ChatGPT Business (formerly Team, $20 USD/user/mo annual or $25 monthly, 2+ seats) to share Projects and Custom GPTs for onboarding and policy Q&A.

Do ChatGPT and Google Gemini train on my business or workspace data?

On business, enterprise, and API tiers (ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, OpenAI API, and Google Workspace Business/Enterprise with Gemini), both providers state in their official terms that customer prompts and workspace files are not used to train public models. On consumer Free and personal paid plans, chat history may be used to improve models unless you disable training in your account settings.
