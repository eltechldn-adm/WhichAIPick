Review Methodology & Evaluation Framework

How we evaluate and categorize AI tools.

⚡ Executive Summary

  • Clear Information: We aim to provide clear, actionable information on AI tools.
  • Structured Data: We evaluate tools based on catalogued features, pricing, and specific use cases.
  • Transparent: We do not accept payment for inclusion or positive recommendations.

1. Our Approach to Tool Evaluation

Our goal is to help users navigate the crowded AI software market. We approach evaluations systematically, focusing on factual data and clear use cases. We believe in providing transparent information so you can make an informed decision.

2. The Data Catalog Process

When we review a tool, we catalog specific structured data points:

A. Product Information & Use Cases

We document what the tool actually does (its capabilities and limitations) and map it to specific workflows. This intent matching powers our Finder recommendations.

B. Pricing Information

We analyze the pricing tiers (Free, Freemium, Paid). We strive to provide accurate pricing, though we explicitly flag when our data may need a freshness review.

C. Lifecycle & Status

We track whether tools are active, discontinued, or have been rebranded (aliases) to ensure our directory reflects the current state of the market.

D. Recommendation Eligibility

We determine if a tool is eligible for recommendation based on data completeness and known limitations. If critical data is missing or unverified, the tool is still searchable but excluded from our primary Finder results.

3. How We Compare Tools

Our comparison engine relies on this structured data to present side-by-side facts. We do not invent numeric "winners." Instead, we contextualize each tool's strengths and highlight specific workflows they are best suited for.

4. Independence and Objectivity

WhichAIPick maintains a strict separation between data curation and affiliate partnerships. While we may earn a commission if you purchase a tool through our links, this does not influence our evaluations or which tools we choose to feature. We do not accept paid placements for higher rankings.

5. Corrections and Freshness Limitations

The AI landscape moves fast. Our data represents a snapshot in time. If you notice outdated pricing, changed features, or broken links, please let us know. We aim to correct factual errors as quickly as possible.
