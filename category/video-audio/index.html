Frequently Asked Questions

How good is AI-generated voice quality?

Modern AI voice synthesis is indistinguishable from human speech in many applications. Top tools like ElevenLabs produce studio-quality output from text input alone.

Can AI tools edit long-form videos automatically?

Yes. Several tools can ingest a raw video recording and automatically cut dead air, add captions, generate highlights clips, and format for multiple platforms without manual editing.

Are AI audio tools royalty-free for commercial use?

Most AI music and audio generation platforms grant commercial licences on paid tiers, but always verify the specific tool's licensing terms before using outputs in commercial projects.
