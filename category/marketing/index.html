Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI tools replace a marketing strategist?

AI tools excel at execution and data analysis. Strategic planning, creative direction, and brand positioning still require human judgment and market intuition.

How do AI tools improve email marketing?

They can generate personalised subject lines, optimise send timing, write segmented email body copy, and A/B test variations at a scale impossible to do manually.

Are there AI tools specifically for social media?

Yes. Many tools specialise in scheduling, caption generation, hashtag research, and cross-platform repurposing from a single piece of source content.
