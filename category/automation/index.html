Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between automation tools and AI agents?

Traditional automation tools follow pre-defined rules and triggers. AI agents make decisions dynamically, adapting to context and choosing from multiple possible actions to achieve a goal.

Do I need coding skills to build automations?

Many automation tools are no-code and designed for non-technical users. More complex agentic systems may require some familiarity with APIs and logic flows.

How reliable are automated workflows?

Reliability depends on the stability of the connected APIs and the quality of the workflow logic. Well-designed automations with proper error handling can run for months without interruption.
