Frequently Asked Questions

Is using AI tools for studying considered cheating?

This depends entirely on the institution and context. AI tools used to explain concepts and aid comprehension are generally considered acceptable. Submitting AI-generated work as your own without disclosure may violate academic integrity policies.

Can AI tools tutor in any subject?

Most AI tutors cover a wide range of subjects. Specialised tools exist for mathematics, programming, language learning, test preparation, and sciences.

Are AI educational tools suitable for children?

Some are designed specifically for younger learners with age-appropriate interfaces and content filters. Always review the specific tool's privacy policy for data handling of minors.
