Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need design skills to use AI design tools?

No. Many tools are built specifically for non-designers and use natural language prompts or template-based interfaces to produce professional results.

Who owns the images AI generates?

Ownership varies by platform. Most commercial AI image generators grant you full usage rights to images created on paid plans. Always check the specific tool's terms of service.

Can AI tools replace a professional designer?

For routine production tasks and asset generation, yes. For complex brand strategy, custom illustration, and nuanced creative direction, human designers remain essential.
